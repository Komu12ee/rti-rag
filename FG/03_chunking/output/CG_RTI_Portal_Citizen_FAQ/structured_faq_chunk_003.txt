Q3. How do I pay RTI fees?
Use the Payment Details stage shown by the portal for the relevant application workflow.
 At Payment Details, select the applicable payment option by ticking the relevant box.
 Click Pay Fee Online.
 The portal directs you to generate an online challan for payment.
 Complete the payment through the bank or payment option shown on the redirected page.
 After a successful transaction, keep the receipt or reference details safely.
Keep for future reference: The manual shows a success screen containing treasury reference number, bank reference
number, amount, date and time, bank name, status, and a Print Receipt option.
BPL note: The manual states that a BPL applicant is not required to pay the fee when the required BPL certificate is
attached. It does not define every fee category or amount for every case.
Source basis: RTI User Manual - Payment Details (Manual pp. 12-13) and BPL instruction references (Manual pp. 6-7).


Q4. How do I file a First Appeal?
The supplied manual documents First Appeal details within the portal workflow. Follow the fields displayed by
the live portal.
 Enter the original RTI application details, including the offline RTI application date and the information-received
status, where the workflow asks for them.
Source-based guide prepared from the supplied CG RTI portal manuals
 Select First Appeal office details: State, District, Base Department, Office Level, Office Name, and Officer
Name.
 Select the appropriate First Appeal Reason Type from the dropdown.
 Enter the First Appeal details and the offline First Appeal date shown by the form.
 Choose the application status and click Save & Next.
 Use Back if you need to correct earlier information.
 Upload documents and complete payment if the workflow requires them.
Manual limitation: The supplied manual documents the fields and workflow, but it does not prove every live-portal
starting menu path for a standalone First Appeal.
Source basis: RTI User Manual - RTI Application Details, First Appeal Office Details, First Appeal Application, document, and payment
steps (Manual pp. 8-12).
