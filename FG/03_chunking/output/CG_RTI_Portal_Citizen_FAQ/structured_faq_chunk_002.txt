CG RTI PORTAL - CITIZEN FAQ
Q1. How do I register on the CG RTI portal?
Register first so that you can create login credentials and use the portal.
 Open the CG RTI portal and select User Registration.
 Read the registration instructions, tick the confirmation/declaration box, and click Next.
 Fill in the required registration details. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are mandatory.
 Select Preview and check the information carefully. Use Edit if any correction is needed.
 Click Submit after reviewing the details.
 Open the verification link sent to the registered email address.
 Use the same email address as the login ID, create a password, and click Create.
Account activation: Registration is completed only after email verification and password creation.
Password rule shown in the manual: At least 8 characters, including one lowercase letter, one uppercase letter, and
one special character.
Source basis: RTI User Manual - registration and account activation workflow (Manual pp. 2-4).


Q2. How do I file an RTI application?
First sign in with your registered email ID and password. The supplied manuals primarily document
registration and appeal-related workflows rather than a full citizen-side fresh-RTI screen-by-screen filing flow.
 Log in to the portal using your registered credentials.
 Use the live portal option for starting a new RTI application, where available.
 Read the instructions shown by the portal and complete any acknowledgement before proceeding.
 Enter the requested RTI details clearly and check the information before submission.
 Provide or select public-authority / PIO / office details if the portal asks for them.
 Upload documents only when the portal requests or allows them.
 Complete the payment step if it is applicable to your application.
 After submission, save the registration number and any acknowledgement or receipt.
Manual limitation: The supplied manuals do not establish an exact current menu path or every field for a new citizen
RTI application. Follow the options displayed in the live portal.
Source basis: RTI User Manual - registration, RTI instruction, PIO/office, document, and payment workflow references.
