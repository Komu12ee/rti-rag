CG RTI PORTAL - CITIZEN FAQ

Q5. How can I check application status?
Keep the registration number generated after submission. It is the key reference for future tracking and
related portal actions.
 Log in to the live portal with your registered credentials.
 Use the application-status or tracking option shown in the live portal, if available.
 Enter or select the registration number and other details requested by the portal.
 Review the current status and save any reference information displayed.
Manual limitation: The supplied manuals do not document a complete citizen-facing Track Application Status screen or
every field used on that screen. Do not rely on an invented menu path; follow the current portal interface.
Source basis: RTI User Manual - registration-number and workflow references; live status tracking screen not fully documented in the
supplied citizen manual.


Q6. How do I find PIO contact details?
Use the PIO and Office Details stage or provide enough office information to search a current officer directory.
 Select the relevant State, District, Base Department, Office Level, Office Name, and Officer Name in the portal
dropdowns.
 After selecting the correct PIO and office, click Next to continue.
 If the officer or office is not shown in the list, use the blue highlighted link to enter details manually.
 The manual-entry screen shows fields such as PIO name, designation, mobile number, office name, office
address,district, base department, and sub-department name.
For the assistant registry search: Provide a department, district, office or school name, office code, or officer email for
more precise current PIO/FAA results.
