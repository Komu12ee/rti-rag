## SECTION lB: PROCEDURE FOR INQUIRIES AND HEARINGS

Q18. What is the procedure followed by Information Commissions when conducting inquiries?

Answer: Section 18 establishes the procedure for inquiries conducted by both Central and State Information Commissions:

- (1) Notice of Inquiry:




- Commission issues notice to concerned public authority
- Informs about nature of inquiry
- Gives opportunity to file written reply
