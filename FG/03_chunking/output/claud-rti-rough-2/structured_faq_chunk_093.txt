## (8) Effect of High Court Orderः

- Can quash commission's decision
- Can remand for fresh consideration
- Can direct specific disclosure
- Can award compensation
- Can dismiss petition upholding commission

The High Court jurisdiction acts as constitutional safeguard ensuring RTI commissions do not violate rule of law or exceed their authority.
