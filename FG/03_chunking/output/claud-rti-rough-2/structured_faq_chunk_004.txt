## (3) Tenure (Amended 2019):

- Previously: 5 years from date of entering office
- Now: Such term as may be prescribed by State Government
- This provides flexibility to state governments
- (4) Remuneration and Conditions: As prescribed by State Government rules
- (5) Disqualifications Cannot hold any other office during tenure and must meet constitutional eligibility
- (6) Removal Can be removed by State Government on grounds of misbehavior or incompetence after inquiry
- (7) In Chhattisgarh: Multiple notification refs showing various Chief Commissioners and Commissioners appointed over time ( See RTI Rule Book references to SIC notifications)

The 2019 Amendment made tenure flexible to allow state adaptation of commission terms.
