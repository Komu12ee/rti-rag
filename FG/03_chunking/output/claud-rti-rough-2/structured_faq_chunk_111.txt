## (8) Transparency Within Limits:

- Act still requires these agencies to publish suo motu information ( Section 4)
- Must publish organizational details
- Must publish rules governing operations

The exemption recognizes national security needs while still maintaining some transparency and preventing complete secrecy. These agencies cannot use exemption to cover corruption or routine information.
