## Question (English): Which documents and file formats are shown on the upload screen?

Answer (English): The upload screen shows slots for RTI supporting documents the offline PIO answer document First Appeal supporting documents, the offline First Appellate Officer answer document, and an optional supporting document for the Second Appeal. The screen notes that PDF, JPEG, and JPG files are allowed, with a maximum file size of 20 MB. For Complaint applications, the manual says supporting documents are optional.
