## RTI Portal User Manual 30 Bilingual Q&A

Answer (English): Select a suitable First Appeal Reason Type from the dropdown list. Enter the First Appeal details, provide the date of the offline First Appeal application, and select the application status from the available dropdown. When the required information has been entered, click Save & Next to proceed to the documents stage.
