## Question (English): Does a BPL applicant need to attach proof?

Answer (English): Yes. The RTI instruction list states that a citizen below the poverty line is not required to pay a fee under the RTI Rules, 2012, but the applicant must attach a copy of the certificate issued by the appropriate government authority along with the application. The BPL details screen separately requires BPL document information when Yes is selected.
