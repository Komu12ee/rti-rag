## Question (English): How can I check my registration details before final submission?

Answer (English): After entering the form details, click Preview. The portal displays the applicant information for review. Check the information carefully. If the entries are correct, click Submit. If there is any change or correction, click Edit modify the information, review it again, and then submit the registration.
