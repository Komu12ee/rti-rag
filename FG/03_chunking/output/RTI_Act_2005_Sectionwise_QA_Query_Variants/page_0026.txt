## Answer

English definition: Applicant not required to give any reason for the request or any personal details except those necessary for contact.

हिंदीं परिभाषाः आवेदक से अनुरोध का कारण या संपर्क हेतु आवश्यक के सिवाय कोई व्यक्तिगत विवरण देने की अपेक्षा नहीं की जाएगी|

Retrieval anchors: No reason required; कारण देने को आवश्यकता नहों ; application; request format; fee; transfer of application; language of application

## Typical contexts

- Validating application format language compliance
- Information requested belongs to a different public authority trigger transfer procedure
- Application demands a 'reason' from applicant non-compliant practice to flag

PIO implementation note: Never reject or delay an application merely because the applicant has not stated a reason. If the subject matter belongs to another public authority transfer per 6(3) and notify the applicant; do not silently reject.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, pp. 11-12
