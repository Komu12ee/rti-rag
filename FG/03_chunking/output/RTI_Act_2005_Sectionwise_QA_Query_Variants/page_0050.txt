## Answer

English definition: Where the CPIOISPIO intends to disclose information supplied by and treated as confidential by a third party, written notice must be given to that third party within 5 days of receipt of the request, inviting written oral representation whether the information should be disclosed; such submission shall be considered. Except for protected trade commercial secrets, disclosure may proceed if competent authority is satisfied public interest outweighs harm to the third party On

हिंदी परिभाषाः जहां CPIOISPIO किसी तीसरे पक्ष द्वारा प्रदाय और गोपनीय मानी गई सूचना को प्रकट करने का इरादा रखता है, अनुरोध की प्राप्ति के 5 दिनों के भीतर उस तीसरे पक्ष को लिखित सूचना दी जाएगी, जिसमें लिखित मौखिक प्रतिनिधित्व हेतु आमंत्रित किया जाएगाः ऐसी प्रस्तुति पर विचार किया जाएगा| संरक्षित व्यापार | वाणिज्यिक रहस्यों को छोड़कर, यदि सक्षम प्राधिकारी संतुष्ट हो कि लोकहित तीसरे पक्ष को नुकसान से अधिक है, तो प्रकटीकरण किया जा सकता है|

PIO implementation note: If third-party information is involved, the standard Section 7 3O-day timeline is superseded by the Section 11(3) 4O-day timeline. The third party must be notified and given 10 days before a final decision, and must be informed of their own Section 19 appeal right if disclosure is decided against their wishes.

- Computing the modified response deadline use 40 days, not the standard 30 days, when third-party consultation is triggered
