## Answer

English definition: When granting access to part of a record, the PIO shall notify the applicant: (a) that only part is being provided; (b) reasons for the decision including findings on material facts; (c) name designation of decision maker; (d) fee details and deposit amount; (e) applicants reviewlappeal rights, appellate authority particulars and time limits.
