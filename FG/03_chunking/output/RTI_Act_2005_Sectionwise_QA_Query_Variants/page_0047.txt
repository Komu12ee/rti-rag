## Answer

English definition: Notwithstanding Section 8, a CPIOISPIO may reject a request for information where providing such access would invoive an infringement of copyright subsisting in a person other than the State.

हिंदी परिभाषाः धारा 8 के होते हुए भी, CPIOJSPIO सूचना के को अस्वीकार कर सकता है जहां ऐसी पहुंच प्रदान करने में राज्य से भिन्न किसी व्यक्ति में निहित कॉपीराइट का उल्लंघन होगा| लिए अनुरोध

Retrieval anchors: Copyright based rejection; कॉपीराइट आधारित अस्वीकृति; copyright; infringement; intellectual property; third party copyright

## Typical contexts

- Requested material (book, manuscript, design software etc. ) is third party copyrighted content held by the public authority

PIO implementation note: Verify copyright ownership before invoking this section if the copyright is held by the government itself , Section 9 does not apply and the request must be evaluated under Section 8 instead.

- Distinguishing private party copyright (exempt) from government owned copyright (not exempt subject to 8(2))

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, p. 17
