## Answer

English definition: authority, body or institution of self-government established by the Constitution, Parliament law, State law, government notification, or substantially financed by government funds. Any

हिंदीं परिभाषाः संविधान, संसद विधि राज्य विधान, अधिसूचना द्वारा या सरकारी निधि से पर्याप्त वित्तपोषित कोई भी प्राधिकरण निकाय या स्वशासन संस्था|

Retrieval anchors Public Authority; लोक प्राधिकरण; definitions; appropriate government; information; record; third party; CPIO; SPIO; competent authority

## Typical contexts

- Any query requiring interpretation of a statutory term
- Determining whether a body qualifies as a 'Public Authority (e.g. NGO substantially financed by government)
- Determining which ' Government' is appropriate for an application
- Determining if requested material qualifies as 'Information' under the Act

PIO implementation note: Use this section first when validating whether the receiving the RTI query is in fact a 'public authority obligated to respond, and whether the material sought falls within the definition of 'information'. body
