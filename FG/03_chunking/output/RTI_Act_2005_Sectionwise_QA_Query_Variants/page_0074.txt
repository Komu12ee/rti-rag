## Answer

English definition: An officer who directs a PIO to act in violation of the Act is also subject to penalties.

हिंदी परिभाषाः PIO को अधिनियम के उल्लंघन में कार्य करने का निर्देश देने वाला अधिकारी भी दंड के अधीन हैl

Retrieval anchors: Officer directing PIO to violate Act; PIO को उल्लंघन हेतु निर्देश देने वाला अधिकारी; obstruction; destruction of records; false information; willful withholding; imprisonment; fine

## Typical contexts

- Determining criminal quasi-criminal liability for a PIO's deliberate misconduct (as distinct from the administrative Section 20 monetary penalty)
- An applicant alleges falsified or maliciously withheld information

PIO implementation note: Distinct from Section 2O's Rs. 25O /day administrative penalty (max Rs. 25,000) this section addresses imprisonment/fine for more serious misconduct such as record destruction or deliberate falsification.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, pp. 166-170 (Penalties and Implementation)
