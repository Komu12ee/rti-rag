## Answer

English definition: CPIOISPIO may seek assistance from any other officer as necessary; that officer is deemed to act as CPIOISPIO for purposes of the Act and shall render all assistance.

हिंदी परिभाषाः CPIOISPIO आवश्यक होने पर किसी अन्य अधिकारी से सहायता मांग सकता हैः ऐसा अधिकारी इस अधिनियम के प्रयोजनों के CPIOJSPIO के रूप में कार्य करता हुआ माना जाएगा| लिए

Retrieval anchors: Assistance from other officers अन्य अधिकारियों से सहायताः CPIO; SPIO; CAPIO; SAPIO; assistant public information officer; 100 days; designation

## Typical contexts

- Validating whether the application was correctly addressed to a designated CPIOISPIO
- No CPIOISPIO designated escalation complaint scenario under Section 18
- Application submitted through an Assistant PIO recalculate response deadline (+5 days)

PIO implementation note: If application was received via CAPIOISAPIO, add 5 days to the standard 3O-day computation in Section 7 before flagging a delay.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, p. 10
