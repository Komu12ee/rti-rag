## Answer

English definition: Fine up to Rs. 25,000 for obstruction; fine up to Rs. 10,000 for destruction willful withholding.

हिंदी परिभाषाः बाधा हेतु 325,000 तक जुर्माना; विनाश| जानबूझकर रोकने हेतु 310,000 तक जुर्माना|

Retrieval anchors: Fine recommendations; जुर्माना सिफारिशें; commission recommended fine; compensation; disciplinary action; criminal prosecution; cumulative penalties

## Typical contexts

- A Secona Appeal results in a finding of bad faith persistent non compliance, or serious misconduct by the PIO
- Applicant seeks compensation for demonstrable loss hardship from delay or refusal

PIO implementation note: Distinguish this Commission-level penalty regime from the statutory daily penalty under Section 20 this section is broader (criminal disciplinary compensatory) and triggered specifically through Commission adjudication.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, pp. 166-170
