## Answer

English definition: RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019 amended Section 13 so that the term of office is 'such term as may be prescribed by the Central Government via Rules, removing the fixed five-year statutory tenure.

हिंदी परिभाषाः RTI ( संशोधन) अधिनियम , 2019 ने धारा 13 में संशोधन किया जिससे पदावधि केंद्र सरकार द्वारा नियमों के माध्यम से 'यथा विहित अवधि' हो गई, निश्चित पांच वर्ष की वैधानिक पदावधि हटा दी गई|

## Typical contexts

- Query about CIC's current tenure or removal grounds
- Determining whether the original Act text or the 201 9 Amendment governs a specific tenure dispute

PIO implementation note: When responding to queries about CIC tenure, cite the 2019 Amendment as the currently governing provision rather than the original 5-year fixed term since the latter has been superseded.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, Section 13 (cross reference to 2019 Amendment)
