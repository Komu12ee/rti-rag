## Answer

English definition: Information received in confidence from a foreign Government.

हिंदी परिभाषाः किसी विदेशी सरकार से विश्वास में प्राप्त सूचना|

Retrieval anchors: Foreign Government Information; विदेशी सरकार की सूचना; exemptions sovereignty; security; contempt of court; parliamentary privilege commercial confidence; trade secrets; fiduciary

## Typical contexts

- Information relates to national security, sovereignty, or foreign relations 8(1)(a)
- Information would breach legislative privilege 8(1)(c)
- Information is under active court gag order - 8(1)(b)
- Information is a trade secret commercial confidence of a third party 8(1)(d)
- Information from foreign government in confidence + 8(1)(f)
- Information was shared in fiduciary trust - 8(1 )e)
- Disclosure endangers lifelsafety or reveals a confidential informant = 8(1)(9)
- Pre-decisional Cabinet deliberation -8(1 )(i) (but check if decision is now finallcomplete)
- Active criminal investigation would be impeded -8(1)(h)
- Purely personal information unconnected to public activity 8(1)(])
- exemption claimed must aaditionally check Section 8(2) public interest override and 8(3) 2O-year rule before finalizing refusal Any

PIO implementation note: This is the primary exemption screening section. For ANY refusal under 8(1 )a) (), the response MUST also document why Section 8(2) public interest override does not apply and (except for 8(1 )a)/(c)/(i)) confirm the information is not older than 20 years under 8(3). A bare citation of a clause without this analysis is an incomplete defective PIO response.
