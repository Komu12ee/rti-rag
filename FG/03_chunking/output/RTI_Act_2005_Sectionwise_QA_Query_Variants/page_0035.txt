## Answer

English definition: Information whose disclosure would prejudicially affect the sovereignty integrity of India, security of the State, strategic scientificleconomic interests, relations with foreign States, or lead to incitement of an offence.

हिंदीं परिभाषाः वह सूचना जिसका प्रकटीकरण भारत की संप्रभुता अखंडता , राज्य की सुरक्षा, रणनीतिक वैज्ञानिक आर्थिक हितों या विदेशी राज्यों के संबंधों को रूप से प्रभावित करेगा , या अपराध को उकसाएगा| प्रतिकूल

Retrieval anchors: Sovereignty and Security; संप्रभुता और सुरक्षा; exemptions; sovereignty; security; contempt of court parliamentary privilege; commercial confidence; trade secrets fiduciary

## Typical contexts

- Information relates to national security, sovereignty, or foreign relations 8(1)(a)
- Information would breach legislative privilege -8(1)(c)
- Information is under active court gag order - 8(1)(b)
- Information is a trade secret / commercial confidence of a third party 8(1)(d)
- Information from foreign government in confidence 8(1)(f)
- Information was shared in fiduciary trust 8(1)(e)
- Disclosure endangers lifelsafety or reveals a confidential informant 8(1)(9)
- Predecisional Cabinet deliberation 8(1)(i) (but check if decision is now finallcomplete)
- Active criminal investigation would be impeded 8(1)(h)
- Purely personal information unconnected to public activity 8(1)(])
- exemption claimed must additionally check Section 8(2) public interest override and 8(3) 2O-year rule before finalizing refusal Any

PIO implementation note: This is the primary exemption screening section. For ANY refusal under 8(1 )(a) (j), the response MUST also document why Section 8(2) public interest override does not apply, and (except for 8(1 )a)/(c)/(i)) confirm the information is not older than 20 years under 8(3). A bare citation of a clause without this analysis is an incomplete defective PIO response.
