## Answer

English definition: Every CPIOISPIO shall deal with requests and render reasonable assistance to reduce oral requests to writing if required.

हिंदी परिभाषाः प्रत्येक CPIOJSPIO पर कार्य करेगा और आवश्यक होने पर उन्हें लिखित रूप में कम करने में युक्तियुक्त सहायता देगा| अनुरोधों

Retrieval anchors: Duty of CPIOISPIO to assist applicant आवेदक को सहायता का कर्तव्यः CPIO; SPIO; CAPIO; SAPIO; assistant public information officer; 100 designation days;

## Typical contexts

- Validating whether the application was correctly addressed to a designated CPIOISPIO
- No CPIOISPIO designated escalation complaint scenario under Section 18
- Application submitted through an Assistant PIO recalculate response deadline (+5 days)

PIO implementation note: If application was received via CAPIOISAPIO, add 5 days to the standard 3O-day computation in Section 7 before flagging a delay.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, p. 10
