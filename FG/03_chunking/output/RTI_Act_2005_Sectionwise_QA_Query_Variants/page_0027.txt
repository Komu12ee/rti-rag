## Answer

English definition: Where information is held by another public authority or more closely connected to its functions the application shall be transferred within 5 days and the applicant informed; the 3O-day clock under Section 7(1 ) restarts from the date of receipt by the correct authority

हिंदी परिभाषाः जहां सूचना किसी अन्य लोक प्राधिकरण के पास है या उसके कार्यों से अधिक निकटता से जुड़ी है, आवेदन 5 दिनों के भीतर अंतरित किया जाएगा और आवेदक को सूचित किया जाएगाः धारा 71 ) की 30 दिवसीय अवधि सही प्राधिकरण द्वारा प्राप्ति की तारीख से पुनः आरंभ है| होती

## Typical contexts

- Validating application format language compliance
- Information requested belongs to a different public authority trigger transfer procedure
- Application demands a 'reason' from applicant non compliant practice to flag

PIO implementation note: Never reject or delay an application merely because the applicant has not stated a reason. If the subject matter belongs to another public authority, transfer per 6(3) and notify the applicant do not silently reject.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, pp. 11-12
