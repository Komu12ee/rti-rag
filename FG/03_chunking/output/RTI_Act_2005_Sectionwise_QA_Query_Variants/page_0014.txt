## Answer

English definition: Subject to the provisions of this Act, all citizens shall have the right to information.

हिंदी परिभाषाः इस अधिनियम के उपबंधों के अधीन रहते हुए, सभी नागरिकों को सूचना का अधिकार होगा|

Retrieval anchors: Right to Information (single provision); सूचना का अधिकार एकल उपबंध ; fundamental right; all citizens; core right; universal access

## Typical contexts

- Validating that the applicant is an Indian citizen (eligibility check)

PIO implementation note: Confirm applicant citizenshipleligibility is not in dispute before evaluating exemptions; this section is the entitlement baseline, not itself a ground for refusal.

- Establishing the baseline entitlement before applying any exemption
