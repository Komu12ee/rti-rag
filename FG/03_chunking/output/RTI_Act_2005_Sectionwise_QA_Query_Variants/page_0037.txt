- 1. What does Section 8(1 )c) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 provide?
- 2. Explain RTI Act Section 8(1 )(c): Parliamentary/Legislative Privilege.
- 3. What is Act 8(1 )c) in RTI? I need the rule for exemptions.
- 5. धारा 81)c) का सरल अर्थ क्या है - संसदीय विधायी विशेषाधिकार?
- 4. सूचना का अधिकार अधिनियम, 2005 की धारा 8(1)c में क्या प्रावधान है?
- 6. RTI Act 8(1 )(c) ka rule kya hai? संसदीय विधायी विशेषाधिकार के बारे नें बताइए|
- 7. In this situation: Information relates to national security, sovereignty, or foreign relations 8(1)(a). Does Section 8(1)c) apply, and what does it say?
- 8. "8(1)(c) RTI law me kya hai? " "8(1)(c) ka matlab? "

## Answer

English definition: Information the disclosure of which would cause a breach of privilege of Parliament or the State Legislature.

हिंदी परिभाषाः वह सूचना जिसका प्रकटीकरण संसद या राज्य विधान मंडल के विशेषाधिकार का उल्लंघन करेगा|

Retrieval anchors: Parliamentary/Legislative Privilege; संसदीय विधायी विशेषाधिकार; exemptions; sovereignty; security; contempt of court; parliamentary privilege; commercial confidence; trade secrets; fiduciary

## Typical contexts

- Information relates to national security, sovereignty, or foreign relations 8(1)(a)
- Information would breach legislative privilege 8(1)(c)
- Information is under active court gag order + 8(1)(b)
- Information is a trade secret / commercial confidence of a third party ~ 8(1)(d)
- Information from foreign government in confidence -8(1)(f)
- Information was shared in fiduciary trust 8(1)(e)
- Disclosure endangers lifelsafety or reveals a confidential informant 8(1)(g)
- Pre decisional Cabinet deliberation + 8(1 )(i) (but check if decision is now final complete)
- Active criminal investigation would be impeded 8(1)(h)
- Purely personal information unconnected to public activity -8(1)(])
- Any exemption claimed must additionally check Section 8(2) public interest override and 8(3) 2O-year rule before finalizing refusal

PIO implementation note: This is the primary exemption screening section. For ANY refusal under 8(1 )a)-(j), the response MUST also document why Section 8(2) public interest override does not apply, and (except for 8(1 )a)/(c)/(i)) confirm the information is not older than 20 under 8(3). A bare citation of a clause without this analysis is an incompleteldefective PIO response. years
