## Answer

English definition: Section 4ः cost of dissemination materials, suo motu print costs; Section 5ः PIOISPIO designation and appointment procedures; Section 6ः application fees (prescribed amount; Section 7ः response procedures, copy and electronic format fees; Sections 13 & 16ः salaries and conditions of CICISIC members; Section 19ः appeal procedures and timelines other matters as prescribed for implementing the Act.
