## Answer

English definition: Application fee: Rs. 10 per application; copy charges: Rs. 2 per page (black & white ; electronic format: cost of medium disc; BPL exemption: no fees for economically poor applicants.

हिंदी परिभाषाः आवेदन शुल्कः प्रति आवेदन 310; प्रतियों के शुल्कः प्रति पृष्ठ 32 (काला और सफेद ; इलेक्ट्रॉनिक प्रारूपः माध्यम डिस्क की लागतः BPL छूटः आर्थिक रूप से गरीब आवेदकों हेतु कोई शुल्क नहीं|

## Typical contexts

- Validating fee charged against an application (application fee, copy charges, electronic format costs)
- Query relates to a Chhattisgarh specific procedural rule rather than the central Act provision
- Applicant claims BPL status validate fee exemption eligibility

PIO implementation note: When computing fees for a PIO response, apply the current fee structure (Rs. 10 application fee, Rs. 2lpage copies BPL exemption) and verify any BPL claim per applicable state rules before billing the applicant.
