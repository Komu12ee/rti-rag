## Answer

English definition: Commission must prepare annual reports; Central Commission submits to Parliament; State Commissions submit to State Legislatures; reports must include recommendations on amendment of RTI revision of fees charges procedural improvements, training needs, resource requirements, statutory improvements, and statistical data on appeals disposal times trends; reports are public and published on websites Act,
