## Answer

English definition: If the PIO fails to comply with the time limits in Section 7(1 ), information shall be provided free of charge.

हिंदीं परिभाषाः यदि PIO धारा 71 ) में समय सीमा का पालन करने में विफल रहता है, तो सूचना निःशुल्क प्रदान की जाएगी|

Retrieval anchors: Free information on default; चूक पर निःशुल्क सूचना; 30 days 48 hours deemed refusal; life and liberty; further fee; sensorily disabled; free of charge; rejection reasons

## Typical contexts

- Calculating whether PIO is within statutory response deadline
- PIO missed deadline trigger free-of-charge and deemed refusal logic
- Query relates to a person s life or liberty apply 48-hour rule
- Drafting a rejection must include reasons, appeal window appellate authority

PIO implementation note: Always check the urgency flag (lifelliberty) first to apply the 48-hour rule instead of 30 days. If the 3Oday (or 48-hour) deadline has lapsed, the response must note that the information is to be supplied free of charge and that a deemed refusal has occurred, which is independently appealable.
