## Answer

English definition: Exempted agencies must still comply with RTI Act to some extent requests are processed with Section 8 exemptions applied; appeals still available; routine administrative information cannot be withheld; corruption allegations are not covered by this exemption; subject to Section 8(2) public interest override.

हिंदी परिभाषाः छूट प्राप्त एजेंसियों को फिर भी कुछ हद तक RTI अधिनियम का पालन करना चाहिएः को धारा 8 के साथ संसाधित किया जाता हैः अपील अभी भी उपलब्ध हैः दिनचर्या प्रशासनिक सूचना नहीं रोकी जा सकतीः भ्रष्टाचार के अभिकथन इस छूट के अंतर्गत नहीं आते; धारा 82 ) सार्वजनिक हित अधिरूहण के अधीन| अनुरोधों छूटों

## Typical contexts

- RTI request is addressed to a body on the Second Schedule list of intelligencelsecurity organisations

PIO implementation note: Before invoking a blanket Section 22-type exemption, verify the request does not concern corruption or human rights violations these categories must still be processed (human rights related disclosure requires CIC approval per the broader Section 24 schedule mechanism).

- Request specifically alleges corruption or human rights violation against an exempted agency exemption does NOT apply
