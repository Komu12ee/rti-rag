## Answer

English definition: Original Act: CIC qualification = held office of Secretary to Government of India or equivalent; tenure of five years from date of entering office or till age 65, whichever earlier; not eligible for reappointment.

हिंदीं परिभाषाः मूल अधिनियमः CIC योग्यता = भारत सरकार के सचिव या समकक्ष पदः पद ग्रहण की तारीख से पांच वर्ष या 65 वर्ष की आयु, जो भी पूर्वतर होः पुनर्नियुक्ति के अपात्र| लिए

Retrieval anchors: Original Sryear tenure; मूल 5वर्ष कार्यकाल; qualification; tenure; secretary level; 2019 amendment

## Typical contexts

- Query about CIC's current tenure or removal grounds
- Determining whether the original Act text or the 2019 Amendment governs a specific tenure dispute

PIO implementation note: When responding to queries about CIC tenure, cite the 201 9 Amendment as the currently governing provision rather than the original 5-year fixed term since the latter has been superseded.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, Section 13 (cross reference to 2019 Amendment)
