## Answer

English definition: The 2019 Amendment made tenure flexible, to be prescribed by State Government instead of a fixed five years.

हिंदीं परिभाषाः 2019 संशोधन ने कार्यकाल को निश्चित पांच वर्ष के बजाय राज्य सरकार द्वारा निर्धारित जाने जितना लचीला बनाया| किए

Retrieval anchors: Post 2019 tenure; 2019 के बाद कार्यकाल; state secretary rank; high court judge; domain expertise; 2019 amendment state
