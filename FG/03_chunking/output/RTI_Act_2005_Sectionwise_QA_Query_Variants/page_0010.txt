## Answer

English definition: material in any form including records documents memos, emails, opinions, advices press releases, circulars, orders, logbooks, contracts, reports papers samples, models, and data material held in electronic form. Any

हिंदी परिभाषाः किसी भी रूप में कोई सामग्री जिसमें अभिलेख दस्तावेज, ज्ञापन, ई॰मेल, मत, सलाह, प्रेस विज्ञप्ति परिपत्र, आदेश लॉगबुक संविदा, रिपोर्ट, कागजपत्र नमूने, मॉडल, इलेक्ट्रॉनिक रूप में डेटा सामग्री सम्मिलित हैl

Retrieval anchors: Information; सूचना; definitions; appropriate government; public authority; record; third party CPIO; SPIO; competent authority

## Typical contexts

- query requiring interpretation of a statutory term Any
- Determining whether a body qualifies as a 'Public Authority (e.g. NGO substantially financed by government)
- Determining which ' Government' is appropriate for an application
- Determining if requested material qualifies as 'Information' under the Act

PIO implementation note: Use this section first when validating whether the body receiving the RTI query is in fact a 'public authority obligated to respond and whether the material sought falls within the definition of 'information'.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, pp. 2-6
