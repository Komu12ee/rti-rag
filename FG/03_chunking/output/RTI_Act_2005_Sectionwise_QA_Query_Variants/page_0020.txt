## Answer

English definition: Information shall be disseminated widely and in such form which is easily accessible, considering cost effectiveness, local language and most effective method in that locality

हिंदी परिभाषाः सूचना व्यापक रूप से सुलभ रूप में, लागत प्रभावशीलता, स्थानीय भाषा, और उस इलाके के सबसे प्रभावी को ध्यान में रखते हुए प्रसारित की जाएगी| पद्धति लिए

Retrieval anchors: Form, cost, and accessibility of dissemination; प्रसार का रूप, लागत और सुगम्यता; suo motu disclosure; proactive disclosure; record maintenance; computerisation; 17 categories; 120 manual publication days;

## Typical contexts

- Information sought is already part of mandatory suo motu disclosure published manual
- Determining whether refusal can be justified when information should already be publicly available
- Validating whether public authority has fulfilled proactive disclosure obligations

PIO implementation note: Before processing as a fresh RTI request, check whether the information is already covered under the public authoritys Section 4(1 )(b) suo motu disclosure manual website if so, the PIO response should direct the applicant to that published source.
