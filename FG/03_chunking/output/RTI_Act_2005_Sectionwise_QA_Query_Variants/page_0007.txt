## Answer

English definition: Central Government for public authorities established under the Constitution, an Act of Parliament or a Proclamation under Article 356; State Government for those established under State Legislature law or a Governors Proclamation under Article 356; otherwise whichever of Central State Government is appropriate.

हिंदी परिभाषाः संविधान, संसद अधिनियम या अनुच्छेद 356 के तहत जारी घोषणा के तहत स्थापित सार्वजनिक प्राधिकरणों के लिए केंद्रीय सरकारः राज्य विधान मंडल के कानून या राज्यपाल की घोषणा के तहत स्थापित हेतु राज्य सरकार; अन्यथा जो भी उपयुक्त होl

## Typical contexts

- query requiring interpretation of a statutory term Any
- Determining whether a body qualifies as a 'Public Authority (e.g. NGO substantially financed by government)
- Determining which ' Government' is appropriate for an application
- Determining if requested material qualifies as 'Information' under the Act

PIO implementation note: Use this section first when validating whether the body receiving the RTI query is in fact a 'public authority obligated to respond and whether the material sought falls within the definition of 'information'.

Source reference: RTI Rule Book, pp. 2-6
