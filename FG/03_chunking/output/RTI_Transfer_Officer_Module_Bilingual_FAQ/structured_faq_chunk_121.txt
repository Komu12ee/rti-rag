## Q66. Which transfer statuses are visible in the manual screenshots?

Answer: The screenshots visibly show labels such as Approval Pending Approved, Pending Relieving Letter and Relieving File Uploaded. The manual does not provide a complete official status glossary , so these should be read as displayed workflow states rather than an exhaustive list.
