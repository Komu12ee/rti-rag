## What this PDF contains

This FAQ explains the RTI Portal Transfer Officer Module in practical terms. It covers the dashboard context; handing over PIO, FAO or Nodal charge to another officer; the receiving officer's approval; transfer status review; generation and printing of the Relieving Letter; document upload and the Transfer of Charge Certificate; and the separate name change process when the email ID remains unchanged. Each English question and answer is followed by the equivalent Hindi question and answer. Where the source manual does not define a feature, timeline or exception, the FAQ explicitly says so instead of making an assumption.
