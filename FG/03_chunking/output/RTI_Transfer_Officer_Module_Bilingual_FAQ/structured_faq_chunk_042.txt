## Q21. Does the manual explain how to correct a wrong recipient entry before or after submission ?

Answer: No. The manual does not describe edit, cancellation, rejection, or correction steps for an incorrectly submitted handover request. Verify the selected charge and recipient details carefully before clicking Submit.
