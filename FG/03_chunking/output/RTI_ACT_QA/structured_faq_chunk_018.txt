## SECTION 9: INFORMATION EXEMPTED FROM DISCLOSURE

Q9. What exemption is provided under Section 9 regarding copyright?

Answerः Section 9 provides an exemption for copyright infringement stating: "Notwithstanding anything contained in Section 8, a Central Public Information Officer or




a State Public Information Officer may reject a request for information where providing such access would involve an infringement of copyright subsisting in a person other than the State " This means the PIO can refuse to provide information if doing sO would violate copyright held by a private individual or entity (but not government copyright). This exemption protects thirdrparty intellectual property rights from compromised through RTI disclosure.
