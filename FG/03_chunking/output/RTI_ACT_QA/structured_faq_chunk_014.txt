## SECTION 7: TIME LIMIT FOR FURNISHING INFORMATION

Q7. What is the prescribed time limit for providing information under Section 71

Answer Section 7(l) establishes that subject to provisos in Section 5(2) and 6(3) , upon receipt of a request, the PIO shall, as expeditiously as possible, and in any case within 30 days of receipt of the request, eitherः

- Provide the information upon payment of the prescribed fee, OR
- Reject the request for any reason specified in Sections 8 and 9

Exception: Where information sought concerns the life or physical liberty of a person, the same shall be provided within 48 hours of receipt.

Section 7(2) provides that if the PIO fails to give a decision within the specified period, the PIO shall be deemed to have refused the request.

Section 7(3) provides that where a decision is taken to provide information on payment of further fees, the PIO shall send an intimation to the applicant with details of request payment deposit, and information about review rights. fees, for

Section 7( 4 ) states that where access to a record is required and the person is sensorily disabled, the PIO shall provide reasonable assistance.

Section 7(5) establishes that information shall be provided in the form requested unless it would disproportionately divert resources or harm record preservation.

Section 7(6) provides that if the PIO fails to comply with time limits information shall be provided free of charge.




Section 7(8) requires that when rejecting a request, the PIO must communicate reasons, appeal period, and appellate authority particulars.
