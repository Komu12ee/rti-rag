## SECTION 22: EXEMPTION TO INTELLIGENCE AGENCIES

Q22. Are intelligence agencies exempted from RTI Act under Section 22?

Answer Section 22 provides limited exemptions to specified intelligence agencies including Intelligence Bureau (IB), Central Bureau of Investigation ( CBI), and other agencies specified by Central Government. However this is NOTa complete exemption:

- These agencies must still comply with RTI Act to some extent
- Requests are still processed with exemptions applied under Section 8
- Appeals still available
- Routine administrative information cannot be withheld
- Corruption cannot be covered by this exemption
- Subject to Section 8(2) public interest override

The exemption recognizes national security needs while maintaining some transparency requirements
