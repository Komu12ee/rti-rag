## SECTION 25: ANNUAL REPORTS

Q25. What are the reporting obligations of Information Commissions under Section 251

Answer: Section 25 mandates that:

- Commission must prepare annual reports on RTI Act implementation
- Central Commission submits to Parliament
- State Commissions submit to State Legislatures
- Reports must include recommendations on:
- Amendment of RTI Act




- Revision of fees and charges
- Procedural improvements
- Training needs for officers
- Resource requirements
- Statutory improvements
- Reports must include statistical data on appeals, disposal times, and trends
- Reports are made public and published on websites
- Government should implement commission recommendations
- Commission tracks implementation in subsequent reports
