## SECTION 4: OBLIGATIONS OF PUBLIC AUTHORITIES

Q4. What are the mandatory obligations of public authorities under Section 4 regarding information management?

Answerः Section 4(1 ) (a) mandates that every public authority shall maintain all its records in such a way that are easily accessible. Records shall be properly maintained, catalogued, and indexed in a form that facilitates access under this Act. All records that are appropriate for computerization shall, within a reasonable time, be computerized and connected through a network through the whole of the country sO that access to information is facilitated. they

Section 4(1) (b) requires every public authority to publish the following information within 120 days of enactment: (i) Organization structure, functions, duties; (ii) Powers and duties of officers; (iii) Decision making procedures and supervision chain; (iv) Norms for discharge of functions (v) Rules, regulations, instructions, manuals ( vi) Categories of documents held; ( vii) Public consultation arrangements; ( viii) Boards committees; (ix) Directory of officers; (x) Monthly remuneration of all officers; (xi) Budget allocation; (xii) Subsidy programmes; ( xiii) Recipients of licenses permits (xiv) Electronic information details (XV ) Citizen access facilities; (xvi) PIO particulars; (xvii) Other prescribed information.

Section 4(2) mandates constant endeavor to provide information proactively through various means including internet to minimize use of the Act.

Section 4(3) & ( 4 ) require information be disseminated in widely accessible form considering cost effectiveness and local language
