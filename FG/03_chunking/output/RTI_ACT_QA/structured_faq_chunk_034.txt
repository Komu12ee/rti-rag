## SECTION 17: POWERS OF STATE INFORMATION COMMISSION

Q17. What powers does the State Information Commission have under Section 17?

Answer Section 17 grants State Information Commissions powers similar to Central Commission but limited to state matters, including:

- Appellate jurisdiction over decisions of state PIOs
- Power to conduct inquiries
- Power to issue orders
- Power to recommend penalties
- Power to conduct suo motu investigations
- Power to hear complaints about RTI implementation
