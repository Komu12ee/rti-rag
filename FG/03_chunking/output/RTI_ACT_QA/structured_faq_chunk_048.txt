## SECTION 21: JURISDICTION OF HIGH COURT

Q21. What is the jurisdiction of High Courts over RTI matters under Section 21?

Answer Section 21 preserves the inherent jurisdiction of High Courts over Information Commission decisions. High Courts can:

- Issue writs of habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari, and quo warranto
- Review decisions of Central State Information Commissions
- Examine for violation of constitutional provisions
- Check for procedural irregularities
- Intervene where jurisdiction is exceeded

High Courts act as the final recourse when RTI appeal process is exhausted. Decisions can be challenged on grounds of unconstitutionality or manifest unreasonableness.
