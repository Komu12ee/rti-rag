## SECTION 12 CENTRAL INFORMATION COMMISSION

Q12. What is the Central Information Commission and its composition as per Section 12?

Answer: Section 12 establishes the Central Information Commission (CIC). The Commission shall be constituted by the Central Government and shall consist ofः

- 4 Chief Information Commissioner
- Such number of Information Commissioners, not exceeding ten, as may be determined by the Central Government

The Commission is constituted to hear appeals against decisions of Central PIOs and to inquire into complaints regarding implementation of the RTI Act at the central level.
