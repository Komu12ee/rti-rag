## SECTION 23: PENALTIES

Q23. What penaltiesare prescribed under Section 23 for violations?

Answer Section 23 provides for penalties for various violations:

- Obstruction of PIO: Imprisonment up to 3 months OR fine up to =250
- Destruction of records: Imprisonment up to 3 months OR fine up to =250
- False information by PIO: Imprisonment up to 2 years OR fine up to =500
- Willful withholding Imprisonment up to 3 months OR fine up to =250
- Commissioning violation: Officer directing PIO to violate receives penalties

Penalties can be imposed as imprisonment, fines, or both.
