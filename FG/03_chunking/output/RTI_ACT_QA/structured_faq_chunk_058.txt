## SECTION 26: RETURN OFDOCUMENTS

Q26. What provisions exist for return or disposal of documents submitted under Section 26?

Answer Section 26 addresses the handling of documents submitted during RTI proceedings:

- Documents provided by applicants may be required to be returned after proceedings
- Records used as evidence can be retained by commission if needed for reference




- Original documents generally returned to providing authority
- Certified copies retained for appeal records
- Confidential documents of third parties returned after decision
- Not publicly disclosed if confidential
- Commission may retain for its records copies
