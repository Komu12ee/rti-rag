## SECTION 27: AMENDMENTS

Q27. Can the RTI Act be amended and what amendments have been made so far?

Answer: Section 27 confirms that Parliament can amend any provision of the RTI Act through its normal legislative process. Amendments made so farः

- RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019 ( Gazette dated 29 October 2019 )
- Changed tenure of CICISIC from fixed 5 years to "such term as may be prescribed"
- Made tenure flexible rather than statutory
- Some procedural amendments
- Fees revised through rules ( multiple amendments 2005-2013)

The 2019 Amendment represented the most significant change since original enactment.
