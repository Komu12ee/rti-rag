## SECTION 1B: PROCEDURE FOR INQUIRIES

Q18. What is the procedure followed by Information Commissions for conducting inquiries under Section 18?

Answer Section 18 establishes that Information Commissions shall follow principles of natural justice while conducting inquiries. The procedure includes:

- Issuance of notice to concerned public authority
- Opportunity to file written reply
- Hearing process (oral or written)
- Examination of persons and documents
- Passing written orders with detailed reasons
- Decision within 90 days of receipt of appeal

The Commission has powers of a civil court while conducting inquiries
