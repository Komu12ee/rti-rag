## Second Appeal [Section 19(10) ]:

- To State Information Commission ( for state matters )
- To Central Information Commission (for central matters )
- Must be filed within 90 days of first appeal decision
- Commission must decide within 90 days (extendable )
- Commission can pass binding orders and recommend penalties
- Can recommend compensation for loss or hardship
