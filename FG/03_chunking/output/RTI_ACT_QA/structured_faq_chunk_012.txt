## SECTION 6: PROCEDURE FOR REQUESTING INFORMATION

Q6. What is the procedure for requesting information under Section 61

Answer Section 6(1 ) establishes that any person desiring to obtain information under this Act may make a request in writing or through electronic means in English, Hindi, or the official language of the area in which the application is being made. The request must be accompanied by the prescribed fee and addressed to either:

- The Central Public Information Officer ( CPIO) or State Public Information Officer (SPIO) of the concerned public authority OR
- The Central Assistant Public Information Officer (CAPIO) or State Assistant Public Information Officer (SAPIO)

The applicant must provide sufficient particulars of the information sought to enable the PIO to identify and locate it.

Section 6(2) states that an applicant is not required to give any reason for seeking information or to provide personal details except those required to contact them.

Section 6(3) provides that when an application is made for information held by another public authority or if the subject matter is more closely connected to another authoritys functions, the receiving authority shall transfer the application to the appropriate authority as soon as practicable, but in no case later than 5 days from receipt. The applicant shall be informed of the transfer immediately.
