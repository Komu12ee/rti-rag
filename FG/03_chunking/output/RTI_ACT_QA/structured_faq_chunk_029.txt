## SECTION l5: STATE INFORMATION COMMISSION

Q15. What is the State Information Commission and its composition under Section 151

Answer Section 15 establishes that every State Government shall constitute a State Information Commission with a composition similar to the Central Commission. It shall consist ofः

- State Chief Information Commissioner
- Such number of State Information Commissioners, not exceeding ten, as may be determined by the State Government

The State Commission has the same powers and functions as the Central Commission but limits its jurisdiction to state public authorities and state level matters
