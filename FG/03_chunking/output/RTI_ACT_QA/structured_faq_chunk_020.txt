## SECTION 1O: PARTIAL DISCLOSURE

Q10. What is the principle of partial disclosure under Section 10?

Answer: Section 1O(1 ) establishes the principle of severability statingः "Where a request for access to information is rejected on the ground that it relates to exempt information, access may nevertheless be provided to that part of the record which does not contain any information exempt from disclosure under this Act and which can reasonably be severed from any part containing exempt information " This means that even if some parts of a record are exempt, the non exempt parts must be separated and provided to the applicant.

Section 1O(2) requires that when access to part of a record is granted, the PIO shall give notice to the applicant informingः (a) That only part of the record requested, after severing the record containing exempt information, is being provided; (b) The reasons for the decision, including any findings on material questions of fact, referring to the material on which those findings were based; (c) The name and designation of the person giving the decision; (d) The details of the fees calculated and the amount the applicant is required to deposit; (e) The applicants rights with respect to review of the decision regarding nondisclosure of the information part, the amount of fee charged or the form of access provided, including the particulars of the appellate authority, time limit, process and any other form of access.
