## First Appeal [Section 19(1)-(5)]:

- To senior officer designated by state OR directly to State Information Commission if no senior officer designated
- Must be filed within 30 days of PIO decision or deemed refusal
- Senior officer must decide within 3O 45 days
- Senior officer can direct disclosure, reduce fees, change access form
