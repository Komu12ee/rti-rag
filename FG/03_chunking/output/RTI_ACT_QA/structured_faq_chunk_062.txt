## SECTION 28: RULE MAKING POWER

Q28. What matters are covered by rule making power under Section 28?

Answer: Section 28 establishes comprehensive rule making authority for Central and State Governments. Matters covered include:

- Section 4: Cost of dissemination materials, costs for suo motu disclosure print
- Section 5ः PIOISPIO designation, appointment procedures
- Section 6: Application fees (prescribed amount)
- Section 7: Response procedures, fees for and electronic formats copies
- Section 13 & 16: Salaries and conditions of CICISIC members
- Section 19: Appeal procedures and timelines
- Other matters: As prescribed for implementing the Act
