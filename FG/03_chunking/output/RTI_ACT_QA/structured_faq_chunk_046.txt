## SECTION 20: TRANSFER OF CASES

Q20. Can cases be transferred between Information Commissions under Section 201

Answer: Section 20 provides for transfer of cases between Central and State Information Commissions whereः

- Matter involves both central and state authorities
- Issue has multi state implications
- Central authority is primarily involved
- Public interest requires transfer

Both commissions have authority to transfer cases and maintain coordination. However, this is rarely used in practice.
