## Q25 (English). Is an RTI fee required for a person below the poverty line?

Answer. The instruction screen states that no RTI fee is required from a citizen below the poverty line under the RTI Rules, 201 2. It also states that the applicant must attach a copy of the certificate issued by the appropriate government along with the application.
