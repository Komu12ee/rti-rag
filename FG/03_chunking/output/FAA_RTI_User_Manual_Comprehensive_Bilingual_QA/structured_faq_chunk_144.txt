## Q71 (English). What is the order of the second appeal application steps shown in the manual?

Answer. The progress display shows RTI Instruction, Second Appeal Application Details, PIO and Office Details, RTI Details, First Appeal Office Details, First Appeal Application, Documents Details, and Payment Details.
