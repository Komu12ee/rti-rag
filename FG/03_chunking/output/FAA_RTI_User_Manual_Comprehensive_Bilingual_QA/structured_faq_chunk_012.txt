## Q5 (English). What details are shown in the user registration form?

Answer. The form shown in the manual includes applicant type, name in English, name in Hindi, email ID, fathers name mother's name, mobile number, caste type age gender, addresses in English and Hindi, PIN code, country state, district and residence type.
