## 4.1 Timeline Complications

Q143. If 1 file RTIon 3Oth of month, when is deadline - 29th or 3Oth? A: Deadline is 30 days from receipt. If received on 3Oth, deadline is 3Oth of next month (or last day of month if shorter ).
