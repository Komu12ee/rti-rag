## 2.2 Exemptions & What Cannot Be Requested

Q51. What information is NOT provided under RTI? A: Information falling under Sections 8&9:

- Sovereignty security matters
- Court forbidden information
- Cabinet deliberations
- Trade secrets
- Personal privacy
- Investigation details (confidential)
- Copyright protected material
