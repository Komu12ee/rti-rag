## Chhattisgarh Government & State RTI Details

Q253. Can I file appeal directly to SIC or must go through first appeal? A: Normally first appeal to designated senior officer first. Can directly approach SIC if: (a) no senior officer designated, (b) senior officer refuses to hear appeal, or (c) after first appeal.
