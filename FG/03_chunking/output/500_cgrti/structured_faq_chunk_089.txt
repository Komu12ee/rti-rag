## 3.l Legal Framework & Interpretation

Q91. What is difference between "shall" and "may" in RTI Act? A: "Shall" = mandatory (must do). "May" = discretionary (can choose). Section 4( 2) says "shall be constant endeavor" (mandatory suo motu).
