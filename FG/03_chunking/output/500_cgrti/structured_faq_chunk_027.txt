## L.4 Fees & Payment

Q30. How do I pay the 3l0 fee? A: Tou can pay through:

- Online portal (rtionline cg govin ) via card UPI
- Post office (postal order)
- Bank draft
- Direct payment at PIO office
- Cash (if deposited directly)
