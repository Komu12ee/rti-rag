## Question: Can T enter officer details manually if the office is not listed?

Answer: Tes. The manual directs users to use the blue highlighted link when the required office and officer details are not available in the listed options. The alternate form shown asks for officer name, designation, mobile number , office name, office address district, base department and sub department name.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed pages 7-8.
