## Question: What relation and reason information is required for a second appeal?

Answer: Select the Second Appeal Relation as Appeal or Complaint, choose the available reason type, enter the reason for appeal in words and select Yes or No for BPL  card holder status.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 6.
