## Question: How do 1 begin a second appeal application on the portal?

Answer: Log in with your credentials. On the user dashboard, use the option for applying for a second appeal for offline applications under PIO and FAO, then click the indicated second appeal button.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 5.
