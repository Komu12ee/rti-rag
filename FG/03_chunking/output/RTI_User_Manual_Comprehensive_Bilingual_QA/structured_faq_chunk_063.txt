## Question: What details are entered in the Second Appeal Details section ?

Answer: Select the Second Appeal Relation as Appeal or Complaint, choose the available reason type, enter the reason for appeal in words, and select Tes or No for BPL card holder status.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 6.
