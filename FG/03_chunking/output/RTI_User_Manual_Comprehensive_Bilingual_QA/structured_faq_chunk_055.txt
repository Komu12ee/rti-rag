## Question: Will 1 receive a registration number after submitting an application?

Answer: The manual states that a unique registration number is issued after submission and can be used for future reference. It also says the applicant or appellant should provide a mobile number to receive SMS alerts.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 6.
