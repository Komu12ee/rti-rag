## Question: What appears after a successful payment transaction?

Answer: The manual shows a transaction success screen after payment. The example displays treasury and bank reference information, the amount, transaction date and time, bank name, and status. A Print Receipt option is also visible in the shown screen.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed pages 12 13
