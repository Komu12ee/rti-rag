## Question: Does the manual mention an RTI fee for a person below the poverty line ?

Answer: The instruction screen says that no RTI fee is required from a person below the poverty line under the cited RTI Rules, 2012. It also says the applicant must attach a copy of the certificate issued by the appropriate government authority
