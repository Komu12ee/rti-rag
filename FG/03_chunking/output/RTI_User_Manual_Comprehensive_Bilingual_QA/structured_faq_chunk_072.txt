## Question: How do T enter PIO and office details in the second appeal workflow?

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 7.

Answerः Enter or select the officer and office details using the displayed fields and dropdown options, then click Next. The screen shown includes State, District, Base Department, Office Level, Office Name, and Officer Name.
