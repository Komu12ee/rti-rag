## Question: What information is shown in the user registration form?

Answer: The form shown in the manual includes applicant type, name in English, name in Hindi, email ID, father 's name, mother's name, mobile number, caste type, age gender , address in English, address in Hindi, PIN code, country, state, district, and residence type. Fields marked with an asterisk are mandatory in the displayed form
