## Question: What should 1 do if my application text is longer than the portal limit?

Answer: The manual states that text entered in the prescribed application column is limited to 500 characters. If the application is longer than 500 characters add the remaining material as a supporting document.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 6.
