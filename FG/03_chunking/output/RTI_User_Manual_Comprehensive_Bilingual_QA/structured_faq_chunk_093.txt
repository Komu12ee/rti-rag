## Question: How do 1 upload documents for a second appeal application?

Answer: On the Document Details page, click Browse, select the file, and click Upload. The manual says a successful upload is shown and the Upload button changes to green with the label Uploaded.

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed page 11.
