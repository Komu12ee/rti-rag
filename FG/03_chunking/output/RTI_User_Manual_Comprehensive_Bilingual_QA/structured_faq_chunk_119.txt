## Question: Does the manual show a receipt option after payment?

Answer: The manual shows a transaction success screen after payment. The example displays treasury and bank reference information, the amount, transaction date and time, bank name, and status. A Print Receipt is also visible in the shown screen option

Source: RTI User Manual, Printed pages 12 13.
