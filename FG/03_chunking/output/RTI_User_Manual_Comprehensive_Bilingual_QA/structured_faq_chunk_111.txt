## Question: What fee choices are visible in the payment screenshot?

Answer: The payment screenshot displays two selectable fee choices, one marked Rs. 100 and another marked Rs. 125. The explanatory text in the manual does not define which option applies to a particular case, so the user should verify the correct live portal choice.
