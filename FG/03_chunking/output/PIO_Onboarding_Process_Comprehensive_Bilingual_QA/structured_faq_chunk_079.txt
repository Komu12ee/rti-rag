## Q41 (English). How does the First Appellate Officer access to the RTI portal? get

Answer. A link is sent to the e-mail ID entered for the First Appellate Officer. The e-mail should be in the gov in or nic in domain. By clicking the link, the FAO creates a password for the RTI portal and logs in.
