## Untitled section

Question 2. How should an application be made?

Answer: Request for obtaining information - Any person who wishes to obtain any information under this Act shall make a request,
in writing or through electronic means, in English or Hindi or in the official language of the area in which the application is being
made, together with such fee as may be prescribed -
(a) to the Central Public Information Officer or the State Public Information Officer, as the case may be, of the concerned public authority;
(b) to the Central Assistant Public Information Officer or the State Assistant Public Information Officer, as the case may be, specifying the
particulars of the information sought: Provided that, where such a request cannot be made in writing, the Central Public Information
Officer or the State Public Information Officer, as the case may be, shall give all reasonable assistance orally to the person making the
request, so that it may be reduced to writing.
