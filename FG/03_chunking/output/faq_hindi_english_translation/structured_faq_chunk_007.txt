## Untitled section

Question 2. What is the application fee for a first appeal?

Answer: On submitting a first appeal under Section 19(1), the applicant must deposit Rs. 50/- (fifty rupees) as an application fee
through a treasury challan, cash, non-judicial stamp, e-stamp, money order, demand draft, banker’s cheque, or postal order.
If a copy of the first appeal order is required by registered post, the applicant shall have to pay an additional Rs. 25/- (twenty-five
rupees) as postal charges.
