## Untitled section

Question 2. What is the application fee for a second appeal?

Answer: On submitting a second appeal under Section 19(1), the applicant may deposit Rs. 100/- (one hundred rupees) as an
application fee through a treasury challan, cash, non-judicial stamp, e-stamp, money order, demand draft, banker’s cheque, or postal
order.
If a copy of the second appeal order is required by registered post, the applicant shall have to pay an additional Rs. 25/- (twenty-five
rupees) as postal charges.
