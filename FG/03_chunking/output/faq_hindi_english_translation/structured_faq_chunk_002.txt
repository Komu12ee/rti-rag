## Untitled section

Question 1. What information may be sought?

Answer: “Information” means any material in any form, including records, documents, memoranda, e-mails, opinions, advice, press
releases, circulars, orders, logbooks, contracts, reports, papers, samples, models, data-related material, and information relating to
any private body to which a public authority may have access under any other law in force at the time.
