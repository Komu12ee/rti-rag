## SECTION 9: INFORMATIONEXEMPTED FROM DISCLOSURE

Q9. What is the copyright exemption under the RTI Act?

Answer: Section 9 provides an exemption for copyright infringement. It states that without prejudice to the provisions of Section 8,a Central Public Information Officer or a State Public Information Officer may reject a request for information where providing such access would involve an infringement of copyright subsisting in a person other than the State. This means that if disclosing information would violate copyright protections held by a private individual or entity not the government), the PIO can refuse to provide that information. However, this exemption applies only to third party copyrights; government copyrights are subject to Section 8(2) public interest override provisions.
