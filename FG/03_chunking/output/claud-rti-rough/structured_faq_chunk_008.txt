## SECTION 4: OBLIGATIONS OF PUBLIC AUTHORITIES

Q4. What are the mandatory obligations of public authorities regarding information management and publication?

Answer: Section 4 imposes comprehensive obligations on every public authority with three components: key

- (1) Record Management [Section 4 (1) (a) ]: Public authorities must maintain all records in a properly catalogued and indexed manner in a form that facilitates access under the RTI Act. All records that are appropriate for computerization must be computerized within reasonable time and connected through a network across the country to facilitate access.
- (2) Mandatory Publications within 120 days [Section 4 (1 ) (b) ]: Public authorities must publish 17 specific categories of information including (i) organization particulars, functions, duties; (ii) powers and duties of officers; (iii) decision making procedures; (iv) functional norms (v) rules and manuals; (vi) document categories; ( vii) public consultation arrangements; ( viii) boards and committees details; (ix) officer directory; (x) monthly remuneration of officers; (xi) budget allocation; (xii) subsidy programme execution; ( xiii) recipients of licenses permits (xiv) electronic information details; (xv ) citizen access facilities; (xvi) PIO particulars; (xvii) other prescribed information.
- (3) Proactive Disclosure [Section 4 (2) ]: It shall be a constant endeavor of every public authority to provide as much information as possible proactively at regular intervals through various means including internet, so that public has minimum need to use the Act.
- (4) Dissemination Standards [Section 4(3) & ( 4 ) ]: Information must be disseminated widely in forms easily accessible to public, considering costreffectiveness, local language, and effective communication methods.
