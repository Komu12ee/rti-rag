## Ql5. What is the State Information Commission and how is it constituted?

Answer: Section 15 establishes State Information Commissions ( SICs) at the state level parallel to the Central Information Commission, with similar structure and powers but limited to state matters:

- (1) Establishment: Every state shall constitute a State Information Commission
