## SECTION l4: POWERS OF INFORMATION COMMISSIONERS

Q14. What are the powers and functions of Information Commissioners?

Answer Section 14 grants Information Commissioners substantial powers to effectively implement the RTI Actः

- (1) Inquiry Powers: Commissioners can inquire into complaints and conduct investigations with the authority of a civil court
- (2) Summon Power: Can summon and examine any public official or authority
- (3) Demand Records: Can demand production of any record or document held by a public authority
- (4) Call for Information: Can call for information and explanations from public authorities
- (5) Conduct Hearings Can hold hearings and take evidence (oral or written)
- (6) Issue Orders: Can pass binding orders directing:
- Disclosure of information
- Compensation for loss or hardship caused
- Improvement of RTI practices
- Punishment of responsible officials (in certain cases)
- Recommend Penalties Can recommend penalties on public authorities or officials forः
- Non compliance with RTI Act
- Destruction of records
- Non maintenance of records
- Willful withholding of information
- (8) Jurisdiction: Limited to appellate matters and complaints relating to RTI Act implementation

These powers make the Information Commission a quasi-judicial body with significant authority to ensure RTI compliance.
