## SECTION 12: CENTRAL INFORMATION COMMISSION

Q12. What is the Central Information Commission and how is it constituted?

Answer Section 12 establishes the Central Information Commission (CIC), the apex body handling RTI appeals at the national level. The Commission is constituted by the Central Government and comprises: for

- One Chief Information Commissioner (CIC)
- Up to ten Information Commissioners (ICs)
