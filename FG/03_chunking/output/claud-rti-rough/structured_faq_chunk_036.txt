## (4) Removal:

- Can be removed by the President on grounds of misbehavior or incompetence
- Only after inquiry by Supreme Court (in case of Chief Commissioner)
- Removal of Information Commissioners requires recommendation from Chief Commissioner
- (5) Independence: Cannot hold any other office during tenure
- (6) Jurisdiction: Uniform across India CIC has authority over all central public authorities

The 2019 Amendment made the tenure flexible (from fixed 5 years) to allow adaptation to governmental needs.
