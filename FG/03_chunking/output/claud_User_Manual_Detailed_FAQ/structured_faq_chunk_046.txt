## Untitled section

Q043. How is a First Appeal different from a Second Appeal?

Legal distinction is not specified in the manual.
Operationally, the manual treats First Appeal as a separate earlier stage whose office and application details
must be recorded before completing the Second Appeal workflow. A Second Appeal application includes PIO
information, RTI details, first-appeal office details, and first-appeal application details. The manual does not
provide the legal definitions or statutory distinction.
