## Untitled section

Q077. What if the dropdown list for officer details is empty?

When the needed office or officer is not listed, the manual directs the user to use the blue manual-entry link. If a
dropdown is technically empty or fails to load, the manual provides no troubleshooting steps; the manual-entry
option is the only documented alternative for unavailable details.
