## Untitled section

Q019. What should I select under "Second Appeal Relation"?

The manual does not define when each relation should be chosen.
Select either Appeal or Complaint. The manual does not supply a decision rule for choosing between them. It
does state the operational consequences of selecting Complaint: payment is not mandatory and supporting
documents are optional. Choose the relation that matches the nature of your case under the live portal and
applicable RTI guidance.
