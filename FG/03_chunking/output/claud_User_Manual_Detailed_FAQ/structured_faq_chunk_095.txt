## Untitled section

Q092. What should I do if I have entered wrong information in the previous step?

Use Back when the step provides it to return to the preceding form and correct earlier entries. For user
registration specifically, use Preview, then Edit before Submit. The manual shows these correction paths but
does not describe corrections after a fully submitted and paid application.
