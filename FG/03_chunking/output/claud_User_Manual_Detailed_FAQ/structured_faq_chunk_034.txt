## Untitled section

Q031. What happens if I select "Yes" for BPL card holder status?

The portal displays or requires the BPL document fields: BPL Document Number, BPL Document Issue Date, and
BPL Issuing Authority. The manual says these details are mandatory for further processing, and the instruction
screen separately requires attachment of the appropriate government certificate.
