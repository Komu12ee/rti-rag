## Untitled section

Q009. What is a Second Appeal application?

Operational description; formal legal definition is not provided.
In this manual, a Second Appeal application is the portal workflow used after an offline application process
involving the Public Information Officer (PIO) and First Appeal Officer (FAO). It collects the applicant's registered
details, the second-appeal relation and reason, officer and office details, RTI details, first-appeal details,
supporting documents, and payment details. The manual is a process guide and does not give a formal legal
definition of a Second Appeal.
