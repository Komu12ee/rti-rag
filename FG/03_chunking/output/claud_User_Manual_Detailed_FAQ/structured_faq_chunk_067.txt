## Untitled section

Q064. What happens if my online payment fails?

Failure handling is not specified in the manual.
The manual shows only the success path and does not describe a failure screen, retry process, reconciliation
procedure, or support escalation. Preserve any transaction reference visible on the payment page and consult
the live portal or its ContactUs channel for a failed-payment case.
