## Untitled section

Q027. What is the BPL Document Number?

No specific number format is stated.
It is the form field for the identifying number of the BPL document or certificate. The manual does not prescribe
a separate number format; enter the document number exactly as it appears on the government-issued BPL
certificate or card.
