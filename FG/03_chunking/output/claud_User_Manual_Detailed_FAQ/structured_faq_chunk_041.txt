## Untitled section

Q038. What details should I enter in the RTI Application section?

Enter the RTI Details narrative, the RTI Apply Date Offline, and select the Information received value. The
screenshot marks these fields as mandatory. After completing them, select Next to proceed to First Appeal Office
Details.
