## Untitled section

Q107. What happens if the applicant is not satisfied with the reply?

Decision criteria are not specified.
The manual does not give a narrative decision rule. It provides the Second Appeal workflow in which the
applicant selects Appeal or Complaint, chooses a reason type, supplies the reason, and records the earlier RTI
and First Appeal information. Use the live portal and applicable RTI guidance to decide the legally appropriate
option.
