## Untitled section

Q040. What does "Information Received" mean?

The option meanings are not specified in the manual.
It is a required dropdown in the RTI Application Details section. The manual does not define the available values
or provide a decision rule for selecting them; choose the option in the live portal that accurately represents the
information outcome in your case.
