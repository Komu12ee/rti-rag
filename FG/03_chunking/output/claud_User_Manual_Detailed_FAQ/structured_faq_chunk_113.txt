Q110. How do I track my application status?

The Applicant Dashboard shows count tiles for Total, Pending, First Hearing, Next Hearing, and Ordered. The
manual does not provide step-by-step instructions for opening an individual application, filtering results, or
interpreting each status beyond the labels shown.
