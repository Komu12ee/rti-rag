## Untitled section

Q055. Can I edit details after clicking Preview but before Submit?

For user registration, yes: the manual instructs users to choose Preview, review the entered details, and select
Edit to change them before Submit. In the multi-step Second Appeal workflow, the manual also permits
corrections to prior information through Back. It does not show a final Second Appeal preview page comparable
to the registration preview.
