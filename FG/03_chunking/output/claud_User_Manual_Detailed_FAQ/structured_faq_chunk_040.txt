Q037. How do I select the correct state and district?

Use the State and District dropdowns in the PIO/office stage. The screenshot shows Chhattisgarh as the state
example and a selectable District field. The manual does not include a state or district master list; select the
jurisdiction that matches the office connected to your RTI matter.
