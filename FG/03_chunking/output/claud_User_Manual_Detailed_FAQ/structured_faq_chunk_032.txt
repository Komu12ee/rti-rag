## Untitled section

Q029. Who is the BPL Issuing Authority?

The manual does not provide an authority list.
The form asks for the BPL Issuing Authority, meaning the government authority shown on the supporting BPL
document. The manual does not list acceptable agencies or authorities, so use the issuing authority stated on the
certificate and retain the certificate copy for upload.
