## Untitled section

Q072. What does the red circle indicate in the progress bar?

Visual interpretation; no formal legend is supplied.
In the screenshots, the red numbered circle marks the stage currently being filled, while prior stages are green.
The manual uses this visual convention but does not formally define it in text.
