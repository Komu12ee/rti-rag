## Untitled section

Q084. What character limit applies to different fields?

Most field-specific character limits are not specified.
The manual specifies three quantitative controls: application text in the prescribed column is limited to 500
characters; a password must be at least 8 characters; and uploaded files may be up to 20 MB. It does not state
maximum character counts for names, addresses, reason fields, officer fields, BPL document number, or other
text boxes.
