## Untitled section

Q012. What are the RTI instructions I need to read before applying?

The instruction screen says the portal can be used by Indian citizens for RTI-related online filing and payment; it
distinguishes mandatory fields from optional fields; it limits the prescribed application text to 500 characters; it
permits longer material as a supporting document; it notes the BPL fee rule and certificate requirement; it says a
unique registration number is issued on submission; and it asks applicants to provide a mobile number for SMS
alerts. It also refers to the original application registration number for reference and to the first-appeal process.
 Read the full instructions on the live screen because they may carry portal-specific updates.
 The manual does not reproduce a separate legal rulebook; it shows procedural instructions for the portal.
