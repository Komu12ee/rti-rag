## Untitled section

Q117. Can I modify an application after it has been submitted and paid for?

Post-submission modification is not specified.
The manual documents correction before submission: Edit on the registration preview and Back in earlier
workflow steps. It does not describe modifications after a Second Appeal has been submitted and paid for. Do
not assume post-payment editing is available.
