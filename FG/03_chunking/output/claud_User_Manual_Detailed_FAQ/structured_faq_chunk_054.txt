Q051. Can I upload documents later?

Later-upload capability is not specified in the manual.
The manual instructs users to upload documents at the Document Details step and then select Next to proceed to
payment. It does not document a post-submission or post-payment upload procedure. Complete the relevant
uploads before moving to payment unless the live portal explicitly provides a later-upload function.
