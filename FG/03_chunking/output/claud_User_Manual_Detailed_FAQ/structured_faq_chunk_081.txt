## Untitled section

Q078. What if I cannot find my state in the dropdown?

A missing-state remedy is not specified in the manual.
The manual provides no alternative procedure for a missing state. The screenshots show Chhattisgarh as the
example state, and the guide is for the Government of Chhattisgarh RTI portal. Use the live portal's available
jurisdiction options and ContactUs if the required state is absent.
