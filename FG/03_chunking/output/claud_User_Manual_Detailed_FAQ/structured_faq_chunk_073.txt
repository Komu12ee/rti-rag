## Untitled section

Q070. What do the numbered steps in the Application Registration process mean?

They identify the ordered stages of the Second Appeal workflow: 1 RTI Instruction; 2 Second Appeal Application
Details; 3 PIO and Office Details; 4 RTI Details; 5 First Appeal Office Details; 6 First Appeal Application; 7
Documents Details; and 8 Payment Details. Use the numbered bar to confirm where you are before moving
forward.
