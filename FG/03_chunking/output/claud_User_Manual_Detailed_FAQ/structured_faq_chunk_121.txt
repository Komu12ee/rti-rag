## Untitled section

Q118. Is there an audit trail for all applications?

Full audit-trail functionality is not specified.
The manual says a unique registration number is issued upon application submission for future reference. The
payment-success screen also shows treasury and bank reference numbers. It does not describe a full audit trail,
event history, timestamps for every edit, or access logs.
