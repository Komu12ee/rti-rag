## Untitled section

Q086. What if I enter an inactive email address?

Post-submission email-change process is not specified.
The manual requires a valid and active email address. The activation link is sent to that address, so an inactive
address cannot complete the documented verification and password-creation path. The manual does not state
whether the portal allows the address to be changed after registration submission.
