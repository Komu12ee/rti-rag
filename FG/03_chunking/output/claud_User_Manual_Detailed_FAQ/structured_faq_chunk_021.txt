## Untitled section

Q018. How do I pay the RTI application fee?

At the Payment Details step, tick the applicable fee option and select Pay Fee Online. The portal directs you to the
e-Challan form. Choose the bank name in the Bank Gateway field and select Submit to finalize the payment. A
transaction-success screen is shown after successful payment.
