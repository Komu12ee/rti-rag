## Untitled section

Q015. Is payment mandatory for all applications?

No. The manual expressly says payment is not mandatory when Complaint is selected under Second Appeal
Relation. It also states that no RTI fee is required from a person below the poverty line under the cited RTI Rules,
2012, provided the applicant attaches the required government certificate. For other cases, the manual shows a
payment step but does not publish a complete payment-exemption matrix.
