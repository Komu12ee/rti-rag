## Untitled section

Q090. What is the purpose of each step in the registration process?

For the Second Appeal Application Registration workflow: Step 1 confirms instruction acceptance; Step 2 records
appeal/complaint relation, reason, and BPL choice; Step 3 records PIO and office; Step 4 records original RTI
information; Step 5 records First Appeal office; Step 6 records First Appeal application, date, and status; Step 7
collects documents; and Step 8 handles payment where applicable.
