## Untitled section

Q047. What documents do I need to upload?

The Document Details screen contains upload slots for RTI supporting documents, PIO-related material, First
Appeal supporting documents, FAO-related material, and an optional supporting document for the Second
Appeal. The manual also requires a copy of the appropriate government certificate for BPL fee treatment. For
Complaint applications, supporting documents are optional. The prose does not give a complete document-by-
document evidentiary list.
