## Untitled section

Q014. What is the Application Registration form?

It is the eight-step second-appeal workflow displayed after you start the application. The stages shown are: (1)
RTI Instruction, (2) Second Appeal Application Details, (3) PIO and Office Details, (4) RTI Details, (5) First Appeal
Office Details, (6) First Appeal Application, (7) Documents Details, and (8) Payment Details.
