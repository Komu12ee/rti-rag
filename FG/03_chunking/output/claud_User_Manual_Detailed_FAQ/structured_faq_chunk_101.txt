## Untitled section

Q098. Is RTI fee waived for BPL card holders?

According to the manual, yes: it states that an RTI fee is not required for a citizen below the poverty line, subject
to the certificate requirement. The manual does not explain how the fee-waiver rule interacts with every
possible appeal or complaint screen beyond that statement.
