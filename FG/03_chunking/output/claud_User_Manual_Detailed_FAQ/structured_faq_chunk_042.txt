## Untitled section

Q039. What is the "RTI Apply Date Offline" field?

This is the date field for the original RTI application that was applied offline. The manual calls it RTI Apply Date
Offline and places it with the RTI Details section, indicating that it records the date of the earlier offline RTI filing.
