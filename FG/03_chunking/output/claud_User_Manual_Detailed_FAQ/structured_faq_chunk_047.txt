## Untitled section

Q044. What details are needed for the First Appeal Office?

The First Appeal Office Details screen uses State, District, Base Department, Office Level, Office Name, and Officer
Name dropdowns. If the office or officer is not listed, use the blue link to enter manual details. The manual-entry
screen shows officer name, designation, mobile, office name, office address, district, base department, and sub-
department name.
