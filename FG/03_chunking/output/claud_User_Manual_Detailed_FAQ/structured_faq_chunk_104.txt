## Untitled section

Q101. What is the Right to Information (RTI) Act?

Formal legal definition is not provided in the manual.
The manual identifies RTI as Right to Information and presents a Government of Chhattisgarh portal for RTI-
related filings. A portal screenshot describes RTI as a fundamental right under Article 19(1), but the manual does
not provide the Act's text, scope, exemptions, or authoritative legal interpretation. This guide therefore cannot
substitute for the Act or official legal advice.
