Q089. In what order do I fill the application form?

Follow the numbered workflow in order: RTI Instruction; Second Appeal Application Details; PIO and Office
Details; RTI Details; First Appeal Office Details; First Appeal Application; Documents Details; and Payment
Details. Use Next or Save & Next to advance, and Back where provided to correct prior information.
