## Untitled section

Q020. What is the difference between Appeal and Complaint?

Legal distinction is not specified in the manual.
The manual distinguishes them only operationally within the Second Appeal Relation field. Complaint has two
stated conditions: payment is not mandatory, and supporting documents are optional. It does not give the legal
definitions, eligibility criteria, or jurisdictional differences between Appeal and Complaint.
