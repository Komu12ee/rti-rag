## Untitled section

Q036. What is the Office Level field?

The field definition and value list are not specified.
Office Level is a dropdown field used while selecting the PIO office. The manual shows the field but does not
define the available levels or how to distinguish them. Select the option that corresponds to the office structure
shown by the portal.
