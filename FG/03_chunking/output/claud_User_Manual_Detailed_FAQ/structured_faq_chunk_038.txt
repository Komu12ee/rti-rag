## Untitled section

Q035. What information do I need about the office?

For dropdown selection, you need enough information to identify the State, District, Base Department, Office
Level, Office Name, and Officer Name. For manual entry, the screen additionally asks for designation, mobile
number, office address, and sub-department information. The manual does not provide a separate office-data
checklist beyond these visible fields.
