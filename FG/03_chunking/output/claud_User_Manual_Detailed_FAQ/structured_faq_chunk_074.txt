Q071. What does the green checkmark indicate in the progress bar?

Visual interpretation; no formal legend is supplied.
Across the screenshots, earlier stages turn green after they have been completed, while the current form stage is
shown in red. This is a visual interpretation of the screenshots; the manual does not provide a formal progress-
bar legend.
