## Untitled section

Q026. What documents do I need if I am a BPL card holder?

The instructions state that a BPL applicant must attach a copy of the certificate issued by the appropriate
government along with the application. When Yes is selected in the BPL field, the form also requires BPL
Document Number, BPL Document Issue Date, and BPL Issuing Authority before the application can proceed.
