## Untitled section

Q075. What if I do not have a valid email address?

The manual states that a valid and active email ID is mandatory for user registration. Because account
verification and password creation depend on a link sent to that email, an inactive or inaccessible address will
prevent completion of the documented activation process. The manual does not describe alternative verification
methods.
