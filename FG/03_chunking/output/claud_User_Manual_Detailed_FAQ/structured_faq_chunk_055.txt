## Untitled section

Q052. If I am a BPL card holder, can I apply for a complaint instead of an appeal?

No explicit BPL-versus-Complaint restriction is stated.
The manual does not state that BPL applicants are prohibited from choosing Complaint. It treats Second Appeal
Relation and BPL status as separate selections. If you select Complaint, the manual says payment is not
mandatory and supporting documents are optional; if you select Yes for BPL, the BPL document details still
become mandatory for further processing.
