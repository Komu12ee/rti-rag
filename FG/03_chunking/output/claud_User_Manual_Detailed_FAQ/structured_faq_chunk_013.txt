## Untitled section

Q010. How do I start a Second Appeal application?

Log in with the verified account credentials. On the Applicant Dashboard, select Apply Second Appeal for offline
applications. The Application Registration workflow opens at the RTI Instruction step. Read the instructions, tick
the acceptance box, and select Next to begin completing the eight stages.
