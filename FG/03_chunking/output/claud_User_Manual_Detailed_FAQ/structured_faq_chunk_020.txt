## Untitled section

Q017. What are the payment options available?

The documented method is Pay Fee Online, which redirects the user to an e-Challan page. On that page, the
manual instructs the user to select a bank name (bank gateway) and submit the challan. The manual does not list
supported banks or confirm card, UPI, wallet, cash, or other methods.
