## Untitled section

Q006. What information is required during user registration?

Every field marked with an asterisk (*) is mandatory. From the registration screenshot, the marked fields are
Applicant Type, Name in English, E-mail ID, Mobile No, Caste Type, Gender, Address in English, Pincode, and
District. The screen also shows non-starred fields such as Name in Hindi, parents' names, Age, Address in Hindi,
Country, State, and Residence Type. The manual does not say whether later portal versions use the identical field
set.
