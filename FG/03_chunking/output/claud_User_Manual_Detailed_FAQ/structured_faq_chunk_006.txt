Q003. How do I verify my email account?

After successful registration, open the verification link sent to the email address used during registration. The
link activates the account and opens the password-creation process. Enter the same email address as the login
ID, create and confirm a compliant password, then select Create. A confirmation window indicates that user
credentials were created successfully.
