## Untitled section

Q065. How do I know if my payment was successful?

The manual says a screen is generated upon successful payment. The illustrated e-Challan success screen shows
a Treasury Reference Number, Bank Reference Number, Amount, Date of Account, Date and Time of
Transaction, Bank Name, Status, and a Print Receipt option. These are the documented indicators to retain.
