## Untitled section

Q068. What is the "e-Challan" process?

After you select an applicable fee and choose Pay Fee Online, the portal redirects to an e-Challan form. Review
the populated fields, select a Bank Gateway, and select Submit. On success, the challan screen displays
transaction references and offers a Print Receipt control.
