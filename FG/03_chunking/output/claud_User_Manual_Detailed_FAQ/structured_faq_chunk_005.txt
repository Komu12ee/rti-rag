## Untitled section

Q002. What is the password requirement for the RTI portal?

The manual requires a password with at least 8 characters. It must include at least one lowercase letter, one
uppercase letter, and one special character. The manual does not specify any separate requirement for numbers,
a maximum length, or a password-expiry rule.
