## Untitled section

Q016. How much does the Second Appeal application cost?

The fee-selection rule is not specified in the manual.
The payment screen shown in the manual contains two selectable fee options: Rs. 100 and Rs. 125. The manual
does not explain which option applies to which type of filing, nor does it state that one fixed price applies to
every Second Appeal. Select only the option the live portal identifies as applicable to your filing.
