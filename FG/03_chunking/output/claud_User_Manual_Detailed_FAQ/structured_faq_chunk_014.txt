## Untitled section

Q011. What is the difference between PIO and FAO?

The manual expands PIO as Public Information Officer and FAO as First Appealing Officer. The workflow treats
them as separate offices and officers: one stage captures PIO and Office Details, while a later stage captures First
Appeal Office Details. The manual does not provide a broader legal comparison of their powers or
responsibilities.
