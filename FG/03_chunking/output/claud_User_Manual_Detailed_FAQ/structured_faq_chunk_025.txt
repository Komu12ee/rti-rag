## Untitled section

Q022. How many characters can I write in the reason field?

Field-specific limits beyond the stated 500-character rule are not listed.
The instruction screen states that the application text entered in the prescribed column is confined to 500
characters. It does not separately publish a different limit for every narrative field, including the Second Appeal
Reason field. Treat 500 characters as the manual's stated limit for the prescribed application text and use an
attachment for a longer explanation.
