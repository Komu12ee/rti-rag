## Question: Which registration fields are shown as mandatory in the example screen?

Answer: The manual states that every field marked with an asterisk is mandatory. The illustrated form visibly marks fields such as Applicant Type, Name in English, Email ID, Mobile No. , Caste Type, Gender, Address in English, Pincode, and District. The live portal may display the definitive required fields, so follow its asterisk markings
