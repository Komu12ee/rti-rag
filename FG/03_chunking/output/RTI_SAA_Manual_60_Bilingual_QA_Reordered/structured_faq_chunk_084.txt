## Question: Is BPL status the same as simply not paying an RTI fee?

Answerः The manual distinguishes the two ideas. The instruction page says that no RTI fee is required for a citizen below the poverty line as per the referenced rules, but the applicant must attach a certificate issued by the appropriate government authority. Separately, the BPL screen requires BPL document details after selecting Yes.
