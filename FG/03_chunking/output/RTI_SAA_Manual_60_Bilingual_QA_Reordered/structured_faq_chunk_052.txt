## Question: What is the purpose of the ' Apply Second Appeal for offline applications option?

Answer: The dashboard screenshot identifies this option as the route for applying for a Second Appeal online in relation to an offline application before the Public Information Officer and the First Appellate Officer. Selecting it opens the application registration workflow described in the manual.
