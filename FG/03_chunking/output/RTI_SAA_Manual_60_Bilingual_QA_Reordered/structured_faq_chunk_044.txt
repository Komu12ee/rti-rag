## Question: Are registration details entered again in the Second Appeal form?

Answer: No. The manual states that the user details entered at registration are fetched into the applicant details section. The applicant should still review the displayed details to make sure are correct. If registration data need correction, the manual describes editing during registration, but it does not give a separate correction process within the fetched applicant details display. they
