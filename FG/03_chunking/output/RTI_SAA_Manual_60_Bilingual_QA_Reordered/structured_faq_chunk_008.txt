## Question: What information is requested in the user registration form?

Answerः The displayed form includes applicant type name in English name in Hindi, email ID, mobile number, fathers name, mother's name, caste type, age, gender, address in English, address in Hindi, pincode, country state, district, and residence type. The form indicates that fields marked with an asterisk are mandatory. Users should complete the form carefully because these registration details are later fetched into the applicant details area of the Second Appeal workflow.
