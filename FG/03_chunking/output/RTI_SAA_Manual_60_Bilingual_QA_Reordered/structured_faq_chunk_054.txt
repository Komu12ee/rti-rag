## Question: What are the eight stages shown in the Second Appeal application progress bar?

Answerः The manual shows the following sequence: RTI Instruction, Second Appeal Application Details, PIO and Office Details, RTI Details, First Appeal Office Details, First Appeal Application, Documents Details, and Payment Details. This progress bar helps the user identify the current stage and the remaining stages.
