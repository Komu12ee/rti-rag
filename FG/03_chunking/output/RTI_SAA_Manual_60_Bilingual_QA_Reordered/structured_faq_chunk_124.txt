## Question: Which questions cannot be answered reliably from this manual alone?

Answer: The manual is designed for portal navigation. It does not fully answer case specific legal questions such as statutory appeal deadlines, legal eligibility, the merits of a complaint, exact fee category selection, or the outcome of an individual case. For these, use current official rules, the live portal, or the competent authority.
