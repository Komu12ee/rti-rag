## Question: Does the manual decide whether 1 should choose Appeal or Complaint?

Answer: No. The manual shows that Appeal or Complaint must be selected as the Second Appeal Relation, but it does not provide legal tests for deciding which one applies. It only describes the portal consequences shown for Complaint: payment is not mandatory and supporting documents are optional.
