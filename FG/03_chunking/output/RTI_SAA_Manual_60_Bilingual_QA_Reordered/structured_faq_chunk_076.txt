## Question: What is the role of the Second Appeal Reason Type dropdown?

Answer: In Second Appeal Details, the user must choose a reason type from the available options and then enter the reason for appeal in words. The dropdown standardizes the category, while the text field lets the applicant explain the specific reason. The manual does not supply standard legal wording for the explanation.
