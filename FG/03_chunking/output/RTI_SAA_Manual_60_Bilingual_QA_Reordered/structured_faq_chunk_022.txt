## Question: What should I do on the User Registration General Information screen before filling the form?

Answerः Read the listed registration instructions, tick the declaration confirming that you have read them carefully and then click Next. The manual presents this acknowledgement before the data-entry form. It is therefore the required gateway from the information screen to the registration form.
