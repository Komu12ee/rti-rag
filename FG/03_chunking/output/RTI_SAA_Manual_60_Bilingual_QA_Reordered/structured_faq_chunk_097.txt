## Question: What selection order is shown for PIO details?

Answerः The PIO and Office Details screen shows dropdowns for State, District, Base Department, Office Level, Office Name, and Officer Name. Select the relevant values from the available lists and click Next. The manual presents this as the stage before entering the original RTI application details.
