## Question: Why does the instruction page ask for a mobile number?

Answer: The instruction list says that the applicant or appellant should submit a mobile number to receive SMS alerts. The manual does not define every alert type, but it identifies the mobile number as the route for these notifications. Use a number that can receive messages.
