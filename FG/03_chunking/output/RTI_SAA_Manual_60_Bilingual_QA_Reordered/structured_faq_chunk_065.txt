## Question: What happens if I select Complaint in the Second Appeal Relation?

Answer The manual gives two explicit conditions for Complaint applications: payment is not mandatory and supporting documents are optional. This means the document-upload and payment parts should be understood in light of the Complaint selection. The manual does not state that every other field can be skipped; the user should still complete the information requested by the application workflow.
