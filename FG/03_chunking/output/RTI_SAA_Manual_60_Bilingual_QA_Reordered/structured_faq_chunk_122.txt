## Question: Does the manual provide the legal reason for the payment options displayed?

Answerः No. The manual shows payment options in the interface and instructs the user to select one and proceed to e-Challan. It does not explain the legal criteria for choosing between the displayed options. A chatbot should not invent a fee classification; it should direct the user to current official guidance or the concerned authority
