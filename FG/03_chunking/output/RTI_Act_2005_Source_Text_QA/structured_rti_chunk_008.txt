## Chapter I - Preliminary

Q. What is the source text for the definition of “information”?

Answer - source text
(f) “information” means any material in any form, including records, documents, memos, e-mails, opinions, advices,
press releases, circulars, orders, logbooks, contracts, reports, papers, samples, models,
data material held in any electronic
form and information relating to any
private body which can be accessed
by a public authority under any other law for the time being in force;
