## Chapter II - Right to information and obligations of public authorities

Legal reference: Section 8

Q. What is the source text of Section 8?

Answer - source text
8. (1) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, there shall be no obligation to give any citizen,
(a) information, disclosure of which
would prejudicially affect the sovereignty and integrity of India, the security, strategic, scientific or economic interests of
the State,
relation with foreign State or lead to incitement of an offence;
(b) information which has been
expressly forbidden to be published
