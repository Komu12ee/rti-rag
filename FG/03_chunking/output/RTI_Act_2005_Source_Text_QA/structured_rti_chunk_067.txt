## Chapter VI - Miscellaneous

Legal reference: Section 23

Q. What is the source text of Section 23?

Answer - source text
23. No court shall entertain any suit,
application or other proceeding in
respect of any order made under this
Act and no such order shall be called
in question otherwise than by way of an appeal under this Act.
