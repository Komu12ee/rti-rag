13.* (1) The Chief Information Commissioner
shall hold office for a term of five
years from the date on which he
enters upon his office and shall not be eligible for reappointment:
Provided that no Chief Information
Commissioner shall hold office as
such after he has attained the age of sixty-five years.
(2)* Every Information Commissioner
shall hold office for a term of five
years from the date on which he
enters upon his office or till he attains the age of sixty-five years, whichever is earlier, and shall not be eligible for

Provided that every Information Commissioner shall, on vacating his
office under this subsection be
eligible for appointment as the Chief
Information Commissioner in the manner specified in sub-section (3) of section 12:
Provided further that where the
Information Commissioner is
appointed as the Chief Information Commissioner, his term of office
shall not be more than five years in

Commissioner and the Chief Information Commissioner.
