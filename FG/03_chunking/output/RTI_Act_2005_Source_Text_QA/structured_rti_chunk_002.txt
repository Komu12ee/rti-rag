## Chapter I - Preliminary

Legal reference: Section 1

Q. What is the source text of Section 1?

Answer - source text
1. (1) This Act may be called the Right to Information Act, 2005.
(2) It extends to the whole of India except the State of Jammu and Kashmir.
(3) The provisions of sub-section (1) of section 4, sub-sections (1) and (2) of section 5, sections 12, 13, 15,16, 24, 27 and
28 shall come into force at once, and the remaining provisions of
this Act shall come into force on the
one hundred and twentieth day of its enactment.
