## Chapter I - Preliminary

Q. What is the source text for the definition of “appropriate Government”?

Answer - source text
(a) “appropriate Government” means in
relation to a public authority which is established, constituted, owned,
controlled or substantially financed
by funds provided directly or indirectly -
(i) by the Central Government or the Union territory administration, the Central Government;
(ii) by the State Government, the State Government;
