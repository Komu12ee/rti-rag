## Chapter II - Right to information and obligations of public authorities

Legal reference: Section 9

Q. What is the source text of Section 9?

Answer - source text
9. Without prejudice to the provisions of section 8, a Central Public
Information Officer or a State Public Information Officer, as the case may be, may reject a request for
information where such a request for
providing access would involve an
infringement of copyright subsisting in a person other than the State.
