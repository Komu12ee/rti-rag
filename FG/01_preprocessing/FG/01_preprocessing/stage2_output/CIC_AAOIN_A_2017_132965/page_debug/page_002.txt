Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri Sanmukh Jugani,
General Manager cum CPIO,
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that they had
provided the requisite reply vide their letter dated 17.02.2017 as well as
13.06.2017. The replies furnished to the appellant are just and proper and hence
the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission
that complete reply has been provided vide letters dated 17.02.2017 as well as
13.06.2017. Along with the letter dated 13.06.2017, 80 relevant pages were
also sent to the appellant concerned. The reply provided is just, proper and
comprehensive. Moreover, as the appellant was not present to contest his case,
interference of the Commission is not called for.
With the above observation, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
