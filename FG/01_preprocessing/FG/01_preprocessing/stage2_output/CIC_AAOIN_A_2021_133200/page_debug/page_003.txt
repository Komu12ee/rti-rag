Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 02.02.2021. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 05.04.2021, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is not satisfied with the reply furnished by the
Respondent with respect to the instant RTI Application. He further added
that his primary issue is regarding payment of gratuity as per the government
norms.
