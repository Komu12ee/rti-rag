Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 02.02.2021. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 05.04.2021, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is not satisfied with the reply furnished by the
Respondent with respect to the instant RTI Application. He further added
that his primary issue is regarding payment of gratuity as per the government
norms.

The Respondent submitted that the contents of the instant RTI Application is
a pure grievance and the same should be taken up through appropriate
grievance redressal mechanism for expedited redressal. Further, as far as the
RTI is concerned adequate information as available on record has been
furnished to the Appellant. The Respondent further added that gratuity of the
Appellant has been calculated as per the extant provisions and vide letter
dated 05.08.2020 the Appellant has been informed about the same. Further,
vide reply dated 07.01.2021 the Appellant has been informed that requisite
information is already furnished to him vide letter dated 05.08.2020.

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that sufficient reply has been
provided by the Respondent vide letter dated 07.01.2021 referring to letter
dated 05.08.2020 which gives an adequate explanation about Appellant’s
gratuity payment. The Commission further observes that the Appellant is
harping on redressing his grievance through the instant RTI Application and
the same is beyond the adjudicatory powers of the Commission. Hence, the
Commission finds no further scope of intervention in the instant matter.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
