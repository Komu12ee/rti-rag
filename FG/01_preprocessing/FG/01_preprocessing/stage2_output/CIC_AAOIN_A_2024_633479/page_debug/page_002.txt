Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:
Appellant: Not present
Respondent: Ms. Shalini Sharma, Jt. GM(HR), AAI participated in the
hearing.
The Respondent stated that the exam was conducted through a third-party
agency. She averred that the information sought is exempted under section
8(1)(d) of the RTI Act since the question paper being the intellectual property
of agency any disclosure of the same would affect the competitive position of
the same.
Decision:
Commission, after perusal of case records and submissions made during
hearing, observes that reply furnished by the Respondent is not meticulous.
It is noted that the denial of question paper under section 8(1)(d) of the RTI
Act is not justified. Accordingly, concerned PIO is directed to revisit the
instant RTI Application and provide a revised reply along with necessary
annexures if any , as available in their records, with regards to the instant
RTI Application, to the appellant, free of cost via speed post, within 30 days
from the date of receipt of this order and accordingly compliance report to
this effect be duly sent to the Commission by the PIO. In doing so PIO must
