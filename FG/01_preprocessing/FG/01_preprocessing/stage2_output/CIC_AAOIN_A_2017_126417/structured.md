Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 30.12.2016 sought information
regarding his representation dated 24.10.2016 for investigation of his complaint
against Shri Kislay Garg on three points as under;
1. Details of the investigations done on the appellant’s complaint.
2. Action taken report and noting on his representation dated 24.10.2016.
3. Copy of the reminder sent by Ministry of Civil Aviation along with
certification of documents.

The CPIO’s reply is not on record. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 02.03.2017. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 22.03.2017.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 20.04.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri Amit Ghoshal,
Under Secretary cum CPIO alongwith
Shri V.K. Tanwar,
Joint General Manager,
Ministry of Civil Aviation
During the hearing, the representative of appellant submitted that he had not
received any reply till date.
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had received
the RTI application dated 30.12.2016 on 13.01.2017 and they in turn transferred it
u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act on 17.01.2017 to the RTI cell, HQ., Airport Authority of
India. He submitted that a reply dated 10.02.2017 was provided to the appellant.
However, on perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the
Commission that reply was not addressed to the appellant concerned but was
addressed to the Ministry of Civil Aviation indicating that reply had already been
provided to the appellant. It was also observed that the reply dated 28.11.2016 was
not relevant as it was provided much before the receipt of the present RTI
application. So, the intimation to the Ministry from the AAI that a reply had been
provided to the appellant was also patently wrong.

In view of the above, a Show Cause notice is issued to the then PIO, Shri
P.K. Asiwal, DGM(ES), CHQ., Airports Authority of India, New Delhi u/s 20 of
the RTI Act to explain the following:-
Why no reply to the RTI application dated 30.12.2016 was provided to the
appellant even after the same application was transferred u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act
by the Ministry of Civil Aviation on 17.01.2017.
The explanation to the above stated Show Cause notice is to be submitted to
the Commission by the respondent CPIO/PIO within 15 days of the receipt of this
order. The present respondent CPIO is to serve a copy of this order to the then
respondent CPIO under intimation to the Commission. On receipt of the
explanation to the said Show Cause notice, further action as deemed appropriate
will be taken.
The respondent CPIO should note that in the event of non-submission of the
explanation within the time stipulated above, the Commission has the liberty to
take the required decision ex-parte against the respondent CPIO/PIO.
The Commission also observed that the investigation report of the
complaint against Shri Kislay Garg as sought for in the above mentioned RTI
application is a third party information exempted under section 8(1)(j) of the RTI
Act. However, due procedure as stipulated section 11(1) as specified in the RTI
Act is to be followed before imparting the information.
The Commission therefore directs the concerned CPIO to issue notice u/s
11(1) of the RTI Act to the third party concerned within five days from the receipt
of this order, informing him of the Commission’s order and of the fact that the
respondent is directed to disclose the sought for information subject to his consent
and invite the third party to make submission in writing regarding whether the
sought for information should be disclosed to the appellant. The third party within
ten days from the date of receipt of such notice, shall inform the respondent
authority about his stand in the matter. In case the third party gives his consent, the

Adjunct Order : 11.10.2018
Respondent : Shri P.K. Asiwal, DGM(ES),

Explanation of Sh. P K Asiwal, Jt. GM(ES), RTI Cell-
He submitted that he is posted and working in RTI Cell, Airports Authority
of India, CHQ under the office of CNO RTI and is not/was never the PIO under
RTI Act. In addition to other responsibilities as Jt. GM (ES) RTI Cell, CHQ, his
job was to receive the RTI applications on behalf of AAI and forward them to the
concerned CPIOs to provide the requisite information to the applicant under the
RTI Act, 2005. In the case of appeals also, RTI Cell forwards the same to the
concerned First Appellate Authority to provide the information/disposed of appeals
under the provisions of RTI Act, 2005.
Brief of the case:
The RTI request dated 30.12.2016 of Sh. Ashok Kumar Mangla was
received in RTI Cell, CHQ on 24.01.2017 on transfer from MoCA u/s 6(3) of the
RTI Act, 2005 vide letter No. AV.20036/4(8)/2017-AAI dated 17.01.2017 and the
same was forwarded to Shri. H S Rawat, AGM (HR) & CPIO vide RTI Cell no.
AAI/RTI/Misc/2017/139 dated 30.01.2017 to provide the sought for information to
the applicant.
Even the CIC’s notice of hearing for appeal/complaint on 25.06.2018 in the
matter of SH. Ashok Kumar Mangla was forwarded to Shri Sanjay Jain, Executive
Director (HR) & FAA of the concerned CPIO vide this office (RTI Cell) letter no.
AAI/RTI/CIC hearing/2018/748 dated 08.06.2018. The same notice of the hearing
