Shri H.S Rawat, the then PIO was also present but could not provide a
justifiable explanation for not providing requisite reply to the applicant.
The Commission hereby imposes token penalty of Rs. 5,000/- on Shri H.S
Rawat for not providing the requisite information under the provision of the RTI
Act. Accordingly, he is directed to pay a sum of Rs 5,000/- in 2 equal monthly
instalments. The General Manager, Airport Authority of India is directed to

remit the same by way of demand draft drawn in favour of ‘PAO CAT’ New Delhi
in 2 equal monthly installments. The first installment should reach the Commission
by 20.12.2018 and the last installment should reach by 20.01.2019. The Demand
