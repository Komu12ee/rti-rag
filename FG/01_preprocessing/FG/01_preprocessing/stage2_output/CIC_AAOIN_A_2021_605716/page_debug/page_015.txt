Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that she has not received the relevant information qua
the instant RTI Application from the Respondent.
The Respondent submitted that they have filed a collective affidavit in
reference to which the Appellant is seeking information in the High Court of
Bombay in W.P. No. 3655/2018 which is pending adjudication. She further
added that providing information qua the instant RTI Application requires
collection and collation.
The Commission interjected and remarked the Respondent but this reply was
not given to the Appellant at the first instance.
