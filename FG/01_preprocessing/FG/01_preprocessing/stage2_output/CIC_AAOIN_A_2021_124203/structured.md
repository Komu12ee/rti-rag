Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Representative of the Appellant stated that the information sought vide
instant RTI Application has been arbitrarily denied by the Respondent under
section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. Upon being asked by the Commission to
establish larger public interest for seeking such third party personal
information, the representative of the Appellant could not provide a cogent
explanation.

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has rightly
denied the desired information under section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act and the
Commissions upholds the same. Moreover, the representative of the
Appellant failed to establish larger public interest for seeking such third
party information.
In view of the above, the Commission finds no further scope of intervention
in the instant matter.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
