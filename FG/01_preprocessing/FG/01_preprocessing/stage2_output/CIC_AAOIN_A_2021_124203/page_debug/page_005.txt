CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/124203
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has rightly
denied the desired information under section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act and the
Commissions upholds the same. Moreover, the representative of the
Appellant failed to establish larger public interest for seeking such third
party information.
In view of the above, the Commission finds no further scope of intervention
in the instant matter.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
