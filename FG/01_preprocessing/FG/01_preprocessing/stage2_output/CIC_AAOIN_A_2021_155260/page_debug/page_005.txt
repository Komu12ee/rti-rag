Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant did not participate in the hearing despite being served the
hearing notice.
The Respondent submitted that the information sought by the Appellant is
not readily available in the desired format, however the seniority lists are
already available on the public domain. Upon being queried by the
Commission, the Respondent clarified that the Appellant is seeking category
wise seniority vide instant RTI Application and the same is not maintained
in such manner, therefore no information could be provided to the Appellant.
