Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 25.10.2016 sought information
regarding permission granted by DGCA and aviation authorities under
AAI/20012/442/2004-ARI(NOC) dated 25.03.2008 & 08.12.2009 in respect of two
points as under:
1. Copy of the application submitted by the National General Agencies Ltd
for obtaining the NOC from the AAI.
2. Copies of plans, annexures and attachments submitted by them to the AAI
for obtaining the above stated NOC.

The CPIO replied on 10.11.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 07.12.2016. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 20.01.2017.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 01.06.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri Girish Srivastav,
Joint General Manager cum CPIO,
Airports Authority of India, Mumbai
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their preliminary letter dated 10.11.2016, the final reply on
21.04.2017 and the First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 20.01.2017.
The replies furnished to the appellant are just and proper and hence the case might
be dismissed.
The reply dated 21.04.2017 was not available in the case record.
Accordingly, the respondent PIO was asked to read the same over the VC facility.
He was intimated to send a copy of the same to the Commission through e-mail for
record.
The appellant submitted that he had not received any reply dated 21.04.2017
from the respondent authority concerned.
The respondent PIO is directed to resend the reply dated 21.04.2017 within
07 days of the receipt of this order to the appellant by speed post keeping the postal
tracking number as record. The despatch details are to be submitted to the
Commission within 07 more days thereafter.

On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the sought for information is a third party information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) and
8(1)(e) of the RTI Act. This information should not have been provided to the
appellant without following due procedure as envisaged u/s 11(1) of the RTI Act.
However, the first appellate authority in its order dated 20.01.2017 had directed the
CPIO to provide all the sought for information to the appellant concerned. This
incident shows serious lack of understanding of the RTI Act on the part of the first
appellate authority.
An advisory is issued u/s 25(5) of the RTI Act to the Chairman, Airports
Authority of India to organise basic training programmes on RTI Act/matters to
the officers (respondent CPIO/PIO and FAA)/ and staff dealing with RTI matters
working in the Airports Authority of India without any further delay. The
Commission desires that this advice should be viewed seriously and an action
taken report be submitted to the Commission within one month of the receipt of the
order.
With the above observation/advisory, the appeals are disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
