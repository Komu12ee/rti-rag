Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 28.02.2017 sought information on
five points as under;
1. Certified copy of his performance appraisal report (PAR).
2. Certified copy of Individual performance Appraisal report (PAR).
3. Other related information.
The First Appellate Authority (FAA) vide order dated 19.05.2017 disposed
of the first appeal. Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from
the respondent authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of
Section 19 of the RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on
22.06.2017.

Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri R.S. Jagpal along with
Smt. Shalini Sharma, Assistant General Manager
cum PIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had complied
with the order of the first appellate authority on 06.06.2017. The reply furnished to
the appellant is just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for this case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
FAA had stated categorically in its reply dated 19th May, 2017 which was
addressed to Shri N.Balachandran, GM(HR)/PIO and Ms. Maya Lavania,
GM(HR)/PIO stating therein that the “PIO did not respond to the appellant with
respect to his RTI request dated 28.02.2017 and therefore the appellant filed the
first appeal for no response within the time limit, which is a violation of the
provisions of the RTI Act, 2005.”
During the hearing, the two respondent PIOs who were present at the
hearing viz. Shri R.S. Jagpal along with Smt. Shalini Sharma, Assistant General
Manager cum PIO, Airports Authority of India submitted a reply dated 06.06.2017
only on point nos. 3,4 and 5 of the above stated RTI application. In regard to point
nos. 1 and 2, an early reply (in hindi) was sent to the appellant which the
Commission wanted to peruse.
After perusing the same, it was noted by the Commission that the reply was
very remotely connected with the information sought in this case. Moreover, the
Commission finds no reply as ordered by the FAA.

It was noted that on point no. 5 of the said RTI application, the respondent
PIO had rightly quoted exemption u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. On other points
although the respondent could have claimed exemption, he chose to disclose the
sought for information to the appellant. The Commission did not like to comment
on it.
In view of the above, the Commission is constrained to issue warning to the
then PIOs, Shri N.Balachandran, GM(HR)/PIO and Ms. Maya Lavania,
GM(HR)/PIO.
The then respondent PIOs are issued warning that full, final and
comprehensive reply to an RTI application should have been provided within the
time period as stipulated under the RTI Act and he should ensure that in future in
every case reply to an RTI application is invariably provided within 30 days of
receipt of the said application.
The respondent PIOs should note that in future if the same mistake is
noticed by the Commission, more stringent action can be taken against them by
the Commission.
The present respondent PIO is directed to serve copies of this warning to the
then PIOs and a copy of the service of the above said warning be submitted to the
Commission within 10 days of the receipt of the order.
The respondent PIOs should note that in regard to point no. 2, the
information pertaining to the appellant should only be provided and no other
information pertaining to the third party should be provided being third party
information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act.
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case, the present respondent CPIO is directed to provide revised
point wise reply on point nos. 1 and 2 as discussed during the hearing complete in
