It was noted that on point no. 5 of the said RTI application, the respondent
PIO had rightly quoted exemption u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. On other points
although the respondent could have claimed exemption, he chose to disclose the
sought for information to the appellant. The Commission did not like to comment
on it.
In view of the above, the Commission is constrained to issue warning to the
then PIOs, Shri N.Balachandran, GM(HR)/PIO and Ms. Maya Lavania,
GM(HR)/PIO.
The then respondent PIOs are issued warning that full, final and
comprehensive reply to an RTI application should have been provided within the
time period as stipulated under the RTI Act and he should ensure that in future in
every case reply to an RTI application is invariably provided within 30 days of
receipt of the said application.
The respondent PIOs should note that in future if the same mistake is
noticed by the Commission, more stringent action can be taken against them by
the Commission.
The present respondent PIO is directed to serve copies of this warning to the
then PIOs and a copy of the service of the above said warning be submitted to the
Commission within 10 days of the receipt of the order.
The respondent PIOs should note that in regard to point no. 2, the
information pertaining to the appellant should only be provided and no other
information pertaining to the third party should be provided being third party
information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act.
