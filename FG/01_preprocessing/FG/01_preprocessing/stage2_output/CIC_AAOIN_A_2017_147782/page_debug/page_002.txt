Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri R.S. Jagpal along with
Smt. Shalini Sharma, Assistant General Manager
cum PIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had complied
with the order of the first appellate authority on 06.06.2017. The reply furnished to
the appellant is just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for this case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
FAA had stated categorically in its reply dated 19th May, 2017 which was
addressed to Shri N.Balachandran, GM(HR)/PIO and Ms. Maya Lavania,
GM(HR)/PIO stating therein that the “PIO did not respond to the appellant with
respect to his RTI request dated 28.02.2017 and therefore the appellant filed the
first appeal for no response within the time limit, which is a violation of the
provisions of the RTI Act, 2005.”
During the hearing, the two respondent PIOs who were present at the
hearing viz. Shri R.S. Jagpal along with Smt. Shalini Sharma, Assistant General
Manager cum PIO, Airports Authority of India submitted a reply dated 06.06.2017
only on point nos. 3,4 and 5 of the above stated RTI application. In regard to point
nos. 1 and 2, an early reply (in hindi) was sent to the appellant which the
Commission wanted to peruse.
After perusing the same, it was noted by the Commission that the reply was
very remotely connected with the information sought in this case. Moreover, the
Commission finds no reply as ordered by the FAA.
