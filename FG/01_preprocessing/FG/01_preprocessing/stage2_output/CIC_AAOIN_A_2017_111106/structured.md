Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 08.09.2016 sought information
on who is the authority to order transfer of Sr. Superintendent (E-2) F/S, i.e.
whether it is within the power exercised by the RHQ or the CHQ. The CPIO
replied on 28.10.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the reply of the
CPIO and filed a first appeal on 16.11.2016. The First Appellate Authority
(FAA)’s order was not on record. Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired
information from the respondent authority, the appellant filed a second appeal
under the provision of Section 19 of the RTI Act before the Central Information
Commission on 11.02.2017.

Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri K.P. Ghosh,
General Manager cum PIO,
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letter dated 28.10.2016. The reply furnished to the
appellant is just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant submitted that he was not satisfied with the reply received
from the respondent as no specific hyper link was mentioned while providing
the said reply.
During the hearing, the respondent PIO further submitted that not only
the relevant hyperlink was provided in their reply but hard copy of the reply was
also provided to the appellant on 04.05.2017.
On perusal of the case record, it was seen that though the reply provided
is just, proper and comprehensive, the point remains that the respondent had
provided a hyperlink and the appellant could have downloaded from the link the
relevant information which he did not do. Later on, a hard copy of the reply was
also provided to him. Interference of the Commission is therefore not called for
in the present case.
With the above observation, the appeal is disposed of.
