CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/142836
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is dissatisfied with the reply furnished by the
Respondent because information with respect to point 1 has been denied and
wrong information with respect to point 2 & 3 has been given.
The Respondent submitted that relevant information as available on record
with respect to point 2 & 3 of the RTI Application has been furnished to the
Appellant. The Commission asked the Respondent to substantiate its reply
with respect to point 1 of the RTI Application, to which the Respondent
submitted that the said reply was furnished by the then CPIO who has now
transferred. However, the same was denied under section 8(1)(d) of the RTI
Act since it contains DPCs of other persons also.
The Commission remarked that such denial under section 8(1)(d) of the RTI
Act, is inappropriate and erroneous and the same should have been denied
under section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. The Respondent concurred with the
above remark of the Commission.
The Commission queried the Respondent if the information sought with
respect to point 1 of the instant RTI Application could be furnished to the
Appellant by invoking severability clause as per section 10 of the RTI Act,
to which the Respondent affirmatively agreed.
