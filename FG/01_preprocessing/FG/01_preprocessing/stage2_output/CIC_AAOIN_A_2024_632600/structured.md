Information sought and background of the case:
The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 20.06.2024 seeking information on the
following points:-
“Copy of complete Note sheet where Chairman, AAI decision noted regarding
Filling up vacancies in Security Directorate on Lateral Movement basis (2021-
2022).”
The CPIO, Airports Authority of India vide letter dated 19.07.2024 replied as under:-
“The question is not specific.”
Dissatisfied with the response received from the CPIO, the Appellant filed a First
Appeal dated 20.07.2024. The FAA vide order dated 26.07.2024 held as under:-
3.1 “Having gone through the matter, it is observed that the CPIO, Shri Jeta Sinha, GM(HR)
vide letter No.A.60011/01/2024-ES-III/238276 dated 19.07.2024 has provided the
reply/information to the appellant.
3.2 It would also be pertinent to mention here that a citizen has a right to seek information
from a public authority which is held by it or which is held under its control. It is important
to note that only such information can be supplied under the Act that is available and
existing and is held by the public authority. It is also important to note that the specific
information can only be furnished under the provisions of the RTI Act, 2005. A non-specific
information is no information under the RTI Act, 2005. Thus, a public authority can only
provide the information as defined in the term 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI
Act, 2005.

3.3 Accordingly, the online first appeal stands disposed of.”
Aggrieved and dissatisfied, the Appellant approached the Commission with the
instant Second Appeal.
Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:
A written submission dated 25.03.2025 has been received from the Appellant
contending that denial of the aforementioned information has deprived him of his
legitimate right being “SC” Executive.
Written submission dated 30.07.2025 has also been received from the PIO, AAI
reiterating the aforementioned facts about the PIO’s reply and the FAA’s order.
Hearing was scheduled after giving prior notice to both the parties.
Appellant: Represented by Shri Narayan Chandra Parida
Respondent: Shri S Suriyanarayanan – PIO/ Joint General Manager was present
during hearing.
The Appellant’s representative simply reiterated the query as mentioned in the RTI
Application. He was unable to state anything further to narrate anything beyond the
same.
The Respondent reiterated from the PIO’s reply, FAA’s order and placed reliance on
the written submission dated 30.07.2025 mentioned hereinabove to substantiate the
fact that response had been duly sent to the Appellant. He maintained that in the
absence of any specific query, no further information could be provided to the
Appellant. He added that policy with respect to filling up of vacancies is available in
public domain.
Decision:
Upon perusal of records of the case and after hearing submissions of both parties, it
is evident that appropriate reply based on records available with the public authority
had been duly sent by the PIO, in terms of the provisions of the RTI Act.
The Commission hereby directs the PIO to send a copy of the written
submission dated 30.07.2025, to the Appellant, within two weeks of receipt of this
order. The Respondent shall also submit a compliance report in this regard before the
Commission, within one week thereafter. No further intervention is warranted in this
case, under the RTI Act.
The appeal is disposed off accordingly.
