Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that she is not satisfied with the reply provided by the
Respondent qua the instant RTI Application.
The Respondent submitted that with respect to point no. 1 to 5, 20 and 23 of
the RTI Application, adequate information, as available on record, has been
provided to the Appellant. She further added that with respect to point no. 6
to 19 of the instant RTI Application, the Appellant is seeking personal
information of third party which is exempted from disclosure under Section
8 1 (j) of the RTI Act.
The Commission interjected and quizzed the Appellant to establish larger
public interest in seeking third party information, qua point no. 6 to 19 of the
instant RTI Application, she could not provide a cogent reply. She further
expressed her desire to obtain the information of the co-accused officers in
point no. 6 to 19 of the instant RTI Application. She furthermore stated the
Respondent has provided a vague reply to point no. 21 of the RTI
Application.
