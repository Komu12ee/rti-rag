Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he has not received the relevant information as
sought in the instant RTI Application from the Respondent. He further added
that with respect to point no. 1 of RTI Application, no opportunity was given
to him by the Respondent Public Authority.
The Respondent submitted that adequate reply qua the instant RTI
Application has been given to the Appellant vide letter dated 18.11.2021 and
13.12.2021.
The Commission interjected and remarked the Respondent that the
information sought by the Appellant is very much specific. The Respondent
concurred with the remarks of the Commission.

The Commission admonishes Ms. Nivedita Dubey, General Manager
(HR)/PIO, for providing such mindless and incongruous replies. The
Commission cautions the Respondent to be careful in future while dealing
with matters pertaining to the RTI Act.
Hence, the Commission deems it fit to direct Ms. Nivedita Dubey, General
Manager (HR)/PIO, to revisit the instant RTI Application and search their
database/records in a prudent manner to find/locate the relevant information
and provide a revised and categorical reply to the Appellant, with a copy
marked to the Commission. The said directions should be complied within
21 days, from the date of receipt of this order. In the event PIO is required to
seek further assistance from concerned office/department for compliance
with the above directions, the same shall be sought under Section 5(4) of
RTI Act.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
