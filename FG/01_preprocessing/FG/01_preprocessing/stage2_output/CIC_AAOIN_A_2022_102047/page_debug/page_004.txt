CIC/AAOIN/A/2022/102047
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he has not received the relevant information as
sought in the instant RTI Application from the Respondent. He further added
that with respect to point no. 1 of RTI Application, no opportunity was given
to him by the Respondent Public Authority.
The Respondent submitted that adequate reply qua the instant RTI
Application has been given to the Appellant vide letter dated 18.11.2021 and
13.12.2021.
The Commission interjected and remarked the Respondent that the
information sought by the Appellant is very much specific. The Respondent
concurred with the remarks of the Commission.
