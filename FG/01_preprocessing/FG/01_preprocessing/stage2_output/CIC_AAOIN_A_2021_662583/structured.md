Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is not satisfied with the information provided by
the Respondent qua the instant RTI Application.
The Commission interjected and quizzed the Appellant to establish larger
public interest in seeking such sort of information, the Appellant apprised
the bench that Vistara Airlines is collecting additional charges from the
passengers but only paying an amount of Rs. 5000 per scheduled domestic
departure towards Regional Connectivity Fund (RCF) to the Regional
Connectivity Scheme (RCS) fund.
The Respondent submitted that information sought in point no. 1, 3 and 5
the instant RTI Application pertains to Vistara Airlines which is private
authority. He further added that after directions passed by the First Appellate

same to the Appellant, with a copy marked to the Commission, within 15
days from the date of receipt of this order.
