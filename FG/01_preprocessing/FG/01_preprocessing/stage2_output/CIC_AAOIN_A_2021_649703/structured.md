Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he has not received the relevant information as
sought in the instant RTI Application from the Respondent.
The Respondent submitted that with respect to point no. 1 of the instant RTI
Application, no such file noting qua the averred letter dated 13.11.2017 is
available in their record. She further added that with respect to point no. 2 of
the instant RTI Application, there is no Manpower Assessment Board
decision with reference to the averred letter dated 13.11.2017 in their record.
The Commission interjected and remarked the Respondent that this reply
should have been given at the first instance rather than saying that the
information is not specific. At the instance of the Commission whether the
direction passed by the First Appellate Authority has been complied with or

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
provided a reply to the Appellant vide letter dated 13.09.2021 on the basis of
available records but the Appellant has expressed his dissatisfaction over the
same and in order to allay his apprehension, the Commission deems it fit to
direct the Respondent to revisit the instant RTI Application and furnish a
revised reply by making an effort to search their database/records in a
prudent manner to find/locate the relevant information qua the instant RTI
Application, to the Appellant, with a copy marked to the Commission. The
said directions should be complied within 21 days, from the date of receipt
of this order. In the event CPIO is required to seek further assistance from
concerned office/department for compliance with the above directions, the
same shall be sought under Section 5(4) of RTI Act.
