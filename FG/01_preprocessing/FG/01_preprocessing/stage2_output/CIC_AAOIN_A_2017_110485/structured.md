Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 06.06.2016 sought information
on six points relating to M/S Dinesh Construction for cutting of trees near
Jaipur airport; amounts paid for the above work along with the copies indicating
such copies of payments, advertisement(s) relating to the notice inviting tender
for the above mentioned work, copy of the tender proposal received, copy of the
work order, details on the number of trees cleared in connection with the above

mentioned work, details of the amounts received after selling the woods and
other related information. The CPIO replied on 14.07.2016. The appellant was
not satisfied with the CPIO’s reply and filed first appeal on 01.08.2016. The
First Appellate Authority (FAA) vide order dated 15.11.2016 disposed of the
first appeal. Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the
respondent authority, the appellant filed a second appeal under the provision of
Section 19 of the RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on
08.02.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri Jagjit Singh Balhara,
Director cum PIO,
Airport Authority of India, Jaipur
Shri S.K. Arora,
Assistant General Manager cum PIO,
Airport Authority of India, New Delhi.
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letter dated 14.07.2016 and the First Appellate
Authority (FAA)’s order dated 15.11.2016. The reply furnished to the appellant
is just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the case record, it was seen that there were two sets of
replies provided by the concerned General Manager and CPIO Regional
Headquarters, Northern Region, New Delhi on 13.06.2016 and another reply
from respondent CPIO dated 15.07.2016 on point nos. 6(i) to 6(iv) which is not
considered proper . The appellant in its second appeal memo had contested the
replies on point nos. 6(i) to 6(iv). The rest of the replies provided were
considered just and proper by the appellant.

However, after the filing of the first appeal by the appellant, the
respondent PIO, Shri Jagjit Singh Balhara, Director cum PIO, Airport
Authority of India, Jaipur had provided a revised reply in regard to point nos.
6(i) to 6(iv) vide their letter dated 20.10.2016.
On perusal of the case record, it was seen that the Commission finds no
infirmity in the above stated reply and also observed that a point wise reply was
provided which was in accordance with the term and conditions of order passed
in connection with the work entrusted to M/s. Dinesh Construction. However it
was noticed by the Commission that copies of the relevant terms and conditions
were not enclosed with the above said reply and it was mentioned only that the
same were provided earlier. In the interest of justice, the respondent PIO,
Airport Authority of India, New Delhi is directed to provide certified copies of
the above said terms and conditions, free of charge u/s 7(6) of the RTI Act
within 10 days of the receipt of the order to the appellant.
The respondent CPIO is further directed to send a report containing the
copy of the revised reply and the date of despatch of the same to the RTI
appellant within 07 days thereafter to the Commission for record.
With the above observation/direction, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
