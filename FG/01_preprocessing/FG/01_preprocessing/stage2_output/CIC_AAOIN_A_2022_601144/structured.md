Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that no information with respect to the instant RTI
Application has been provided by the Respondent till date.
The Respondent submitted that since all the points of the instant RTI
Application are in query form and situation specific they were considered to
be beyond the purview of definition of ‘information’ as per section 2(f) of
the RTI Act. However, upon revisiting the instant RTI Application it has
been observed that the relevant information is already available on the public
domain and the same can be accessed by the Appellant.
The Respondent further submitted that vide point 1, 2 & 4 of the RTI
Application the Appellant is seeking situation specific information with
respect to guidelines which is not available on record, but guidelines sought
vide point 3 is already available on the website. The Appellant raised
contention regarding non availability of the same on the website so the
Commission in order to verify checked the website during hearing and found
it available.
The Commission queried the Respondent regarding information on rest of
the points, to which the Respondent submitted vide points 5-14 of the RTI
Application the Appellant is seeking information regarding various reports
and it is a subject matter pertaining to the Safdarjung Airport which is an

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though the Respondent
has attended the instant RTI Application within the stipulated time frame,
the reply furnished by them is evasive and inappropriate. However, the
Respondent has provided adequate explanation with respect to point 1-4 of
the RTI Application and the Commission finds no infirmity with the same.
Nevertheless, as regards the information with respect to point 5-14 of the
RTI Application, the Commission deems it fit to direct the Respondent to
furnish a revised categorical reply to the Appellant after obtaining the
requisite information on the averred points from the concerned custodian
within the time frame of 15 days from the date of receipt of this order.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
