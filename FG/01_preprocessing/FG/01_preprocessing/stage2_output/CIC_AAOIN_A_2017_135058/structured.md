Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 02.02.2017 sought inspection of
all files of Vehicle Parking Rights Contract/tender for which a technical bid was
opened on 22.12.2016, especially the appellant wanted to inspect the records of
the parties who participated in the above said tender, notings of AAI in this
regard, comparative statement, charts of prices quoted by these parties who
participated in the said tender etc. The CPIO provided an interim reply on
02.03.2017. On receiving no final response from the CPIO, the appellant filed
first appeal on 04.03.2017. The First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order is not

During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that they had
provided the requisite reply vide their letter dated 02.03.2017 and the first
appellate authority (FAA)’s order dated 31.05.2017. In compliance of the First
Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order, the respondent authority vide its letter dated
11.07.2016 had invited the appellant for joint inspection of relevant record and
the appellant had inspected the concerned files.
Since the letter dated 31.05.2017 was not available in the case record, the
respondent PIO was asked to read the same over the VC facility. He was
intimated to send a copy of the same to the Commission through e-mail for
record.
The appellant submitted that joint inspection of relevant records was
undertaken by him along with the PIO concerned and that he was satisfied with
the result of the joint inspection.
However, he submitted that the joint inspection of relevant records took
place after a delay of about four months.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission
that the reply provided is just, proper and comprehensive and the delay involved
