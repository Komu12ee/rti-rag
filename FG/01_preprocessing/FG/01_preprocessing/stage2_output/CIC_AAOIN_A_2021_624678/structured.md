Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant did not participate in the hearing despite being served the
hearing notice.
The Respondent submitted that information sought vide instant RTI
Application is vague and non-specific due to which it cannot be deciphered
what is exactly being sought by the Appellant.
The Commission asked the Respondent to further elaborate the above stance,
to which the Respondent submitted that in the year 2021 the department has
conducted approximately 4-5 recruitments for various posts and categories
and it cannot be made out from the instant RTI Application that for which
recruitment process information is required. Therefore the contents of the
instant RTI Application falls beyond the purview of section 2(f) of the RTI
Act.

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
furnished an appropriate reply with respect to the instant RTI Application
and the Commission is of the considered opinion that appellant has neither
specifically mentioned the selection process nor the specific posts. So, the
Commission upholds the stance taken by the Respondent and no relief in the
instant matter can be provided to the Appellant.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
