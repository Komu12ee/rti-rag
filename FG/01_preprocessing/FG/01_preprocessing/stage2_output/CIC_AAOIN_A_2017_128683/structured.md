Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 12.01.2017 sought information
on five points as under:
1. Copy of complete documents related to the process of recruitment of
Jr. Executive (HR) conducted in the year 2012 by AAI.
2. Cut off marks for general category candidates for Jr. Executive (HR)
conducted in the year 2016.
3. Marks secured by the appellant bearing roll no 2803250198 in the
above said examination.
4. Other related information.

The CPIO replied on 06.02.2017. The appellant was not satisfied with
the reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 04.03.2017. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA)’s disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated
22.03.2017. Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the
respondent authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of
Section 19 of the RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on
25.04.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri Rishykesh Priyadarsay,
Deputy General Manager cum CPIO,
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that they had
provided the requisite reply vide their letter dated 06.02.2017 and the First
Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 22.03.2017. The replies furnished to
the appellant are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for her case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission
that proper reply was not provided to the appellant on point no. 5. However, on
this point, the respondent submitted that since it was an online examination, no
record is available for the same. The rest of the replies are considered to be just
and proper by the Commission.
The respondent authority is directed either to file an affidavit that no
record is available on point no. 5 or to provide information in this regard.
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the
appellant in the present case, the respondent CPIO is directed to provide revised
reply on point no. 5 as discussed during the hearing complete in all respects to
the appellant as available on record (legible copies), free of charge u/s 7(6) of
the RTI Act within 15 days of the receipt of the order.
