The appellant on not receiving any reply on time filed first appeal on
28.11.2016. The First Appellate Authority (FAA) vide order dated 13.01.2017
disposed of the first appeal. The CPIO replied on 17.01.2017. Aggrieved with the
non-supply of the desired information from the respondent authority, the appellant
filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the RTI Act before the
Central Information Commission on 23.03.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri Pramod Kumar,
Deputy General Manager cum PIO,
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had provided the
requisite reply vide their letter dated 17.01.2017 and the First Appellate Authority
(FAA)’s order dated 13.01.2017. The reply furnished to the appellant is just and
proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant submitted that he received the relevant reply from the
respondent on point no. 1 on 30.03.2017 only. He received no relevant reply on
para 2 of the said RTI application.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
for point no. 2 i.e. copy of the plan submitted by M/s Acme Realities Pvt. to the
Civil Aviation department is a third party information exempted under Section
8(1)j of the RTI Act. However, the same could have been provided to the appellant
if the respondent authority have followed the procedure stipulated under section
11(1) of the RTI Act timely. The CPIO, Shri K.P Jose submitted that notice u/s
11(1) was issued on 19.06.2018 to the third party i.e. M/s Acme Realities Pvt. for
seeking its consent to impart with the sought for information but no response has
been received from them till date.

The Commission observed that even after FAA order dated 13.01.2017, the
CPIO did not take any prompt action which is deplorable. However, since the then
CPIO has retired from his services, issuance of any warning will not serve any
purpose. The Commission, therefore, finds it appropriate to issue an advisory to the
CMD, Airport Authority of India to ensure that that case like this does not happen
in future.
An advisory is issued u/s 25(5) of the RTI Act to CMD, Airport Authority of
India to organise basic training programmes on RTI Act/matters to the officers
(respondent CPIO/PIO and FAA)/ and staff dealing with RTI matters working in
Airport Authority of India without any further delay. The Commission desires
that this advice should be viewed seriously and action taken report be submitted to
the Commission within one month of the receipt of the order.
In view of the fact that the third party had not submitted any
objections/consent when the respondent authority had approached it vide its letter
dated 19.06.2018 to make a representation against the proposed disclosure of the
information, the Commission considers that the third party is sleeping over his
right and is of the view that in the absence of any express objection from the third
party, the said information can be disclosed to promote transparency. The CPIO is,
therefore, directed to provide complete information as sought for on point no. 2 of
the above said RTI application to the appellant within 7 days from the receipt of
this order, free of cost u/s 7(6) of the RTI Act.
With the above direction /advisory, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
