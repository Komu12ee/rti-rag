Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
provided adequate reply qua the instant RTI Application to the Appellant
vide letter dated 05.04.2021 at that point of time. The Commission further
observes that as the departmental inquiry proceedings against the Appellant
have been culminated in the month of February, 2022, the Commission
deems it fit to direct the Respondent to intimate the latest position to the
