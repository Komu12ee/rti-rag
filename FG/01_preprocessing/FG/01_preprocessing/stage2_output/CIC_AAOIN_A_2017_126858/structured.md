Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 20.12.2016 sought information regarding
authorization for landing of aeroplanes in Jodhpur airport as under;
1. List of passenger aeroplanes, names of their companies and arrival and
departure timings of flights to and from the Jodhpur airport.
2. List of aeroplanes which were kept waiting for Flight Landing Birth from
01.01.2016 to 31.12.2017, details of periods of waiting of these flights,
reason(s) for them keeping such flights waiting for the Flight Landing Birth.
The CPIO replied on 03.01.2017. The appellant was not satisfied with the
CPIO’s reply and filled first appeal on 09.02.2017. The CPIO again replied on
