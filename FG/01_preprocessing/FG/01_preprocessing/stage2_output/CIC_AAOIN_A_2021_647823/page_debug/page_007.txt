Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the information sought
pertains to third party and as such it has been rightly denied by the
Respondent. Moreover, the Appellant has failed to establish larger public
interest for seeking such third party information. In view of the above the
Commission deems it fit to uphold the stance taken by the Respondent.
