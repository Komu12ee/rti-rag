Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is not satisfied with the reply furnished with
respect to the instant RTI Application by the Respondent because the
information sought has been arbitrarily denied by them.
The Respondent submitted that since the information sought is regarding
DPC of Smt. Manisha Kamble and since it is a third party information it falls
under the category of exempted information and as such could not be
provided to the Appellant.
The Commission queried the Respondent whether they have obtained
consent/dissent of the concerned third party for divulging such information,
to which the Respondent replied in negative. The Commission further
remarked that the CPIO vide reply dated 24.05.2021 denied to furnish the
requisite information under section 11(1) of the RTI Act but Section 11 of
the RTI Act is not a denial clause, rather it’s a procedural clause whereby
consent/dissent is sought from the person to whom the information pertains,
therefore the CPIO should have denied the information invoking the
appropriate exemption clause as rightly done by the First Appellate
Authority vide order dated 13.07.2021. The Respondent concurred with the
above remark of the Commission.
The Appellant interjected and expressed dissatisfaction with the stance taken
by the Respondent, so the Commission queried the Appellant to establish
larger public interest for seeking such third party personal information to
which the Appellant could not provide cogent explanation.
