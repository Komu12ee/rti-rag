Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 10.11.2016 sought information
regarding the death of his wife while boarding a flight at Leh Airport on two points
as under:
1. CCTV footage on 12.10.2016 at Leh Airport from 7.15 a.m to 9.15 a.m
till boarding the flight G8-204 concerning images of his family members as well as
the doctor who had attended the ailing wife by administering her oxygen etc.
2. CCTV footage on 12.10.2016 inside the aircraft G8-204.

The CPIO replied on 05.01.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal. The First Appellate Authority (FAA)
disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 31.03.2017. Aggrieved with the
non-supply of the desired information from the respondent authority, the appellant
filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the RTI Act before the
Central Information Commission on nil.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Interim Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Absent
The respondent is absent despite valid and timely notice.
The respondent was absent despite valid and timely notice of the CIC for
which warning is issued to the concerned respondent PIO, Assistant Manager,
Airport Authority of India, KBR Airport NH 1D, Leh, Jammu and Kashmir. The
concerned PIO should desist from committing such lapse in future. He should
either take prior permission of the Commission before absenting himself from the
hearing or he should send proper representative to the Commission’s hearing after
obtaining due permission from the Commission for doing so and after briefing the
authorised representative duly.
The concerned registry of this bench is directed to send a copy of this order
to the CMD, Airport Authority of India for information and necessary remedial
action and action taken report be submitted to the Commission within one month
of the receipt of the order.
On perusal of the relevant case records, it was noted by the Commission that
the presence of the respondent authority is important for deciding the case as a
larger public interest involving passenger safety is involved in this case and also to

On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the respondent PIO had claimed in his reply that the said CCTV footage was auto
deleted after 20 days, hence, relevant records as sought for in the said RTI
application are not found available. He is directed to file an affidavit in this regard
to the Commission.
The appellant submitted during the hearing that even after such auto deletion
of CCTV footage records, suitable technology exists for recovery of the deleted
footage.

The present respondent CPIO, is directed to submit an affidavit indicating
that when the RTI application was received, the entire relevant records of the
CCTV footage were already auto deleted as per schedule of preservation of these
records prevailing at that time and further that effort for data recovery did not
succeed at the appropriate time. This affidavit is to be submitted within one month
of the receipt of this order with a copy duly endorsed to the appellant within the
same time period.
The Commission also noted that the preservation of CCTV footage and its
auto deletion has become a matter of concern as a number of RTI applications had
been dealt in the CIC as second appeal cases in which appellants had requested for
copies of CCTV footages. However, every time the respondents take the same plea
as discussed above(auto deletion) and that no technology exists for recovery of the
deleted CCTV footage data.
An advisory is issued u/s 25(5) of the RTI Act to the Chairman, Airport
Authority of India for reviewing this procedure for preservation of CCTV footages
of airport premises all over India so that such denial can be involved in a minimum
no. of cases only.
With the above observation/direction/advisory, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
