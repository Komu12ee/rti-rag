The present respondent CPIO, is directed to submit an affidavit indicating
that when the RTI application was received, the entire relevant records of the
CCTV footage were already auto deleted as per schedule of preservation of these
records prevailing at that time and further that effort for data recovery did not
succeed at the appropriate time. This affidavit is to be submitted within one month
of the receipt of this order with a copy duly endorsed to the appellant within the
same time period.
The Commission also noted that the preservation of CCTV footage and its
auto deletion has become a matter of concern as a number of RTI applications had
been dealt in the CIC as second appeal cases in which appellants had requested for
copies of CCTV footages. However, every time the respondents take the same plea
as discussed above(auto deletion) and that no technology exists for recovery of the
deleted CCTV footage data.
An advisory is issued u/s 25(5) of the RTI Act to the Chairman, Airport
Authority of India for reviewing this procedure for preservation of CCTV footages
of airport premises all over India so that such denial can be involved in a minimum
no. of cases only.
With the above observation/direction/advisory, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
