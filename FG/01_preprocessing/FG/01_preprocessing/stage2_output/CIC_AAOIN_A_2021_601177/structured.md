Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for
and take appropriate legal action against the Respondent.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that she has not received the relevant information qua
the instant RTI Application from the Respondent.
The Respondent submitted they have not received the averred email in their
office qua which the Appellant has sought the information in the RTI
Application. He further added that the said information might be available in
Regional Executive Director of western and southern region of their office.
At the instance of the Commission whether they have transferred the instant
RTI Application under Section 6 (3) of the RTI Act to the concerned
custodian of the records, the Respondent replied in negative.
At the instance of the Commission whether the Respondent has invoked the
provision of Section 5 (4) of the RTI Act and tried to obtain the relevant
information from the concerned custodian of the records, the Respondent
replied in negative.
At the instance of the Commission whether they have transferred the instant
hearing notice of the Commission to the concerned custodian of the record,
the Respondent replied in negative.

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
neither transferred the instant RTI Application under Section 6 (3) of the
RTI Act to the concerned custodian of the records, nor, invoked the
provision of Section 5 (4) of the RTI Act and tried to obtain the relevant
information from the concerned custodian of the records. Hence the
Commission expresses displeasure at the conduct of the Respondent and
accordingly admonishes them for treating the instant matter in a cavalier
manner and cautions the concerned CPIO to remain careful in future while
dealing with the matters related to the RTI Act.
The Commission in order to facilitate the Appellant, deems it fit to direct the
present CPIO to make an effort to search their database/records in a prudent
manner to find/locate the relevant information as sought in the instant RTI
Application and provide the same to the Appellant by invoking the
Severability Clause under Section 10 of the RTI Act. If the relevant
information is not available in their office, the Commission deems it
expedient to direct the present CPIO to make an effort to obtain the relevant
information as sought in the RTI Application from the concerned custodian
of the record and provide a copy of the same to the Appellant, by invoking
the Severability Clause under Section 10 of the RTI Act, with a copy marked
to the Commission. The said directions should be complied within 21 days,
from the date of receipt of this order.
On the contrary, if the relevant information is still not found in the official
records of the Respondent, the Commission deems it fit to direct the
concerned CPIO to submit an appropriate affidavit, on non-judicial stamp
