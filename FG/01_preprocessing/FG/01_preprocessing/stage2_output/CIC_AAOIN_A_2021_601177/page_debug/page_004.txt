CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/601177
The CPIO vide online reply dated 29.09.2020, informed to the Appellant as
under:
“The applicant sought information against email dt. 10.08.2020
addressed to Minister of Civil Aviation. The said email has not
received in Dte. of Operations. The applicant may be asked to file
application to RED (Western Region) and RED (Southern Region).”
Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 16.10.2020. The
First Appellate Authority vide online reply dated 22.10.2020, informed as
under;
“Having gone through the Appeal and examination of the reply given
by CPIO, it is found that reply given by CPIO is in order. Thus the
Appeal dated 16.10.2020 stands disposed off.”
