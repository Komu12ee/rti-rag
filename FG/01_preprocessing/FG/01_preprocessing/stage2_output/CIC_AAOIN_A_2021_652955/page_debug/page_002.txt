Information sought:
The Appellant filed an online RTI Application dated 12.08.2021 seeking
information on the following three points:
“Please furnish the below requested details of the top 50 candidates in
final merit list prepared to the post of the Manager(Engg - Electrical)
recruitment under Advt No.2/2018.
1) Name/Roll number of candidate
2) Total marks secured by candidate (Written & Interview)
3) Please prepare a merit list containing top 50 candidates to the post
of the Manager(Engg - Electrical) recruitment under Advt
No.2/2018 with Name/Roll number of candidate and Total marks
secured by candidate (Written & Interview) and furnish the same.”
The CPIO vide online reply dated 02.09.2021, denied information to the
Appellant as under:
“Third Party information as per Section – 11 to the RTI Act, 2005.”
