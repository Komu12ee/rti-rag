8. (1) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, there shall be no
obligation to give any citizen,—
(d) information including commercial confidence, trade secrets or
intellectual property, the disclosure of which would harm the competitive
position of a third party, unless the competent authority is satisfied that
larger public interest warrants the disclosure of such information.
The sought for information if disclosed may disclose certain commercial
secrets and other details of agencies which took part in the said tendering process
and such inspection/disclosure can harm the competitive position of such
agencies/firms. The Commission’s considered view is therefore that if the sought
for information is disclosed to the appellant by way of grant of permission for
undertaking of inspection of documents submitted by the above said third party
firm, the third parties’ interest will be jeopardized which is evidently not in the
public interest.
With the above observation, the appeal is disposed of.
