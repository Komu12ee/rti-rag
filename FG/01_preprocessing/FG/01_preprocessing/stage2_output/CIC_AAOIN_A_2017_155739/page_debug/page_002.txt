Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Absent
Both the parties were found absent during the hearing at the CIC.
The respondent, CPIO Airport Authority of India was absent despite valid
and timely notice of the CIC for which a warning is issued to the concerned
respondent PIO. The concerned PIO should desist from committing such lapse in
future. He should either take prior permission of the Commission before absenting
himself from the hearing or he should send proper representative to the
Commission’s hearing after obtaining due permission from the Commission for
doing so and after briefing the authorised representative duly.
The appellant was not present to plead for this case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the appellant wanted to inspect the entire records in connection with the award of
license for Maxi Cab services at Vijayawada railway station. The Commission
could not be briefed about how many agencies had responded to the E-tender
notice issued by the Airports Authority of India, Vijaywada. However, whatsoever
may be the number of firms who had applied in connection with the e-tender
notice, the fact remains that the sought for information would have included
information about turnover of these competing firms, their income tax returns, the
firms’ sales tax records and other commercial details. In case the sought for
information is disclosed i.e. the inspection of documents submitted by these firms
which took part in the above said e-tender notice is agreed to, the appellant would
have access to host of information pertaining to these competing firms which are
vis-à-vis the applicant. This sought for document inspection is therefore not only
against the provision of 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act (right to third party privacy) but it
