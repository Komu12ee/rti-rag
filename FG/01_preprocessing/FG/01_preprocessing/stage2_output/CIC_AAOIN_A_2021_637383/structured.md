## Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:

The Appellant stated that the Respondent has not furnished the requisite information with respect to the instant RTI Application. Upon being queried

<!-- Page 4 -->
<!-- extraction_method: direct_text | confidence: 1.00 -->
CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/637383
by the Commission to specifically mention the query sought vide instant RTI
Application, the Appellant stated that list of candidates that has been
furnished by the Respondent is not complete as there were 166 candidates
and the list that has been furnished only contains name of 104 candidates.
The Respondent submitted that even though the contents of the instant RTI
Application is not clear and specific, whatever information is available on
record has been furnished to the Appellant.
Further, with respect to the contention raised by of the Appellant, the
Respondent submitted that recruitment is generally done in different regions
and for different apprenticeships and the Respondent are from northern
region and they conducted interview of 104 candidates only, therefore, the
said list has been furnished to the Appellant.
The Commission further queried the Respondent regarding information of
other candidates, to which the Respondent submitted that interviews of the
other candidates have been taken by other regions, so furnishing the same
will require collection and collation from about 20-22 airports which will
attract section 7(9) of the RTI Act.
Upon being asked by the Commission regarding the information with
respect to training centers of the selected candidates, the Respondent
submitted that information with respect to training centers of the selected
candidates are never disclosed on the public domain and the same is only
intimated to the concerned selected candidate, so the same cannot be
provided.
