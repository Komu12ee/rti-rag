During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that the Airports
Authority of India was not the custodian of the sought for information. He was
directed to file an affidavit in this regard.
The present respondent CPIO is directed to submit an affidavit that Airports
Authority of India was not the holder of the sought for information within one
month of the receipt of this order with a copy duly endorsed to the appellant within
the same time period.
A warning is also issued to the then respondent PIO for not transferring the
said RTI application on point nos. 2,3 and 4 to the custodian of the information i.e.
the CPIO holding the sought for information.
The concerned registry of this bench is directed to send a copy of this order
to the Chairman, Airports Authority of India for information and necessary
remedial action.
With the above observation/direction/warning, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
