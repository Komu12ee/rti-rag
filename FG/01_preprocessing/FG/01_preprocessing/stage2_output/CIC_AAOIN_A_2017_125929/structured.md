Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 10.06.2016 sought information on
four points as under;
1. Copy of notification by which lands of survey/block no. 14/1, 14/2, 25,
26,27,28 Sim of village Sardarnagar and survey/block no. 87/2 and 87/3
of Mouje Hansol Village had been acquired by the Airport Authority of
India.
2. To whom the amount of compensation was paid.
3. Cheque numbers through which the said compensation amount was paid
to the land owners.

4. Date of passing of award order.
The CPIO replied on 13.07.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the
CPIO’s reply and filed first appeal on 25.07.2016. The First Appellate Authority
(FAA) vide order dated 14.09.2016 disposed of the first appeal. Aggrieved with the
non-supply of the desired information from the respondent authority, the appellant
filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the RTI Act before the
Central Information Commission on 28.01.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri Manoj Gangwal,
Airport Director cum APIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letter dated 13.07.2016 and the First Appellate
Authority (FAA)’s order dated 14.09.2016. The replies furnished to the appellant
are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
proper reply was not provided to the appellant on point nos. 2,3 and 4 of the said
RTI application. A more comprehensive reply should have been provided to the
appellant as all the sought for information is eminently disclosable under the
relevant provisions of the RTI Act in the form of certified true copies of the
documents sought e.g. note sheets, letters, correspondences, e-mails etc.
The reply on point no. 1 of the said RTI application was found by the
Commission to be just and proper.

During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that the Airports
Authority of India was not the custodian of the sought for information. He was
directed to file an affidavit in this regard.
The present respondent CPIO is directed to submit an affidavit that Airports
Authority of India was not the holder of the sought for information within one
month of the receipt of this order with a copy duly endorsed to the appellant within
the same time period.
A warning is also issued to the then respondent PIO for not transferring the
said RTI application on point nos. 2,3 and 4 to the custodian of the information i.e.
the CPIO holding the sought for information.
The concerned registry of this bench is directed to send a copy of this order
to the Chairman, Airports Authority of India for information and necessary
remedial action.
With the above observation/direction/warning, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
