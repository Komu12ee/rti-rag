First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 27.06.2017. The replies furnished

The advocate for the appellant submitted that he was not satisfied with the
reply received from the respondent.
During the hearing, the representative of the respondent PIO submitted that
although original records as sought through the above mentioned RTI application
are available with the DIAL, they have information on some points only.
On perusal of the relevant case records, it was observed that proper reply
was not provided to the appellant. On point no 1, copy of the necessary permission
sought from AERA for utilizing the User Development Funds for the purpose of
construction of ATC is to be provided as the respondent had submitted that
information on this point was available with them. The sought for information on
point nos. 2 and 7 of the said RTI application is not covered u/s 2(f) of the RTI
Act. On point nos. 3-6 of the above stated RTI application, the respondent
submitted that records as sought by the appellant are not available with them and
are with DIAL. The respondent is directed to go through the relevant records again
and in case the information as sought by the appellant is not available after
thorough search, the respondent is directed to submit an affidavit in this regard.
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case, the respondent CPIO is directed to provide revised reply on
point no 1 as discussed during the hearing complete in all respects to the appellant
as available on record(legible copies), free of charge u/s 7(6) of the RTI Act within
15 days of the receipt of the order.
The respondent CPIO is further directed to send a report containing the copy
of the revised reply and the date of despatch of the same to the RTI appellant
within 07 days thereafter to the Commission for record.
