Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the information sought in
the instant RTI Application pertains to the office of DGCA and the then
CPIO rather than transferring the instant RTI Application under Section 6
(3) of the RTI Act to the concerned custodian of record has out rightly
denied the information to the Appellant under Section 8 (1) (j) of the RTI
Act which even does not pertain to their office and this kind of approach is
not within the letter and spirit of the RTI Act and he is admonished for the
same. The Commission advises the Respondent to acquaint themselves well
with the provisions of the RTI Act in order to avoid such blatant and tedious
mistakes. Hence, the Commission deems it fit to direct the Respondent to
provide a revised reply to the Appellant after obtaining it from the concerned
custodian of information, with a copy marked to the Commission within 21
days from the date of receipt of this order and assistance from concerned
office/department for compliance with the above directions, the same shall
be sought under Section 5(4) of RTI Act.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
