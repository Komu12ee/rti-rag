The Commission in order to facilitate the Appellant, deems it fit to direct the
present CPIO to make an effort to search their database/records in a prudent
manner to find/locate the relevant information as sought in the instant RTI
Application and provide the same to the Appellant, by invoking the
Severability Clause under Section 10 of the RTI Act. If the relevant
information is not available in their office, the Commission deems it
expedient to direct the present CPIO to make an effort to obtain the relevant
information as sought in the RTI Application from the concerned custodian
of the record and provide a copy of the same to the Appellant, by invoking
the Severability Clause under Section 10 of the RTI Act, with a copy marked
to the Commission. The said directions should be complied within 21 days,
from the date of receipt of this order.
On the contrary, if the relevant information is still not found in the official
records of the Respondent, the Commission deems it fit to direct the
concerned CPIO to submit an appropriate affidavit, on non-judicial stamp
paper deposing the factum of non-availability of the relevant record as
sought in the RTI Application. The said affidavit should be submitted to the
Commission with its copy duly endorsed to the Appellant, within 30 days
from the date of receipt of this order.
