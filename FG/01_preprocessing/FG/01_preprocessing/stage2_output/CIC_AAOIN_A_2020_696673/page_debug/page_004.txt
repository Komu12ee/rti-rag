CIC/AAOIN/A/2020/696673
The CPIO vide online reply dated 29.09.2020, informed to the Appellant as
under:
“The applicant sought information against email dt. 10.08.2020
addressed to the Minister of Civil Aviation. The said email has not
received in Dte. of Operations. The applicant may be asked to file
application to RED (Western Region) and RED (Southern Region).”
Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 09.10.2020. The
First Appellate Authority vide online reply dated 22.10.2020, informed as
under:
“Having gone through the Appeal and examination of the reply given by
CPIO, it is found that reply given by CPIO is in order. Thus the Appeal dated
09.10.2020 stands disposed off.”
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that she has not received the relevant information qua
the instant RTI Application from the Respondent.
