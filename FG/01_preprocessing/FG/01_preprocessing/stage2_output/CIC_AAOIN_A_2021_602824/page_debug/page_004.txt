CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/602824
Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 28.10.2020. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 19.11.2020, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for
and take appropriate legal action against the Respondent.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that she has not received the relevant information qua
the instant RTI Application from the Respondent.
The Respondent submitted that the information sought by the Appellant is
not specific and the same is vague and accordingly they have provided a
reply to this effect to the Appellant.
