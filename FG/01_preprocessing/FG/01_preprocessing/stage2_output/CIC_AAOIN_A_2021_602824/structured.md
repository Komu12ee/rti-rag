Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the reply provided by the
Respondent qua the instant RTI Application is inadequate and evasive as the
Appellant has precisely elaborated the points of information sought, hence
the Commission expresses displeasure at the conduct of the Respondent and
accordingly admonishes him for treating the instant matter in a cavalier
manner and further cautions the concerned CPIO to remain careful in future
while dealing with the matters related to the RTI Act.
Accordingly, the Commission directs the present CPIO to provide revised
reply qua the RTI Application to the Appellant, by invoking the Severability
