Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that the information as sought by him has been
arbitrarily denied under section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. The Appellant further
added that he is only seeking for marks allotted and not the details of the
DPC of other candidates and the same cannot be regarded as third party
personal information.

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent furnished
an adequate reply to the Appellant with respect to the instant RTI
Application within a stipulated time frame. The Commission further
observes that the Respondent has rightly denied the information under
section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. In this regard, the Commission relies on the
judgment passed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India in the case of
CPIO, Supreme Court of India v. Subhash Chandra Agrawal Civil
Appeal No. 10044 of 2010 wherein it has been held as under:
“59…in our opinion, would indicate that personal records,
including name, address, physical, mental and psychological
status, marks obtained, grades and answer sheets, are all treated
as personal information. Similarly, professional records,
including qualification, performance, evaluation reports, ACRs,
disciplinary proceedings, etc. are all personal information.
Medical records, treatment, choice of medicine, list of hospitals
and doctors visited, findings recorded, including that of the
family members, information relating to assets, liabilities,
income tax returns, details of investments, lending and
borrowing, etc. are personal information. Such personal
information is entitled to protection from unwarranted invasion
of privacy and conditional access is available when stipulation
