CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/641742
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that the information as sought by him has been
arbitrarily denied under section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. The Appellant further
added that he is only seeking for marks allotted and not the details of the
DPC of other candidates and the same cannot be regarded as third party
personal information.
