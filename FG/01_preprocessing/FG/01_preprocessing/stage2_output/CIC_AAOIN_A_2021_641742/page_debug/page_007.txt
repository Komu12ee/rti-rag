Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent furnished
an adequate reply to the Appellant with respect to the instant RTI
Application within a stipulated time frame. The Commission further
observes that the Respondent has rightly denied the information under
section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. In this regard, the Commission relies on the
judgment passed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India in the case of
CPIO, Supreme Court of India v. Subhash Chandra Agrawal Civil
Appeal No. 10044 of 2010 wherein it has been held as under:

including name, address, physical, mental and psychological
status, marks obtained, grades and answer sheets, are all treated
as personal information. Similarly, professional records,
including qualification, performance, evaluation reports, ACRs,
disciplinary proceedings, etc. are all personal information.
