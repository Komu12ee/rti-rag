Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that complete information with respect to the instant
RTI Application has not been furnished. He further stated that he is not
satisfied with the information furnished specifically with respect to point 3,

4 & 5. During the hearing, the Commission found out that the Appellant had
been trying to mislead the court by letting another person named Shri.
Surender Bhanot answer to all the above queries on behalf of the Appellant
without Commission’s prior approval & knowledge. The Commission takes
note of this erroneous act of the representative of the Appellant and
admonished him for the same.
The Respondent submitted that adequate information as available on record
has been furnished to the Appellant and further, First Appellate Authority
vide order dated 01.12.2020 provided a categorical reply with respect to the
instant RTI Application. The Commission queried the Appellant to ascertain
whether he has received the information furnished by the First Appellate
Authority, to which the Appellant stated that he has received the information
but there has been an inordinate delay in furnishing the same.
The Commission remarked that the Appellant is unnecessarily changing
stances, as he first stated that complete information has not been furnished
and then affirmed to have received the requisite information but contested on
delay of the same.
Upon being queried by the Respondent regarding the delay in reply, to
which Smt. Avneet Kaur, CPIO submitted that reply was finalized on
01.12.2020 but it was dispatched on 16.12.2020 because the person
responsible for dispatching was suffering from COVID at that time.
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that there was delay on the part

of the Appellant in filing the instant Second Appeal as well as on the part of
the Respondent in furnishing reply to the RTI application. Keeping in mind
the constraints and unfortunate circumstances caused due to COVID
Pandemic at that point of time, the Commission deems it fit to condone the
delays of both the parties.
The Commission further observes that the Respondent has adequately
attended the instant RTI Application. Further the First Appellate Authority
has furnished the complete information to the Appellant who further
ascertained the same. However, the Appellant is contesting on the veracity
of the matter, which is beyond the adjudicatory powers of the Commission.
Hence, the Commission finds no further scope of intervention in the instant
matter.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
