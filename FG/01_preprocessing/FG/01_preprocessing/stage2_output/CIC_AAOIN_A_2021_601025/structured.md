Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for
and take appropriate legal action against the Respondent.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that with respect to point no. 1 of the RTI Application,
the Respondent has not provided the desired information.
The Respondent submitted that they are ready to provide copy of that part of
minutes of meeting held on 09.11.2017 pertaining to reconstitution of the
averred sub-committee.
A written submission has been received by the Commission from Shri.
Parimesh Manocha, Manager (Corporate Affairs & Company Secretary) &
Central Public Information Officer, Airports Authority of India vide letter
dated 22.04.2022, wherein the commission has been apprised as under:

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that with respect to point no. 1
of the RTI Application, the Respondent has agreed to provide that part of
minutes of meeting held on 09.11.2017, pertaining to reconstitution of the
sub-committee, hence the Commission deems it fit to direct the Respondent
to provide the same to the Appellant, with a copy marked to the
Commission, within 10 days from the date of receipt of this order.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
