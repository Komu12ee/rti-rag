Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that the information as sought by him has been denied
arbitrarily as the information sought pertained to the Appellant himself.
The Respondent submitted that a disciplinary enquiry had been initiated
against the Appellant for unauthorized absence for more than 2 years. At that
point of time, enquiry was ongoing therefore the desired information had
been denied to the Appellant. The Respondent further submitted that in
compliance of the First Appellate Authority’s Order as well as to redress
Appellant’s grievance the Respondent has provided an opportunity to the
Appellant to inspect relevant records on any date as per his convenience and
the Respondent also agreed to Appellant’s request for videography of
records during inspection, but the Appellant still never visited the office.
The Commission sought Appellant’s willingness to avail the opportunity of
inspection of records to which the Appellant out rightly denied. Therefore,
the Commission queried the Respondent whether the requisite information
can be provided to the Appellant by invoking severability clause of the RTI
Act, to which Respondent affirmatively agreed and the Appellant also
agreed to pay for all the documents.

Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
appropriately attended the instant RTI Application within a stipulated frame.
The Commission further observes that the Respondent has also provided an
opportunity to the Appellant to inspect the relevant records which the
Appellant did not avail. However, during the course of hearing the
Respondent has agreed to provide the desired information to the Appellant
and the Appellant has also agreed to pay for the same.

In the light of the submission made during the course of hearing, the
Commission deems it expedient to direct the Respondent to provide the
relevant information qua the instant RTI Application by invoking the
provision Severability Clause under Section 10 of the RTI Act, to the
Appellant, with a copy marked to the Commission, within 30 days from the
date of receipt of this order.
The present CPIO is further directed that while complying with the
directions of the Commission, all the personal information/identifying
particulars of any third parties should be adequately redacted/blackened out.
The Commission further directs the present CPIO that no information shall
be disclosed to the Appellant which is exempted from disclosure under the
provisions of Section 8 and 9 of the RTI Act.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
