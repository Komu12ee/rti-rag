CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/603782
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
appropriately attended the instant RTI Application within a stipulated frame.
The Commission further observes that the Respondent has also provided an
opportunity to the Appellant to inspect the relevant records which the
Appellant did not avail. However, during the course of hearing the
Respondent has agreed to provide the desired information to the Appellant
and the Appellant has also agreed to pay for the same.
