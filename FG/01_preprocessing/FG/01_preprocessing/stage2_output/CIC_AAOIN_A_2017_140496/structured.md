Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 23.11.2016 sought information on
eight points as under:
1. Details of travel including bookings, cancellations and transactions done
by the husband of the appellant as the same are needed in respect of a court case
between the appellant and her husband.
2. Details of domestic air travels undertaken from 2007 till the date of filing
of the RTI application by her husband along with details of co-passenger(s) on
each such travel.
3. Details of international air travels undertaken by the husband of the
appellant from 2007 till the date of filing of the RTI application along with details
of co-passenger(s) on each such travel.
