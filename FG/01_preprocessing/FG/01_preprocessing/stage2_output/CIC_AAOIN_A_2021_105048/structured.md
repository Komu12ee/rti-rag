Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he requested hard copy of the relevant documents
from PIO but the same is refused stating that it is a voluminous document.
The Respondent submitted that adequate information has been provided to
the Appellant and further when the Appellant raised dissatisfaction with the
same, an opportunity of inspection was provided to the Appellant because
desired documents were voluminous in nature. The Appellant then availed
the inspection on 11.08.2020.

The Respondent further submitted that the Appellant again requested for
hard copies even after availing the inspection, for which the Appellant was
asked to be specific vide email dated 25.08.2020 but there was no response
from the Appellant. Again an email dated 04.09.2020 was sent to the
Appellant, seeking specific details of documents for which hard copies are
sought.
Upon being asked by the Commission to be specific the Appellant stated that
he requires hard copies of running bills, payments details and work progress
as respectively sought in point 7, 9 and 10 of the instant RTI Application.
The Commission further queried the Appellant whether he is willing to pay
the documentation fees for the said documents and Appellant affirmatively
agreed for the same.
The Respondent while concurring with the above arrangement submitted
they will have to count the pages so that exact documentation fee can be
intimated to the appellant.
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though the Respondent
has adequately dealt with the instant RTI Application, the Appellant is
desirous of hard copies of the documents as sought in point 7, 9 and 10 of
the RTI Application. In the light of the submissions, the Commission deems
it fit to direct the Respondent to ascertain the no. of pages and the respective
documentation fee and intimate the same to the Appellant within a weeks’
time from the date of receipt of this order. The Respondent is further
