During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letters dated 26.04.2017, 05.06.2017 and the First
Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 31.07.2017. The replies furnished to the
appellant are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The reply dated 31.07.2017 was not available in the case record,
accordingly, the respondent PIO was asked to read the same over the VC facility.
He was intimated to send a copy of the same to the Commission through e-mail for
record.
The appellant was not present to plead for this case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the reply provided is just, proper and comprehensive. It was also noted that the
sought for information is covered u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act being information
related to third parties and hence non disclosable but after the order of first
appellate authority, the respondent had provided all the sought for information as a
measure towards ushering in greater transparency in the respondent organization.
