Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he has received the reply furnished by the
Respondent but he is not satisfied with the same.
The Commission queried the representative of the Appellant to specify and
elaborately explain as to what sort of information is being sought vide point
2, 3 and 4 of the instant RTI Application but the representative of the
Appellant has failed to do the same.
The Respondent submitted that disclosable information with respect to the
instant RTI Application has already been furnished to the Appellant. The
Respondent further submitted that the Appellant vide point 1 of the RTI
Application seeking for marks obtained by all the selected candidates in their
physical endurance test and since the said information falls under the
category of exempted information as per section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act and
as such it could not be furnished and the same has been intimated to the
Appellant. He further added that since the information sought vide point 2, 3
& 4 of the RTI Application is not clear, the same could not be provided.
The Commission further queried the Respondent whether they have
intimated the marks scored by the Appellant’s Son to the Appellant, to
which the Respondent submitted that the same has been furnished to the
Appellant. He further added that the Appellant is a habitual RTI applicant
