Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 22.11.2016 sought copies of the
reasons recorded about charging of goods in duty free shops in terms of US Dollars
by the concessionaire from Indian citizens while they purchase liquor and other
goods from duty free shops situated at IGI, Airport Terminal – III, New Delhi. The
CPIO’s reply is not on record. Due to non-receipt of any reply from the CPIO, the
appellant filed first appeal on 02.01.2017. The first appellant Authority (FAA)
disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 11.01.2017. Aggrieved with the
non-supply of the desired information from the respondent authority, the appellant
filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the RTI Act before the
Central Information Commission on 27.03.2017.

Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Absent
The respondent was absent despite valid and timely notice of the CIC for
which warning is issued to the concerned respondent PIO. The concerned PIO
should desist from committing such lapse in future. He should either take prior
permission of the Commission before absenting himself from the hearing or he
should send proper representative to the Commission’s hearing after obtaining due
permission from the Commission for doing so and after briefing the authorized
representative duly.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the then CPIO did not provide any information. Aggrieved with the non-supply of
the desired information from the respondent authority, the appellant filed first
appeal on 02.01.2017. The first appellate authority (FAA) had disposed of the
appeal vide its order dated 11.01.2017 in which the FAA clearly ordered the then
GM(admn.)/CPIO to provide the requisite information to the appellant within 07
days of the receipt of his order by the then Govt (admn) and PIO. However, on
perusal of the case papers, the Commission is unable to find any compliance of the
then FAA’s order. So, the Commission under these circumstances has no option
but to issue show-cause notice to the then CPIO and GM(Admn), AAI.
In view of the above, a Show Cause notice is issued to the then CPIO,
Airports Authority of India u/s 20 of the RTI Act to explain the following:-
Why no reply was provided to the appellant even after the receipt of the
order of the First Appellate Authority ordering him to provide necessary
information to the appellant.

The explanation(s) to the above stated Show Cause notice is to be submitted
to the Commission by the respondent CPIO within 15 days of the receipt of this
order. The present CPIO is also to submit a report to the Commission indicating
the name, address, mobile no., present place of posting and designation of the
CPIO working at the relevant post at the relevant period. On receipt of the
explanation to the said Show Cause notice, further action as deemed appropriate
will be taken.
The respondent CPIO should note that in the event of non-submission of the
explanation within the time stipulated above, the Commission has the liberty to
take the required decision ex-parte against the respondent CPIO.
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case, the present respondent CPIO is directed to provide point wise
reply complete in all respects to the appellant as available on record in the form of
certified true copies of the documents sought e.g. note sheets, letters,
correspondences, e-mails etc.(legible copies), free of charge u/s 7(6) of the RTI
Act within 15 days of the receipt of the order. For this purpose, the concerned
CPIO/PIO, can take assistance of any other office/department u/s 5(4) of the RTI
Act.
The respondent CPIO is further directed to send a report containing the copy
of the revised reply and the date of despatch of the same to the RTI appellant
within 07 days thereafter to the Commission for record.
With the above Show cause/direction/warning, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.

A Show Cause notice was issued to the then CPIO, Airports Authority of
India u/s 20 of the RTI Act to explain the following:-
Why no reply was provided to the appellant even after the receipt of the
order of the First Appellate Authority ordering him to provide necessary
information to the appellant.
Explanation from Sh. Manoj Gangal, Airport Director & PIO-
He made the following submission :
(i) The applicant’s application was received by DGM (ES)/CNO RTI
vide his letter No. AAI/RTI/Misc/2016/1683 dated 06.12.2016 and he
forwarded the same to GM(JVC) & CPIO who was the custodian of
information. Hence, RTI application was never forwarded to him i.e.
then GM(Admn)/CPIO and presently Airport Director, Ahmedabad.
(ii) As far as the date of hearing which was scheduled for 02.07.2018 is
concerned, the same had also never been served to him as a result of
which he could not appear.
The GM(JVC) & CPIO representative submitted that they had transferred
the RTI application u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act to G.M (Commercial) vide letter dated
14.12.2016.
There was no correspondence on record from the GM(Commercial) office. It
appears that the GM (Commercial) posted on 14.12.2016 was responsible for not
replying to the RTI application. However, the fact relevant as to present RTI
application is the GM (Admn)/PIO was directed to provide the information in the
First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 11.01.2017 and Sh. Manoj Gangal,
Airport Director was the then GM (Admn)/PIO.
Decision:
The then GM (Admn)/PIO did not explain the reason for not complying with
the First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order for which he is issued a strict warning.

Although the said RTI application was not received by him, as soon as the First
Appellate Authority’s order was received by him, he was duty bound to provide a
comprehensive reply to the appellant.
The then respondent CPIO, GM (Admn) Ahri Manoj Gangal and the then
GM (Commercial) posted on 14.12.2016 are issued warning that full, final and
comprehensive reply to the said RTI application should have been provided
within the time period as stipulated under the RTI Act and they should ensure that
in future in every case reply to each and every RTI application is invariably
provided within 30 days of receipt of the said application.
The respondent CPIOs should note that in future if the same mistake is
noticed by the Commission, more stringent action can be taken against them by the
Commission.
With the above warning, the showcause proceeding is treated as closed.
