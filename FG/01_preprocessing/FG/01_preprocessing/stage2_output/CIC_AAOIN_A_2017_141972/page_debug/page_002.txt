The CPIO replied on 22.03.2017. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 22.03.2017. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 10.04.2017
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 20.05.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri R.S. Jagpal,
Assistant General Manager cum PIO,
Air Port Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letter dated 22.03.2017 and the First Appellate
Authority (FAA)’s order dated 10.04.2017. The replies furnished to the appellant
are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant contested replies on point nos. 2-5 of the above stated RTI
application.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the sought for information on point nos. 2- 4 of the above stated RTI application is
a third party information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act and the same cannot
be provided to the appellant unless due procedure as envisaged under section 11(1)
of the RTI Act is applied in the present case.
The sought for information on point no. 5 is a third party information
exempted u/s 8(1)(j) and 8(1)(g) of the RTI Act. Hence, the same cannot be
provided to the appellant.
