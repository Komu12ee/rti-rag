that the CPIO had made it a habit of not attending the hearing at the CIC and
authorizing same official to represent her every time during the hearing. The only
reason in support of her continued non attendance at CIC as mentioned in the letter
authorizing some junior official to represent her at the CIC’s hearing was that she
was pre-occupied with urgent court matter. The Commission came to the
conclusion that the said CPIO had made it a regular habit of absenting herself from
the CIC’s hearing on flimsy grounds without seeking prior permission of the
Commission for absenting herself from the hearing, This practice is not good for
the dignity and proper conduct of business for the Commission. The Commission
is also, by this way, deprived of the assistance of a senior official in their decision
making process.
In view of the above, a show cause notice is issued to the then respondent
CPIO, Smt. Nivedita Dubey, General Manager, HR u/s 20 of the RTI Act to
explain the following:-
For absenting herself without any valid reason & why prior permission of
the Commission was not taken for absenting herself from the Commission’s
hearing.
The explanation(s) to the above stated Show Cause notice is/are to be
submitted to the Commission by the respondent CPIO/PIO within 15 days of the
receipt of this order. The present CPIO is also to submit a report to the
Commission indicating the name, address, mobile no., present place of posting and
designation of the CPIO working at the relevant post at the relevant period. The
present respondent CPIO is to serve a copy of this order to the then respondent
CPIO under intimation to the Commission. On receipt of the explanation to the
said Show Cause notice, further action as deemed appropriate will be taken.
The respondent CPIO should note that in the event of non-submission of the
explanation within the time stipulated above, the Commission has the liberty to
take the required decision ex-parte against the respondent CPIO/PIO.
