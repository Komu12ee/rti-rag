Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 22.09.2016 sought information on
three points as under:
1. Details for each post of Assistant Manager, Manager & Senior Manager at
the CNS department of AAI for the month of July, starting from year 2002 to 2012,
sanctioned strength for each post, manpower available, total vacancies, number of
promotional vacancies & number of DR vacancies.
2. Total number of vacancies upgraded to higher posts and total no. of posts
surrendered in the lower cadre, post wise for such upgradation(matching savings).

3. Copies of relevant rules under which promotion had been effected
retrospectively for an officer.
The CPIO replied on 24.10.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 04.12.2016. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 20.12.2016.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 02.05.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Smt. R. Sumani,
Joint General Manager, representative of CPIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite replies vide their letters dated 24.10.2016 and 26.06.16 and the First
Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 20.12.2016. The replies furnished to the
appellant are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The reply dated 26.06.2016 was not available in the case record, the
respondent PIO was accordingly asked to read the same over the VC facility. He
was intimated to send copies of the same to the Commission through e-mail for
record.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
on 30.07.2018 the respondent CPIO, Smt. Nivedita Dubey, General Manager was
not present at the CIC for attending hearing before the same bench in some case
and on 02.08.2016 also she did not attend the CIC’s hearing in person. It was noted

that the CPIO had made it a habit of not attending the hearing at the CIC and
authorizing same official to represent her every time during the hearing. The only
reason in support of her continued non attendance at CIC as mentioned in the letter
authorizing some junior official to represent her at the CIC’s hearing was that she
was pre-occupied with urgent court matter. The Commission came to the
conclusion that the said CPIO had made it a regular habit of absenting herself from
the CIC’s hearing on flimsy grounds without seeking prior permission of the
Commission for absenting herself from the hearing, This practice is not good for
the dignity and proper conduct of business for the Commission. The Commission
is also, by this way, deprived of the assistance of a senior official in their decision
making process.
In view of the above, a show cause notice is issued to the then respondent
CPIO, Smt. Nivedita Dubey, General Manager, HR u/s 20 of the RTI Act to
explain the following:-
For absenting herself without any valid reason & why prior permission of
the Commission was not taken for absenting herself from the Commission’s
hearing.
The explanation(s) to the above stated Show Cause notice is/are to be
submitted to the Commission by the respondent CPIO/PIO within 15 days of the
receipt of this order. The present CPIO is also to submit a report to the
Commission indicating the name, address, mobile no., present place of posting and
designation of the CPIO working at the relevant post at the relevant period. The
present respondent CPIO is to serve a copy of this order to the then respondent
CPIO under intimation to the Commission. On receipt of the explanation to the
said Show Cause notice, further action as deemed appropriate will be taken.
The respondent CPIO should note that in the event of non-submission of the
explanation within the time stipulated above, the Commission has the liberty to
take the required decision ex-parte against the respondent CPIO/PIO.

On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
proper reply was not provided to the appellant as the respondent stated in his reply
that no recorded information is available which is deplorable for which the then
respondent PIO, Shri H.S. Rawat, Assistant General Manager, HR needs to be
issued a warning.
The then respondent PIO, Shri H.S. Rawat, Assistant General Manager, HR
is issued warning that full, final and comprehensive reply to an RTI application
should have been provided within the time period as stipulated under the RTI Act
and he should ensure that in future in every case reply to an RTI application is
invariably provided within 30 days of receipt of the said application.
The respondent CPIO should note that in future if the same mistake is
noticed by the Commission, more stringent action can be taken against him by the
Commission.
The present respondent CPIO is directed to serve a copy of this warning to
the then CPIO and a copy of the service of the above said warning be submitted to
the Commission within 10 days of the receipt of the order.
The Commission also observed that proper reply was not provided to the
appellant on all the points of the RTI application. On point nos. 1 & 2 of the stated
RTI application, complete information in whatever shape available on record with
the respondent authority should be provided to the appellant. However, if no such
records are available, the respondent is directed to file an affidavit in this regard.
On point no. 3 of the above stated RTI application, details of the relevant hyperlink
of the concerned website should be provided to the appellant.
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case in respect of some points contained in the stated RTI
application, the respondent CPIO is directed to provide revised point wise reply as
discussed during the hearing complete in all respects to the appellant as available
on record in the form of certified true copies of the documents sought e.g. note
