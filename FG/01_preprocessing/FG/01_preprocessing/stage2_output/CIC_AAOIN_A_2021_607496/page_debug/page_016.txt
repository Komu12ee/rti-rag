Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though a timely reply has
been provided by the Respondent qua the RTI Application, the reply
provided by the Respondent on point no. 1 of the RTI Application vide letter
dated 07.02.2021 and 11.03.2021, are inadequate and inappropriate. Hence,
the Commission directs the present CPIO to provide revised and specific
information on point no. 1 of the RTI Application to the Appellant, with a
copy marked to the Commission, within 10 days from the date of receipt of
this order.
In the event Respondent is required to seek further assistance from any other
office/officer for compliance with the above directions, the same shall be
sought under Section 5(4) of RTI Act.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
