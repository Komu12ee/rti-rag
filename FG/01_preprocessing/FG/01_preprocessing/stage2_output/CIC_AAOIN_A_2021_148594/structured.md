Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 22.09.2021. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 05.10.2021, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is not satisfied with the reply furnished by the
Respondent with respect to the instant RTI Application as they have
furnished incomplete and misleading reply.

The Respondent submitted that a categorical reply with the respect to the
instant RTI Application has been furnished to the Appellant on the basis of
the information as available on record.
The Appellant interjected that the Respondent has given the list of
participants as sought vide point 1 of the RTI Application but did not inform
them individually for the hearing. He further added that information for
point 2 has not been provided and as regards point 3 of the RTI Application
neither the said order copy has been furnished nor the follow up has been
done.
The Respondent submitted that list of persons who participated in the
pension Adalat has been received from the Ministry and it has been
furnished to the Appellant as it is. Further, no such affidavit is submitted by
the Airports Authority of India, as the participant directly plead their matter
before the Adalat and accordingly the same been informed to the Appellant.
The Respondent further added that as regards the information with respect to
point 3 of the RTI Application, there is no such order copy because no
notesheet has been maintained. Only the comments of the pension Adalat
has been received from the Ministry vide email dated 27.08.2019 and as a
follow action a letter dated 28.08.2019 has been issued by them. She further
added that comments of the Ministry vide email date 27.08.2019 as well the
copy of letter dated 28.09.2019 has been furnished to the Appellant.
The Appellant further interjected that he is still not satisfied with the
explanation given by the Respondent as no order copy has been furnished, to
which the Respondent submitted that the complete information as available
on their record has been furnished.
