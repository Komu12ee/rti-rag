On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the sought for information is a third party information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) and
8(1)(e) of the RTI Act. This information should not have been provided to the
appellant without following due procedure as envisaged u/s 11(1) of the RTI Act.
However, the first appellate authority in its order dated 20.01.2017 had directed the
CPIO to provide all the sought for information to the appellant concerned. This
incident shows serious lack of understanding of the RTI Act on the part of the first
appellate authority.
An advisory is issued u/s 25(5) of the RTI Act to the Chairman, Airports
Authority of India to organise basic training programmes on RTI Act/matters to
the officers (respondent CPIO/PIO and FAA)/ and staff dealing with RTI matters
working in the Airports Authority of India without any further delay. The
Commission desires that this advice should be viewed seriously and an action
taken report be submitted to the Commission within one month of the receipt of the
order.
With the above observation/advisory, the appeals are disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
