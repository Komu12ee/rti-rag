Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 04.06.2021. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 17.06.2021, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that no information with respect to the instant RTI
Application has been furnished by the Respondent.
The Respondent submitted that contents of the instant RTI Application are
hypothetical in nature and in query form which fall beyond the purview of
section 2(f) of the RTI Act therefore no information can be provided to the
Appellant.

The Commission queried the Respondent as to why initial reply was
furnished after filing of first appeal, to which the Respondent submitted that
Appellant filed a case before Hon’ble High Court of Allahabad which was
not concluded, therefore the initial reply got delayed.
A written submission has been received by the Commission from Shri. G.L.
Verma, CPIO & General Manager (HR), Airports Authority of India vide
letter dated 24.08.2022, and the same has been marked to the Appellant
wherein the Commission has been apprised as under:
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that appropriate reply has been
provided by the Respondent. The Commission is of the considered opinion
that the information sought in the instant RTI Application is merely
clarificatory in nature, therefore the same is beyond the purview of Section
2(f) of the RTI Act.

The Commission relies on the judgment of the Hon’ble Supreme Court of
India in the matter of CBSE Vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay, Civil Appeal No.
6454/2011, which reads as under:
“A public authority is also not required to furnish information
which require drawing of inferences and/or making of assumptions.
It is also not required to provide ‘advice' or ‘opinion' to an
applicant, nor required to obtain and furnish any ‘opinion' or
‘advice' to an applicant.”
In the light of the above decision, the Commission finds no further scope of
intervention in the instant matter.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
