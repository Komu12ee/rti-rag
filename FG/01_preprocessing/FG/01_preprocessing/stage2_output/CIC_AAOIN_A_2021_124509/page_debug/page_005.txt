The Commission queried the Respondent as to why initial reply was
furnished after filing of first appeal, to which the Respondent submitted that
Appellant filed a case before Hon’ble High Court of Allahabad which was
not concluded, therefore the initial reply got delayed.
A written submission has been received by the Commission from Shri. G.L.
Verma, CPIO & General Manager (HR), Airports Authority of India vide
letter dated 24.08.2022, and the same has been marked to the Appellant
wherein the Commission has been apprised as under:
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that appropriate reply has been
provided by the Respondent. The Commission is of the considered opinion
that the information sought in the instant RTI Application is merely
clarificatory in nature, therefore the same is beyond the purview of Section
2(f) of the RTI Act.
