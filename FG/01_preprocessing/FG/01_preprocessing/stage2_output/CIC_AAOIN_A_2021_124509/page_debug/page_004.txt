CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/124509
Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 04.06.2021. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 17.06.2021, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that no information with respect to the instant RTI
Application has been furnished by the Respondent.
The Respondent submitted that contents of the instant RTI Application are
hypothetical in nature and in query form which fall beyond the purview of
section 2(f) of the RTI Act therefore no information can be provided to the
Appellant.
