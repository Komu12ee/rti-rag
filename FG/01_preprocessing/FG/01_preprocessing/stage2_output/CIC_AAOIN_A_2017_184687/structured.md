Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 19.04.2016 sought information on
five points as under:
(1) Whether sale of liquor is permissible at International Airports on the eve
of National Holiday i.e. Mahatma Gandhi’s Birthday on 02 October and on the eve
of Independence and republic days.

(2) Copies of policy guidelines, to sell or not to sell, liquor at Airports on
the eve of national holidays.
(3) List of all International Airports that do not sell liquor on the eve of
National Holidays as stated above.
(4) Complete list of the International Airports which are solely operated by
Airport Authority of India and not by concessionaire.
(5) List of those International Airports which are solely operated by Airport
Authority of India in which liquor is not sold on the eve of National Holidays.
The CPIO’s reply is not on record. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 28.05.2016. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 12.07.2016.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed a second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of
the RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 27.10.2016.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.

Appellant : Representative of the appellant
Shri Rajkumar
Respondent : Absent
During the hearing, the representative of the appellant submitted that no
reply whatsoever was provided to him so far.
It is very unfortunate that apart from transfer under Section 6(3) of the RTI
Act by the General Manager to different airport authorities, no proper reply was
provided to the appellant. Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired
information from the respondent authority, the appellant filed first appeal on

28.05.2016. The first appellate authority vide his order dated 12.07.2016 severely
criticized the CPIO concerned for non-provisioning of the relevant information to
the appellant which is as follows:-
“ In view of the above, I am of the considered opinion that GM(Admn)/PIO
should devote equal time to RTI issues so that appeals are reduced to bare
minimum thus the precious time of superior is saved. I therefore direct the
GM(Admn)/PIO to collect and compile the information and provide to the
same to the applicant within 15 days.”
Apart from this severe stricture, he also ordered GM(Admn)/PIO to collect
and provide the sought for information to the appellant with 15 days of the receipt
of such order of the FAA.
The above facts are deeply distressing. An advisory u/s 25(5) of the RTI
Act is issued to the Chairman cum CMD , AAI to institute an enquiry against the
then GM(Admn)/PIO to enquire into the circumstances in which even a senior
officer such as GM flagrantly violated the order of the first appellate authority
who was even more senior in rank.
In view of the above, a Show Cause notice is issued to the PIO, Shri Manoj
Kumar Gangal, General Manager(Admn.) Airport Authority of India u/s 20 of the
RTI Act to explain the following:-
(i) Why he was absent despite valid and timely notice on the day of
hearing i.e. on 15.05.2018; before the CIC and
(ii) Why no reply was provided to the appellant in all these years despite
the clear order of the first appellate authority ordering him to do so.
The explanation to the above stated Show Cause notice is to be submitted to
the Commission by the respondent CPIO/PIO within 15 days of the receipt of this

order. The present CPIO is also to submit a report to the Commission indicating

It was also observed that all the points are eminently disclosable. A more
comprehensive reply should have been provided to the appellant under the relevant
provisions of the RTI Act .
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case, the present respondent CPIO and GM(admn.) is directed to
provide point wise reply as the subject is considered to be in genuine pubic
interest, complete in all respects to the appellant as available on record in the form
of certified true copies of the documents sought e.g. note sheets, letters,
correspondences, e-mails etc.(legible copies), free of charge u/s 7(6) of the RTI
Act within 15 days of the receipt of the order. For this purpose, the concerned
CPIO/PIO, can take assistance of any other office/department u/s 5(4) of the RTI
Act.
The respondent CPIO is further directed to send a report containing the copy
of the revised reply and the date of dispatch of the same to the RTI appellant
within 07 days thereafter to the Commission for record.
With the above show cause/direction, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.

Adjunct Order : 26.09.2018
Respondent : Shri Manoj Gangal
Airport Director, Ahmedabad (The then CPIO)
Shri S.K Upadhaya, JGM (Admin) cum CPIO
Shri Manoj Gangal submitted that presently, he is working as Airport
Director, Ahmedabad w.e.f. 15.04.2017. In the capacity of General
Manager{Admn.) at Corporate Hqrs., New Delhi, he had received the said RTI
application dated 19.04.2016 from Sh. Aseem Takyar seeking information about
sale of liquor on national holidays at International Airports. He further submitted
that not being the custodian of the sought for information, he transferred the above
stated RTI application under the provision of Section 6(3) of the RTI Act, 2005 to
the custodians of required information i.e. the Executive Director(Commercial),
Regional Executive Director, NR/ER/SR/WR/NER and Airport Directors of
Kolkata and Chennai vide letter No. AAI/MedicaI/RTI/Tramsfer-16 dated
03.05.2016 for furnishing the sought for information to the complainant/applicant
directly.
On receipt of the order of the First Appellate Authority on 12.07.2016, it
became clear to Sh. Gangal that the requisite reply had not reached the RTI
applicant from the custodians of the sought for information and keeping this fact in
consideration, he once again wrote to the custodians of the information vide letter
no.AAI/MedicaI/RTI/Transfer-16/535 dated 10/12.08.2016 with request to furnish
the information directly to the applicant urgently.
Sh. Manoj Gangal also submitted that the CIC had called for personal
hearing on 15.05.2018 the notice for which unfortunately had not been received by
him, as he had been posted at Ahmedabad Airport as Airport Director since
15.04.2017 and was not at the Corporate hqrs at Delhi. He had intention neither for
withholding the sought for information nor for not appearing before the
Commission on the date fixed for hearing in the present case.

Shri S.K Upadhaya, JGM (Admin) cum CPIO submitted to the Commission
that the subject matter on which information was sought by the applicant does not
fall within the purview of the administration of the Corporate hqrs and as such the
then PIO had transferred the case to other PIO(s). The subject matter pertained to
the Commercial Directorate and Operation Directorate of the Airport Authority of
India. The order of the Commission for hearing was also forwarded to the CPIO of
Operations Directorate with a request to attend the CIC hearing.
Shri Manoj Gangal, the then General Manager (Administration), Corporate
hqrs stated that he could not comply with the directions of the then First Appellate
Authority as the sought for information was under his custody. Shri Gangal had
subsequently been transferred to Ahmedabad as Airport Director, Ahmedabad.
Decision:
Based on the submission, it is clear that Shri Gangal, the then
GM(Administration) had made sincere efforts to collect the sought for information
after the First Appellate Authority also vide his order no.
AAI/Medical/RTI/Transfer-16/535 dated 12.08.2016 ordered him to provide the
sought for information to the appellant. However, the concerned PIOs i.e The
Executive Director (Commercial), Airport Authority of India, The Regional
Executive Director, Airport Authority of India, The Airport Directors, Airports
Authority of India and PIOs of NR,ER,SR,NER & Kolkata Airport/Chennai
Airport did not provide the requisite reply. Hence, all the above stated PIOs are
issued strict warning and given last opportunity to provide the sought for
information to the appellant within 7 days from the receipt of this order to avoid
penal proceeding u/s 20 of the RTI Act.
Shri S.K Upadhaya, JGM (Admin) cum CPIO is ordered to serve copies of
this order to all the above mentioned PIOs for compliance within a period of 7 days
