Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Respondent has
adequately attended the instant RTI Application within a stipulated time
frame. The Commission further observes that the Respondent has traced the
requisite information from their records and the inspection of the same has
been facilitated to the Appellant during the course of hearing. Further, the
Respondent has furnished the copies of such documents to the Appellant as
earmarked by him during inspection and the Appellant has expressed his
satisfaction with the same. In view of the above, no further action lies in the
instant matter.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
