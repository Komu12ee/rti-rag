CIC/AAOIN/A/2021/643822
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for
and take appropriate legal action against the Respondent.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that information sought vide instant RTI Application
has not been furnished till date on the pretext that the same is not readily
available/ not traceable.
The Respondent submitted that though the information sought was not
readily available the opportunity to inspect the relevant records have been
offered to the Appellant and the same has been availed by the Appellant
wherein the Appellant requested for copies of 3 pages which were furnished
to him. The Appellant further confirmed the same vide letter dated
12.08.2021.
The Respondent further submitted that after strenuous efforts the requisite
information are now traced and the same can be inspected by the Appellant
during the course of hearing itself.

over by the Respondent and the Appellant concurred with the same. Further,

direction of the Commission wherein the Appellant has inspected the
