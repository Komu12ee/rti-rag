The appellant vide RTI application dated 10.03.2016 sought information
on the follow up of the Hon’ble High Court Mumbai’s order regarding
prohibiting any construction within a radius of 20 kms. from the periphery of
the Mumbai Airport boundary. The appellant also sought details of a
Committee which had been constituted by the Airport Authority of India
(Mumbai Regional Office) for examining in details the order of the Hon’ble
High Court, Mumbai. The RTI application sought information on the address
of the high power Committee which had rightly been constituted by the Airports
Authority of India, Mumbai whether the said Committee had started
proceedings and whether the final report in this regard has been prepared or not.
