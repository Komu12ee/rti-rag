Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 23.11.2016 sought information on
eight points as under:
1. Details of travel including bookings, cancellations and transactions done
by the husband of the appellant as the same are needed w.r.t a court case between
the appellant and her husband.
2. Details of domestic air travel from 2007 till the date of filing of the RTI
application by her husband along with the details of co-passengers with him during
each travel.

3. Details of international air travel from 2007 till the date of filing of the
RTI application by her husband along with the details of co-passengers with him
during each travel.
4. Other related information.
The CPIO replied on 28.11.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 23.11.2016. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 13.12.2016.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 28.03.2016.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri Sant Kumar,
Joint General Manager cum PIO,
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letter dated 28.11.2016 and the First Appellate
Authority (FAA)’s order dated 13.12.2016. The replies furnished to the appellant
are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for her case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
sought for information is a third party information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI
Act, it cannot be provided unless due procedure as envisaged u/s 11(1) of the RTI
Act is applied. The respondent authority also submitted that they are not the holder
of information for any air travel bookings or cancellations as sought by the

appellant in her RTI application. On this point, the Commission observes that no
direction can be given to parties which were involved in booking of airlines tickets
such as internet site of “Make my Trip” etc as such parties do not fall under the
purview of Section 2(h) of the RTI Act. The information as sought for vide point
nos. 5-8 of the said RTI application also seem to be held by the immigration
authorities under the Ministry of Home Affairs. However, no order can be passed
to the respondent authority for accessing such information u/s 5(4) of the RTI Act
from the immigration authorities as the entire information sought for (1-8) in the
said RTI application dated 23.11.2016 is exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act,
being information pertaining to a third party.
With the above observation/direction, the appeal is disposed of.
