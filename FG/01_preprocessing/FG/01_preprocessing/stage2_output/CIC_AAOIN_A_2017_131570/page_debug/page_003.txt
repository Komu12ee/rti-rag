appellant in her RTI application. On this point, the Commission observes that no
direction can be given to parties which were involved in booking of airlines tickets
such as internet site of “Make my Trip” etc as such parties do not fall under the
purview of Section 2(h) of the RTI Act. The information as sought for vide point
nos. 5-8 of the said RTI application also seem to be held by the immigration
authorities under the Ministry of Home Affairs. However, no order can be passed
to the respondent authority for accessing such information u/s 5(4) of the RTI Act
from the immigration authorities as the entire information sought for (1-8) in the
said RTI application dated 23.11.2016 is exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act,
being information pertaining to a third party.
With the above observation/direction, the appeal is disposed of.
