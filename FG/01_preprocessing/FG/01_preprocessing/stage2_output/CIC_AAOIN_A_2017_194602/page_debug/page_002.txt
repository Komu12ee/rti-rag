During the hearing, the respondent PIO submitted that they had never
received the RTI application dated 19.05.2016 but a copy of the second appeal
dated 20.12.2016 along with the said RTI application was received on
01.05.2017 from the CIC.
The respondent CPIO is accordingly directed to affirm on affidavit to the
Commission that the above dated RTI application was never received by the
public authority concerned prior to 01.05.2017, within 07 days of the receipt of
the order.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the case record, it was seen that the respondent authority
received the RTI application on 01.05.2017 and provided the requisite reply on
05.05.2017 which is well within the time period stipulated under the provision
of the RTI Act.
