Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 29.09.2016 sought information on
four points as under:
1. Details of the compliance made by Mumbai International Airport Pvt.
Ltd regarding the Mandatory Capital Projects set out under the schedule 7 of the
Operation Management Development Agreement (OMDA) dated 4th April, 2006
executed between Airport Authority of India and Mumbai International Airport
Pvt. Ltd (clause 8.2.1 of the OMDA) and liquidated damages levied in terms of
clause 8.2.3 of the OMDA.
2. Copy of the master plan of the Mumbai Airport in terms of clause 8.3.6
of the OMDA and liquidated damages levied in terms of clause 8.3.6 of the
OMDA.

3. Copies of the minutes of the meetings of OMDA implementation
oversight committee (in terms of clause 8.5.9 of the OMDA) held till the date of
filing of the RTI application.
4. Details of annual fee paid by Mumbai International Airport Pvt. Ltd to
Airport Authority of India (clause 11.1.2.1 of the OMDA).
The CPIO replied on 18.10.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 15.11.2016. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 21.12.2016.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed a second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of
the RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 14.03.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Absent
The respondent was absent despite valid and timely notice of the CIC for
which a warning is issued to the concerned respondent PIO. The concerned PIO
should desist from committing such lapse in future. He should either take prior
permission of the Commission before absenting himself from the hearing or he
should send proper representative to the Commission’s hearing after obtaining due
permission from the Commission for doing so and after briefing the authorised
representative duly.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
point no. A of the RTI application is not covered u/s 2(f) of the RTI Act. However,
information in regard to the point nos. B,C and D of the RTI application is
disclosable but the reply provided by the CPIO on two of these three points is not

proper. The CPIO had denied the sought for information on point B without taking
recourse to any exemption clause u/s 8 of the RTI Act. The reply on point no. C
was also not proper as the appellant was advised to seek information from the
Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA) without transferring the said RTI application
u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act to the MOCA. The reply provided on point no. D was
proper.
Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case in regard to the points B and C of the said RTI application, the
respondent CPIO is directed to provide revised point wise reply on point nos. B
and C of the above stated RTI application complete in all respects to the appellant
as available on record in the form of certified true copies of the documents sought
e.g. note sheets, letters, correspondences, e-mails etc.(legible copies), free of
charge u/s 7(6) of the RTI Act within 15 days of the receipt of the order. For this
purpose, the concerned CPIO/PIO, can take assistance of any other
office/department u/s 5(4) of the RTI Act.
The respondent CPIO is further directed to send a report containing the copy
of the revised reply and the date of despatch of the same to the RTI appellant
within 07 days thereafter to the Commission for record.
With the above observation/direction/warning/advisory, the appeal is
disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
