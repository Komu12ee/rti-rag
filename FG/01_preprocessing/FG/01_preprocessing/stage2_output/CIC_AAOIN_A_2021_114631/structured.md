Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that purpose of installing
CCTV cameras in public places is to ensure surveillance and security and
disseminating of CCTV footage of such area would not only hamper the
day-to-day official activities of the Respondent, but it also affects safety and
security of the other persons and accordingly it is plausible that the
information sought in the RTI application stands squarely covered by the
exemption clauses contained in Sections 8(1) (a), (j) and (g) of the RTI Act.
