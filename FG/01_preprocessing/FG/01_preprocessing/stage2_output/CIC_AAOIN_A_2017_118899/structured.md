Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 25.07.2016 sought copy of the
application form submitted by Shri Sudhir Kumar along with all documents
attached with it while applying for the post of ATC in AAI. The CPIO replied on
19.08.2016. The appellant was not satisfied with the reply of the CPIO and filed
first appeal on 01.09.2016. The First Appellate Authority (FAA) disposed of the
appeal by virtue of its order dated 14.09.2016. In compliance with the order of
FAA, the CPIO provided a revised reply on 05.10.2016. Aggrieved with the non-
supply of the desired information from the respondent authority, the appellant filed

second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the RTI Act before the Central
Information Commission on 17.03.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : smt. R. Sumani,
Joint General Manager, representative of PIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the representative of the respondent APIO submitted
that they had provided the requisite reply vide their letters dated 19.08.2016,
05.10.2016 and the First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 14.09.2016.
The replies furnished to the appellant are just and proper and hence the case might
be dismissed.
The appellant submitted that he was not satisfied with the reply received
from the respondent as wrong employee id was mentioned by the CPIO in his
revised reply dated 05.10.2016.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
the reply provided is just, proper and comprehensive as the sought for information
is a third party information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. Hence,
interference of the Commission is not called for in the present case.
It was also observed by the Commission that the latter part of the reply dated
05.10.2016 was not correct as the respondent authority had mentioned a wrong
employee id which was different from the one submitted by the appellant in his
RTI application for which the then respondent PIO, Shri H.S. Rawat is held
responsible and needs to be issued a warning.
The then respondent PIO, Shri H.S. Rawat is issued warning that full, final
and proper reply to an RTI application should have been provided within the time

case proper reply to an RTI application is invariably provided within 30 days of
