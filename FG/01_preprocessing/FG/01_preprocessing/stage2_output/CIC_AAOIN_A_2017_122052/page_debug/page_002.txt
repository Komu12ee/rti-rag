Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present (in Delhi)
Respondent : Shri Anand Pal Singh,
Assistant General Manager, representative of PIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the representative of respondent PIO submitted that
they had provided an interim reply on 10.02.2017, the First Appellate Authority
(FAA)’s order dated 23.03.2017 and final reply on June, 2017. The replies
furnished to the appellant are just and proper and hence the case might be
dismissed.
The appellant submitted that he received final reply in June, 2017 and was
satisfied with the same.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the Commission that
proper reply was provided to the appellant and the appellant himself admitted
during the hearing that he did not want to pursue this case any longer.
With the above observation, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
