Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : Shri G.L. Verma,
Assistant General Manager, representative of
PIO,
Airports Authority of India
During the hearing, the representative of respondent PIO submitted that they
had provided the requisite reply vide their letter dated 10.01.2017 and the First
Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated 14.02.2017. The replies furnished to the
appellant are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the case record, it was noted by the Commission that the reply
provided is just, proper and comprehensive except on point no. 5 of the said RTI
application. However, it was also observed that the sought for information on this
point is a third party information exempted under Sections 8(1)(j) and 8(1)(g) of
the RTI Act. Hence the same cannot be provided to the appellant. Moreover, as the
appellant was not present to contest his case, interference of the Commission is not
called for.
With the above observation, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
