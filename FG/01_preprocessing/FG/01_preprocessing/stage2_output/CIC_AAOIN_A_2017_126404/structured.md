Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 30.12.2016 sought information
regarding her representation dated 04.06.2016 regarding domestic violence and
dowry harassment by Shri Kislay Garg on three points as under;
1. Details of the investigations done on the appellant’s complaint.
2. Action taken report and noting on his representation dated 24.10.2016.
3. Copy of the reminder sent by Ministry of Civil Aviation along with
certification of documents.
The CPIO’s reply is not on record. The appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal. The First Appellate Authority (FAA)

During the hearing, the representative of appellant submitted that he had not
received any reply till date.
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had received
the RTI application dated 30.12.2016 on 13.01.2017 and had in turn transferred it
u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act on 17.01.2017 to the RTI cell, HQ., Airport Authority of
India and submitted that a reply dated 10.02.2017 was provided to the appellant.
However, on perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the
Commission that the said reply was not addressed to the appellant concerned but
was addressed to the Ministry of Civil Aviation indicating that the said reply had
already been had provided to the appellant. It was also observed that reply dated
28.11.2016 is not relevant and it was given much before the submission of the
present RTI application, so the intimation to the Ministry from the AAI that a reply
had been provided to the appellant was also patently wrong.
In view of the above, a Show Cause notice is issued to the then PIO, Shri
P.K. Asiwal, DGM(ES), CHQ., Airports Authority of India, New Delhi u/s 20 of
the RTI Act to explain the following:-

ADJUNCT ORDER (Based on Submission): 07.09.2018
The respondent submitted that he was posted in and worked in the RTI CelI,
Airports Authority of India, CHQ under the office of CNO RTI and he was never
designated as the PIO under RTI Act. In addition to other responsibilities as Jt. GM
(ES) RTI Cell, CHQ, his job was only to receive the RTI applications on behalf of
AAI and forward them to the concerned CPIOs to provide the requisite information
to the applicant under RTI Act, 2005. In the case of appeals also, RTI Cell
forwarded the same to the concerned First Appellate Authority to provide the
requisite information under the provisions of RTI Act, 2005.
The RTI request dated 30.12.2016 of Ms. Sumedha was received in the RTI
Cell, CHQ on 24.01.2017 on transfer from MoCA u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act, 2005
vide letter No. AV.20036/4(7)/2017-AAI dated 20.01.2017 and the same was
forwarded to Shri H S Rawat AGM(HR) and CPIO vide RTI Cell ION No.
AAI/RTI/Misc2017/136 dated 30.01.2017 to provide the sought for information to
the applicant.
He submitted that as a routine, the CIC notice of hearing for appeal in the
above mentioned matter was also forwarded to Shri Sanjay Jain, Executive
Director (HR) & FAA of the concerned CPIO vide this office (RTI Cell) letter
No.AAI/RTI/CIC Hearing/2018/747 dated 08.06.2018.

It was stated that at the time of RTI application of Ms. Sumedha, Shri H S
Rawat, Asst, GM (IIR) was the CPIO and as on now, the charge of CPIO has ben
taken by Shri K Nagaraj, GM (HR) and a copy of Commission’s order had already

directions issued by Hon'ble CIC.
In the light of above facts, it is clarified that the undersigned is not the
CPIO/PIO and was working as DGM (ES) in the RTI Cell, CHQ, Airports
Authority of India. Hence it is requested that the show cause notice issued to the
undersigned may be withdrawn.
Decision:
In view of the above explanation, the Commission finds no malafide
intention on the part of the answering respondent as the only duty assigned to the
him was to forward the RTI application to the concerned CPIOs and that he was
neither a designated CPIO nor was he authorized to work in that capacity. Hence,
the show-cause notice issued to the respondent official needs to be withdrawn.
The Commission accordingly decides to withdraw the show cause notice
issued to Shri P.K. Asiwal, DGM(ES), CHQ., Airports Authority of India u/s 20 of
the RTI Act.
With the above observation, the show-cause proceeding is closed.
