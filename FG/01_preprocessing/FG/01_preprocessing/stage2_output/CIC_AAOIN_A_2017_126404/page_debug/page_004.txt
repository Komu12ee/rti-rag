The respondent submitted that he was posted in and worked in the RTI CelI,
Airports Authority of India, CHQ under the office of CNO RTI and he was never
designated as the PIO under RTI Act. In addition to other responsibilities as Jt. GM
(ES) RTI Cell, CHQ, his job was only to receive the RTI applications on behalf of
AAI and forward them to the concerned CPIOs to provide the requisite information
to the applicant under RTI Act, 2005. In the case of appeals also, RTI Cell
forwarded the same to the concerned First Appellate Authority to provide the
requisite information under the provisions of RTI Act, 2005.

Cell, CHQ on 24.01.2017 on transfer from MoCA u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act, 2005
vide letter No. AV.20036/4(7)/2017-AAI dated 20.01.2017 and the same was
forwarded to Shri H S Rawat AGM(HR) and CPIO vide RTI Cell ION No.
AAI/RTI/Misc2017/136 dated 30.01.2017 to provide the sought for information to
the applicant.

above mentioned matter was also forwarded to Shri Sanjay Jain, Executive
