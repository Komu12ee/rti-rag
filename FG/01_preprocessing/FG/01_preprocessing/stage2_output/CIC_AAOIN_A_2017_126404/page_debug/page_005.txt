It was stated that at the time of RTI application of Ms. Sumedha, Shri H S
Rawat, Asst, GM (IIR) was the CPIO and as on now, the charge of CPIO has ben
taken by Shri K Nagaraj, GM (HR) and a copy of Commission’s order had already
been forwarded him with a request to take appropriate action for compliance of the
directions issued by Hon'ble CIC.
In the light of above facts, it is clarified that the undersigned is not the
CPIO/PIO and was working as DGM (ES) in the RTI Cell, CHQ, Airports
Authority of India. Hence it is requested that the show cause notice issued to the
undersigned may be withdrawn.
Decision:
In view of the above explanation, the Commission finds no malafide
intention on the part of the answering respondent as the only duty assigned to the
him was to forward the RTI application to the concerned CPIOs and that he was
neither a designated CPIO nor was he authorized to work in that capacity. Hence,
the show-cause notice issued to the respondent official needs to be withdrawn.
The Commission accordingly decides to withdraw the show cause notice
issued to Shri P.K. Asiwal, DGM(ES), CHQ., Airports Authority of India u/s 20 of
the RTI Act.
With the above observation, the show-cause proceeding is closed.
