During the hearing, the representative of appellant submitted that he had not
received any reply till date.
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had received
the RTI application dated 30.12.2016 on 13.01.2017 and had in turn transferred it
u/s 6(3) of the RTI Act on 17.01.2017 to the RTI cell, HQ., Airport Authority of
India and submitted that a reply dated 10.02.2017 was provided to the appellant.
However, on perusal of the relevant case record, it was noted by the
Commission that the said reply was not addressed to the appellant concerned but
was addressed to the Ministry of Civil Aviation indicating that the said reply had
already been had provided to the appellant. It was also observed that reply dated
28.11.2016 is not relevant and it was given much before the submission of the
present RTI application, so the intimation to the Ministry from the AAI that a reply
had been provided to the appellant was also patently wrong.
