22.02.2016. The First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order is not on record.
Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 08.04.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Absent
Respondent : PIO, AAI Absent in Porbandar
Shri G.S. Sure, Assistant Manager
Jet Airways(India) Ltd.
The respondent, AAI was absent despite valid and timely notice of the CIC
for which a warning is issued to the concerned respondent PIO. The concerned
PIO should desist from committing such lapse in future. He should either take
prior permission of the Commission before absenting himself from the hearing or
he should send proper representative to the Commission’s hearing after obtaining
due permission from the Commission for doing so and after briefing the authorized
representative duly.
The appellant was not present to plead for his case.
On perusal of the case record, it was noted by the Commission that the
reply provided is just, proper and comprehensive. However, it was noted by the
Commission that the RTI application was wrongly transferred to Jet Airways
which is not a public authority declared u/s 2(h) of the RTI Act for which warning
needs to be issued to Airport Authority of India.
The then respondent CPIO, Airport Authority of India is issued warning for
wrongly transferring the RTI application to the Jet Airways which was not in order
under the RTI Act as Jet Airways is not a public Authority u/s 2(h) of the Act. The
respondent CPIO should be more careful while dealing with the RTI matters in
future.
