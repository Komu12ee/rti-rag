Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though the Respondent
has furnished a timely reply with respect to the instant RTI Application, the
Appellant has expressed dissatisfaction with respect to the information
furnished with respect to points 6, 8, 9 and 10 of the RTI Application. In the
light of the submissions made during hearing, the Commission deems it fit
to direct the Respondent to furnish a revised categorical reply with respect to
point 6, 8, 9 and 10 of the RTI Application to the Appellant within a time
frame of 15 days from the date of receipt of this order.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
