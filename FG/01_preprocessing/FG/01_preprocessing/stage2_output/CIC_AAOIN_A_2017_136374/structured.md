Facts:
The appellant vide RTI application dated 17.02.2017 sought information
regarding examination held on 17.01.2016 for the post of Manger on three points
as under:
I. Marks obtained by the appellant bearing Roll no 4701110005 in the
examination held on 17.01.2016.
II. Copy of the answer sheet of the appellant.
III. Copy of merit list of candidates shortlisted for interview along with
answer copies of all such candidates.
The CPIO replied on 31.03.2017. The Appellant was not satisfied with the
reply of the CPIO and filed first appeal on 19.04.2017. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA) disposed of the appeal by virtue of its order dated 19.04.2017.

Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 25.05.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri Rishykesh Priyadarshy
Deputy General Manager cum CPIO
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letters dated 31.03.2017, 01.05.2017 and subsequent
reply dated 17.07.2018 and the First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated
19.04.2017 The replies furnished to the appellant are just and proper and hence the
case might be dismissed.
The appellant submitted that he had not received the reply dated 17.07.2018
from the respondent authority concerned.
The respondent PIO is directed to resend the reply dated 17.07.2018 within
07 days of the receipt of this order to the appellant by speed post keeping the postal
tracking number as record. The despatch details are to be submitted to the
Commission within 07 more days thereafter.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was observed by the Commission
that the replies provided by the respondent by virtue of its letter dated 18.07.2018
on point no. 1 & latter part of point no. 3 of the above stated RTI application are
just and proper. However, on point no. 2, the respondent CPIO had submitted that
records are not available. He is to file an affidavit in this regard. On first part of
point no. 3, the respondent authority is directed to provide copy of merit list in case
the same had already been published by them without disclosing the name of any
third party mentioned therein.

Be that as it may, since no desired information was provided to the appellant
in the present case, the respondent CPIO is directed to provide revised point wise
reply on point no. 2 and first part of point no 3 as discussed during the hearing,
complete in all respects to the appellant as available on record in the form of
certified true copies of the documents sought e.g. note sheets, letters,
correspondences, e-mails etc.(legible copies), free of charge u/s 7(6) of the RTI
Act within 15 days of the receipt of the order. For this purpose, the concerned
CPIO/PIO, can take assistance of any other office/department u/s 5(4) of the RTI
Act.
OR
In case the relevant records are not available even after thorough search, the
present respondent CPIO on point no. 2 is directed to submit an affidavit indicating
the date of destruction / weeding out of the said records along with a copy of the
order of the competent authority authorising such destruction / weeding out within
one month of the receipt of this order with a copy duly endorsed to the appellant
within the same time period.
With the above observation/direction, the appeal is disposed of.
Copies of the order be sent to the concerned parties free of cost.
