Aggrieved with the non-supply of the desired information from the respondent
authority, the appellant filed second appeal under the provision of Section 19 of the
RTI Act before the Central Information Commission on 25.05.2017.
Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Present
Respondent : Shri Rishykesh Priyadarshy
Deputy General Manager cum CPIO
Airport Authority of India
During the hearing, the respondent CPIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letters dated 31.03.2017, 01.05.2017 and subsequent
reply dated 17.07.2018 and the First Appellate Authority (FAA)’s order dated
19.04.2017 The replies furnished to the appellant are just and proper and hence the
case might be dismissed.
The appellant submitted that he had not received the reply dated 17.07.2018
from the respondent authority concerned.
The respondent PIO is directed to resend the reply dated 17.07.2018 within
07 days of the receipt of this order to the appellant by speed post keeping the postal
tracking number as record. The despatch details are to be submitted to the
Commission within 07 more days thereafter.
On perusal of the relevant case record, it was observed by the Commission
that the replies provided by the respondent by virtue of its letter dated 18.07.2018
on point no. 1 & latter part of point no. 3 of the above stated RTI application are
just and proper. However, on point no. 2, the respondent CPIO had submitted that
records are not available. He is to file an affidavit in this regard. On first part of
point no. 3, the respondent authority is directed to provide copy of merit list in case
the same had already been published by them without disclosing the name of any
third party mentioned therein.
