Grounds for Second Appeal
The CPIO did not provide the desired information.
Order
Appellant : Representative of the appellant
Shri Anil Tiwari, Advocate

Airport Authority of India,
New Delhi
During the hearing, the respondent APIO submitted that they had provided
the requisite reply vide their letter dated 21.04.2017 and the First Appellate
Authority (FAA)’s order dated 02.06.2017. The replies furnished to the appellant
are just and proper and hence the case might be dismissed.
The representative of the appellant submitted that he was not satisfied with
the reply received from the respondent and submitted that due procedure envisaged
under section 11(1) of the RTI Act should be followed by the respondent authority
in the present case.
On perusal of relevant case records, it was noted by the Commission that
the sought for information is a third party information exempted u/s 8(1)(j) of the
RTI Act and hence the same cannot be provided to the appellant unless due
procedure as envisaged under section 11(1) of the RTI Act is applied. However,
application of the due procedure u/s 11(1) of the RTI Act will be cumbersome in
this case as the candidates information in regard to whom are sought in the said
RTI Act are spread all over India. Moreover, no larger public interest angle is seen
to be involved in this case.
