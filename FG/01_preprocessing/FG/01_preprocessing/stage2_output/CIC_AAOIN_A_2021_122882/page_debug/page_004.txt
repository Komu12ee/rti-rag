The Commission queried the Respondent whether its possible to furnish
information with respect to points which are not voluminous, to which the
Respondent submitted that information with respect to point 1, 3, 5 & 6 can
be provided to the Appellant, except for point 4 & 8 as point 4 for is merely
a statement where the Appellant is not asking any information and point 5 is
not specific and clear. He further submitted that information with respect to
rest of the points are voluminous in nature for which the Appellant can avail
inspection of relevant records.
The Commission queried the Appellant, whether he is willing to avail
inspection of relevant records, to which the Appellant affirmatively agreed.
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though the Respondent
has furnished a timely reply with respect to the instant RTI Application, no
information was furnished to the Appellant, stating that the same is
voluminous in nature. During hearing the Respondent submitted that
information with respect to points 1, 3, 5 & 6 can be provided to the
Appellant except for point 4 & 8 which are non-specific in nature. In view of
the above the Commission deems it fit to direct the Respondent to furnish a
revised categorical reply with respect to points 1, 3, 5 & 6 to the Appellant
within a time frame of 15 days from the date of receipt of this order.
