Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a First Appeal dated 06.04.2021. The
First Appellate Authority vide order dated 05.05.2021, informed as under:
Grounds for Second Appeal:
The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. Appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.
Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that no information has been furnished by the
Respondent stating that the same is voluminous in nature. The Commission
interjected that the Respondent has offered inspection, but the same has not
been availed, to which the Appellant stated that since the distance is very far
and availing the same would incur heavy expenses.
The Respondent submitted that the information sought vide instant RTI
Application is voluminous in nature therefore the same could not be

The Commission queried the Respondent whether its possible to furnish
information with respect to points which are not voluminous, to which the
Respondent submitted that information with respect to point 1, 3, 5 & 6 can
be provided to the Appellant, except for point 4 & 8 as point 4 for is merely
a statement where the Appellant is not asking any information and point 5 is
not specific and clear. He further submitted that information with respect to
rest of the points are voluminous in nature for which the Appellant can avail
inspection of relevant records.
The Commission queried the Appellant, whether he is willing to avail
inspection of relevant records, to which the Appellant affirmatively agreed.
Decision:
Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though the Respondent
has furnished a timely reply with respect to the instant RTI Application, no
information was furnished to the Appellant, stating that the same is
voluminous in nature. During hearing the Respondent submitted that
information with respect to points 1, 3, 5 & 6 can be provided to the
Appellant except for point 4 & 8 which are non-specific in nature. In view of
the above the Commission deems it fit to direct the Respondent to furnish a
revised categorical reply with respect to points 1, 3, 5 & 6 to the Appellant
within a time frame of 15 days from the date of receipt of this order.

As regards points 2, 7 and 9 being voluminous in nature, the Commission
finds expedient to direct the present CPIO to provide an opportunity to the
Appellant to inspect the relevant records as sought in the present RTI
Application on a mutually decided date and time duly intimated to the
Appellant telephonically and in writing. Copy of documents, if desired,
should be provided to the Appellant free of cost up to 25 pages, and beyond
those, prescribed fees shall be charged as per the RTI Rules, 2012. The
present CPIO is further directed that while complying with the directions of
the Commission, all the personal information/identifying particulars of any
third parties should be adequately redacted/blackened out. The Commission
further directs the present CPIO that no information shall be disclosed to the
Appellant which is exempted from disclosure under the provisions of
Section 8 and 9 of the RTI Act.
The said direction should be complied with, by the CPIO within 60 days
from the date of receipt of this order and a compliance report of the same be
duly sent to the Commission, enumerating the details of documents
inspected and copy of documents provided.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
